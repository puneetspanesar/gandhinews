India will host the **25th Council of Ministers of the Indian Ocean Rim Association (IORA)** in New Delhi on Friday, November 27, bringing together the foreign ministers of the group's member states. The meeting follows the 29th Committee of Senior Officials, which sat in the capital on November 25 and 26.

External Affairs Minister S Jaishankar has already begun the bilateral meetings around it. On Thursday he met Mauritius Foreign Minister **Dhananjay Ritish Ramful**, who arrived in Delhi that morning, and wrote on X that it was "a great pleasure to meet FM @RitishRamful of Mauritius on the sidelines of the @IORAofficial Council of Ministers meeting."

## What the association is

IORA's own account describes the Council of Ministers as the group's **top decision-making body**. It is made up of the foreign ministers of member states and sets overall policy and strategic direction, reviews how cooperation is going and decides on new areas to work in. The Committee of Senior Officials is the second-highest body: it reviews the reports of IORA's working mechanisms, checks that ministerial decisions are being carried out, sets economic cooperation priorities and prepares the ground for ministers.

The Council elects a chair for two years, based on voluntary offers from members. **India holds the chair for 2025 to 2027**. The association's Secretariat is in Ebene, Mauritius, the country whose foreign minister Jaishankar met on Thursday.

## What India has said

The Ministry of External Affairs announced the Delhi meetings on Wednesday, November 25. It described the Council as meeting at a **"critical juncture for the Indo-Pacific region"**, according to reports of the announcement, and said the gathering would highlight the Indian Ocean as a region of peace, stability, predictability and sustainable development. It tied the meeting to India's MAHASAGAR vision, short for Mutual and Holistic Advancement for Security and Growth for All in the Region, which argues for an open, inclusive and rules-based maritime order.

India's chairship carries the theme "Innovation, Openness, Resilience and Adaptability in the Indian Ocean Region", the same words used for the 28th Committee of Senior Officials, which India hosted in New Delhi on June 15 and 16, 2026.

According to reports, ministers and officials from 23 member states and 12 dialogue partners are attending. Those named include Sri Lanka's Foreign Minister Vijitha Herath, Singapore's Minister of State for Foreign Affairs Alvin Tan and Somalia's State Minister of Foreign Affairs Ali Mohamed Omar. Malaysia, the UAE, Australia and Indonesia were also expected. IORA's page lists no agenda or expected outcome documents, and none has been announced.

## How the meetings have run

The last Council of Ministers, the 24th, was held virtually on May 21, 2025, under Sri Lanka's chairmanship, with India as vice-chair. It adopted **IORA guidelines on combating illegal, unreported and unregulated fishing**, a Climate Change Strategic Agenda and the Colombo Communiqué. The meetings covered six priority areas: maritime safety and security; trade and investment; disaster risk management; fisheries management; academic, science and technology cooperation; and tourism and cultural exchanges. Blue economy and women's economic empowerment run across all of them.

## Why it matters to India

For India the meeting is a chance to set the agenda of a group that spans the east African coast, the Gulf, South and Southeast Asia and Australia, on subjects that touch its own coastline: shipping safety, fishing rules, disaster response and trade. The association's dialogue partners include China, the United States, Russia, Japan and the European Union, so the wider IORA family is one in which several major capitals take part.

## What happens next

The Council of Ministers meets on Friday, November 27, in New Delhi. Ministers are expected to review cooperation under the priority areas and identify new ones. Any joint outcome document, and any bilateral meetings Jaishankar holds on the sidelines, will be known only after the session.
