India go into the fifth and final one-day international against New Zealand at Bay Oval, Mount Maunganui, on Sunday, November 15, 2026, **leading 2-1** after the heaviest defeat of their tour. New Zealand won the fourth ODI on Friday by 244 runs, and the home side have since lost the bowler who did the damage. The match starts at 3 pm local time, 7:30 am IST, according to reports.

## What happened in the fourth ODI

New Zealand made 333 batting first, a target of 334. Openers Will Young and Tim Seifert put on 85 in 12 overs, Seifert scoring 58 off 48 balls, and Young, who survived two early chances, went on to **125 off 108 balls**. India's bowlers pulled things back late, conceding only 68 runs in the last 10 overs.

The chase was over in the first over. **Matt Henry removed Rohit Sharma, Virat Kohli and captain Shreyas Iyer**, leaving India 0 for 3. Ruturaj Gaikwad fell to Kyle Jamieson with the score on 9 for 4. Ishan Kishan and Axar Patel added 44, the only real stand of the innings, before Matthew Fisher had Axar caught and Henry bowled Kishan.

India were **all out for 89 in 26.4 overs**. Kishan's 27 off 53 balls was the top score, with Axar Patel and Naman Dhir on 21 each. Henry finished with 6 for 27 and was named Player of the Match.

## A bad day in the record books

By the margin, it is India's **second-biggest ODI defeat by runs**, behind the 245-run loss to Sri Lanka in Sharjah in 2000. For New Zealand, it is their second-biggest ODI win by runs, after the 290-run win over Ireland in 2008. India's 89 is their sixth-lowest ODI total and the eighth time they have been bowled out for under 100. Their lowest remains 54, also against Sri Lanka in 2000.

Kohli has now made 7 and 0 in the last two matches, and Rohit and Iyer both went for ducks on Friday.

## Team news for the finale

New Zealand have confirmed, according to reports, that **Henry will not play the fifth ODI**: he leaves to join the Test squad ahead of the first Test against India, which begins on November 19. One report says Jamieson is also going to the Test camp, though another predicted side still lists him. Jacob Duffy is expected to lead the attack. Blair Tickner, who has been under concussion protocols and a seven-day stand-down after being hit in the second ODI, is likely to come in if cleared, and one report says debutant Brett Randell is the alternative.

The rest of the predicted New Zealand side has Young, Seifert, Nick Kelly, Finn Allen, Michael Bracewell, Dean Foxcroft or Mitch Hay, Kristian Clarke and Fisher around captain Mitchell Santner. These are predictions, as New Zealand have not named their eleven.

For India, **Gurnoor Brar is out** because he has been picked for the Test squad, and Mohammed Siraj is expected to take his place after bowling little on this tour. Yash Thakur is another option. Kuldeep Yadav's place has been questioned after a hard day, but he is in the predicted side. The expected eleven is Rohit, Gaikwad, Kohli, Iyer, Kishan, Axar, Dhir, Auqib Nabi, Kuldeep, Siraj and Arshdeep Singh. India's squad is not final until the toss.

## How the series stands

India won the first ODI at Eden Park on November 4 by five wickets on DLS and the second at Wellington on November 7 by four runs on DLS. The third, at Hamilton on November 10, was abandoned without a ball bowled. ## What is at stake

The match is not a decider in the strict sense. India lead 2-1, so a New Zealand win would **level the series at 2-2** with one no result and keep their home record unbeaten, while an India win would make it 3-1. A washout would leave India winners at 2-1. In 127 ODIs between the sides, India have won 65 and New Zealand 53, with eight no results and one tie.

Previews say the new ball swings at Bay Oval before batting gets easier, so the openers must see off the first overs. That is the problem Henry set India on Friday, and it is the first thing Rohit and Gaikwad face on Sunday.

## What happens next

The first ball of the fifth ODI is at 7:30 am IST on Sunday, November 15, 2026. The Test series then begins at Wellington on November 19, 2026, and several players on both sides will leave for it after this match.
