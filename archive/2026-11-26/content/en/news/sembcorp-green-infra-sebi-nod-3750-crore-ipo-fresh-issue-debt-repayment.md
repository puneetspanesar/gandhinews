The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has cleared the way for **Sembcorp Green Infra's ₹3,750 crore initial public offering**. The regulator issued its final observations on the renewable power producer's draft papers on November 13, according to reports, the last formal step before the company can file its final prospectus and fix dates.

The issue is **entirely a fresh issue of equity shares**, with no offer for sale. That means every rupee raised goes to the company and none to the Sembcorp group, which owns it. The company is the Indian renewables arm of Singapore's Sembcorp Industries, a portfolio company of the investor Temasek. Its promoters are Sembcorp Utilities and Sembcorp Industries.

## What the draft papers say

The draft red herring prospectus, filed in August 2026, lists the shares at a face value of ₹10 each. The price and the number of shares have not been set. According to the draft papers, the net proceeds will go **first to repaying or prepaying borrowings** of the company and certain subsidiaries named in the document, with any remainder going to general corporate purposes.

The business is large and profitable. As of March 31, 2026, Sembcorp Green Infra had **105 projects across 13 states and Union Territories**, 84 of them operating and 21 under construction. Its portfolio is about 7.64 GW: **3.60 GW operating** and 4.04 GW in various stages of development. The mix spans wind, solar, hybrid and storage, and 1,433 MWh of battery energy storage is under construction. Reports on the filing say it ranks among the top 10 renewable power producers in India by operational capacity.

## The numbers behind the issue

Financials in the papers show steady growth. Revenue from operations rose to **₹2,652.50 crore in FY26 from ₹2,318.29 crore** in FY25. Earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation rose to ₹2,098.73 crore from ₹1,837.12 crore, a margin of more than 74%. Net profit climbed to ₹371.08 crore from ₹298.83 crore.

The company is rated AA+ (Stable) by CRISIL, ICRA and India Ratings, as of March 31. Seven firms are managing the issue: Axis Capital, Citigroup Global Markets India, HSBC Securities and Capital Markets (India), ICICI Securities, Kotak Mahindra Capital, CLSA India and IIFL Capital Services.

## How we got here

Sembcorp Green Infra became a bigger player in December 2025, when it bought 100% of **ReNew Sun Bright Private Limited** from ReNew Private Limited. In August 2026 it filed its draft papers for the ₹3,750 crore issue, and SEBI's observations came about eleven weeks later.

The clearance comes in a busy week for the regulator's IPO desk. In the seven days to November 14, SEBI's observations were reported on nine issues, including the payments company Razorpay (November 3, reported size ₹5,000 crore to ₹6,000 crore, filed through the confidential route in June) and MakeMyTrip India (November 6, an offer for sale reported at over $1 billion). Mahanadi Coalfields, whose [offer for sale by Coal India](/en/news/mahanadi-coalfields-ipo-rhp-filed-coal-india-10-per-cent-offer-for-sale) is already at the final-prospectus stage, was on the same list. SEBI has put the draft of Inox Clean Energy in abeyance, a pause and not a rejection.

## Why it matters

For savers, a listed Sembcorp Green Infra would add one more way to hold India's **renewable power build-out** through the stock market. Reports on the filing cite estimates that over 80% of India's new capacity additions will come from renewables by FY31, and Crisil's projection of 50 to 55 GW of energy storage additions by that year. They also put the company's bid success ratio on complex hybrid and storage-linked projects at 77.32% over FY24 to FY26.

For the company, the debt repayment is the stated purpose of the issue: the money goes to retiring borrowings, not to the owners. With 4.04 GW more capacity in development, the balance sheet is what the offer is meant to strengthen.

## What happens next

SEBI's observations are not an endorsement of the company's business or a check of its numbers. Sembcorp Green Infra must now file its red herring prospectus with the Registrar of Companies, announce a price band and open the issue for bids. Under SEBI's rules, an issue must open within 12 months of the observations, so the window runs to November 2027. No dates have been announced.
