Jharkhand turned 26 on **Sunday, November 15, 2026**, but the state's government has chosen not to hold its big Foundation Day ceremony today. The evening offering of **Chhath, the festival's third day**, falls on the same date, and the administration and most of the public are busy with ghat arrangements. The **main state-level event has been moved to November 28 at Khelgaon, Ranchi**, the date on which Chief Minister Hemant Soren's government took oath two years ago.

## What today looked like

The state was created out of Bihar on November 15, 2000, and the date is also the birth anniversary of **Bhagwan Birsa Munda**, marked nationally as Janjatiya Gaurav Diwas. This year the day began with a **"Run for Cleanliness" between 7 and 8 am in every district**, led by the deputy commissioners and ending at the main Chhath ghats, where participants cleaned the steps and banks. In Ranchi the run went from Morabadi to Albert Ekka Chowk. In Khunti, Birsa Munda's birthplace, a Janjatiya Gaurav Padyatra was taken out with senior leaders and local residents.

Chief Minister Soren inaugurated the **Jharkhand Pavilion at the trade fair in Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi**; the time of the inauguration was not given. Railways have told trains to slow down at **48 sensitive locations** in the state during the evening arghya, and heavy vehicles are barred from entering Deoghar town on November 15 and 16 to manage Chhath crowds.

## Greetings from Delhi

Prime Minister Narendra Modi wrote on X that the state is "a unique blend of tribal pride, cultural diversity and natural wealth", and said it would "reach new heights of development", pointing to its potential in minerals, energy, handicrafts and tourism. In a separate post for Janjatiya Gaurav Diwas he paid tribute to Birsa Munda and said the government was "leaving no stone unturned" for tribal communities.

President Droupadi Murmu, who served as Jharkhand's Governor from 2015 to 2021, also posted on both occasions. "This land, home to divine figures such as Dharti Aba Bhagwan Birsa Munda and great heroes such as Sidho-Kanhu," she wrote, would, with the people's effort, "take the state to new heights of development and prosperity".

Modi is due to launch **246 tribal development projects worth about ₹3,000 crore** from Gadchiroli in Maharashtra at 11 am today, as our earlier report details. Gadchiroli is the host venue for the national Janjatiya Gaurav Diwas event, and the announced package has **no separate allocation for Jharkhand**; the state's benefits come through national schemes such as Eklavya Model Residential Schools and the Dharti Aaba Janjatiya Gram Utkarsh Abhiyan, which was launched from Hazaribagh in October 2024. Jharkhand is home to several Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Groups, among them the Birhor, Asur and Korwa, whose villages are covered by the package's multi-purpose centres.

## The fortnight and the November 28 ceremony

The government has turned the day into a **fortnight running from November 15 to 28**. From November 17 to 25 there will be sports, drama, dance, music and essay contests at panchayat, block and district level, with the best performers going to the state level. Selected participants will perform at Khelgaon on November 26 and 27, and a fair with departmental stalls and district showcases of local products and tourist sites will run there from November 26 to 28.

On November 28 the government plans to lay the **foundation stones of RIMS-2 and the Jamtara Medical College**, along with about half a dozen other projects, and to inaugurate road schemes online from lists sent by all districts. Assets will be distributed to beneficiaries, appointment letters handed to selected candidates, and institutions that did well at panchayat and state level felicitated. A cabinet meeting has been proposed for November 18 to clear new schemes ahead of the event.

## Awards move too

The **Jharkhand Student Award**, announced earlier for November 15, now features in the November 28 programme. The state set aside about **₹1.46 crore in cash prizes** for 235 students: board toppers of the JAC, CBSE and ICSE examinations get **₹3 lakh, ₹2 lakh and ₹1 lakh** for the first three places, 12 CM School of Excellence toppers receive laptops, and 50 students who cleared JEE or NEET through the Akanksha coaching scheme are honoured. The plan lists 226 laptops, 80 mobile phones and nine tablets. Police headquarters is separately selecting recipients for **21 Governor's Medals and 60 Jharkhand Police Medals**.

## Why it matters and what next

For readers, the practical effect is that the announcements people expect on Foundation Day, new hospitals, roads, jobs and prizes, now come in two weeks, not today. The date also lets the Soren government present two years in office alongside the state's anniversary. The Chhath arghya continues this evening at ghats across the state, and the IMD has forecast light rain in parts of Jharkhand after the festival. The next fixed points are the proposed cabinet meeting on November 18 and the ceremony at Khelgaon on November 28.
