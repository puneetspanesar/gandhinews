The India Meteorological Department (IMD) has warned of **heavy rain over Tamil Nadu, Puducherry and Karaikal until November 27**, with rain turning fairly widespread to widespread from Tuesday, November 24, to Thursday, November 26. The Regional Meteorological Centre (RMC) in Chennai has put Chennai and 20 other districts under a yellow alert for Tuesday.

The cause is a set of easterly waves, which IMD's national release of November 22 (issued at 1 pm IST) describes as a trough in the easterlies running from the southwest Bay of Bengal to north coastal Andhra Pradesh. No cyclone or depression is named in the release.

## What IMD says

The release gives the following sequence for the three regions, which it treats together:

```timeline
{"title":"IMD warning for Tamil Nadu, Puducherry and Karaikal","items":[{"date":"Nov 22 to 23","text":"Isolated to scattered rain"},{"date":"Nov 23 to 27","text":"Isolated heavy rainfall"},{"date":"Nov 24 to 26","text":"Fairly widespread to widespread rain"},{"date":"Nov 23 to 28","text":"Thunderstorms, lightning and gusts of 40 to 50 km/h, up to 60 km/h"},{"date":"Nov 27 to 28","text":"Isolated to scattered rain"}]}
```

IMD defines heavy rain as 64.5 to 115.5 mm in 24 hours. The RMC's yellow alert for Tuesday speaks of isolated falls of 6 to 11 cm, in the same range. The national release gives no district-wise list; those come from the Chennai centre.

## Where the rain is expected

For Tuesday, the RMC's forecast names Chennai, Tiruvallur, Chengalpattu, Kancheepuram, Ranipet, Tiruvannamalai and Villupuram in the north, and Cuddalore, Thanjavur, Tiruvarur, Nagapattinam and Pudukkottai in the delta belt, along with Puducherry and Karaikal. Another forecast lists nine delta and southern districts, including Mayiladuthurai, Sivaganga, Ramanathapuram and Thoothukudi, under the heavy-rain warning.

The RMC's day-wise outlook, issued earlier, moves the heavier rain inland after Tuesday: Theni, Dindigul and Madurai on Wednesday, November 25, and Karur, Tiruchirappalli, Dindigul and Madurai on Thursday. Reports of the forecast flag a risk of waterlogging in south Chennai (Velachery, Pallikaranai, Perungudi, Adyar) and in parts of the central and northern city (Mambalam, Kodambakkam, T Nagar, Manali, Madhavaram). No rainfall total for Tuesday had been published when this report was written.

## How the season has gone

This is the latest in a run of spells since the northeast monsoon set in. On October 21 [rain shut schools in Chennai and three neighbouring districts](/en/news/chennai-rain-schools-shut-four-districts-low-pressure), after Vadapalani recorded **132.9 mm** in a night. On October 25 the RMC raised a [red alert for five districts](/en/news/tamil-nadu-red-alert-pudukkottai-thanjavur-deep-depression) as a Bay depression approached. On November 2 Cyclone Murasu's outer rain gave Parrys **149.2 mm**, the highest in the city that day, as we reported in our [account of that morning](/en/news/chennai-rain-murasu-parrys-149-mm-sameeran-minor-stagnation). The last forecast spell, over Deepavali on November 8, is in our [earlier report](/en/news/tamil-nadu-kerala-heavy-rain-deepavali-eight-districts-imd).

Despite those downpours, season totals were short as of November 19: figures reported that day put Chengalpattu 50% below normal and Chennai 28% below. A wet week narrows that gap but does not close it.

## What the authorities have asked

No school closure order for Tuesday had been reported when this was written, and no new instruction from the state government was available. IMD's own advice is aimed at farmers: **harvest mature rice, groundnut, pulses and millets** and store them dry, strengthen field bunds and drainage, support tomato, chilli and creeper vegetables, and clear drainage around coconut basins.

IMD says action may be taken on orange and red warnings, and that forecast accuracy falls with longer lead times, so the later days of this spell may be revised.

## What next

IMD's district-wise warnings, updated on its website, and the RMC's daily bulletin from Chennai will show whether Tuesday's yellow alert is raised. The rain is expected to ease into isolated to scattered showers on November 27 and 28.
