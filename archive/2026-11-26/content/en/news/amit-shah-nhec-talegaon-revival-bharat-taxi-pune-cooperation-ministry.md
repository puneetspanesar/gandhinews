**Union Home Minister and Minister of Cooperation Amit Shah** on Sunday performed the bhoomi pujan for the revival of the National Heavy Engineering Cooperative, known as NHEC, at **Talegaon Dabhade in Pune district**, a factory complex of about 110 acres that makes sugar-mill machinery and has stood idle for years. Speaking at the ceremony, he said production would begin **before December 2027** and that sugar-mill manufacturing would resume at the site.

The same function carried two more announcements. Shah virtually inaugurated the **western regional office of the Central Registrar of Cooperative Societies** in Pune, and launched **Bharat Taxi**, a cooperative-owned cab service, in the city. The Ministry of Cooperation put out the details in a release at 7:49 pm.

## What NHEC will make

The release describes NHEC as the cooperative that once supplied sugar mills and centrifugal machinery around the world. Shah said its mills were exported to **11 countries, including Zambia, Ethiopia and Vietnam**, and that at its peak the order book was so full that delivery of a new order could take about four years. The factory then stopped operating, and its roughly 110 acres were increasingly exposed to illegal encroachment.

The revived unit will not be limited to sugar mills. According to Shah, it will build **power-generation plants for sugar mills, compressed biogas (CBG) plants, ethanol plants and dairy-processing equipment**, and will make containers for export. A second phase is meant to produce containers for the railways, the defence sector and overseas buyers. He said every rupee earned would go to cooperative institutions, not to private shareholders.

## How the revival was put together

Shah laid out the steps in some detail. The **National Dairy Development Board (NDDB) first took over as administrator** of the institution. Audits that had been pending for two years were then completed, and the board of directors was reconstituted. At its first meeting on **October 9, 2026**, the board resolved to rebuild the factory.

The money came from within the cooperative sector. Shah named **Amul, NDDB, the National Cooperative Development Corporation (NCDC), IFFCO, NABARD and IPL** as the institutions that mobilised the investment. A plan has also been prepared, he said, to reschedule all of NHEC's liabilities and loans and settle them transparently. During the programme, NHEC exchanged a letter of intent with IDMC Limited, and share certificates were handed to Bharat Taxi drivers.

## A new registrar's office for the west

The Pune office of the Central Registrar will **regulate multi-state cooperative societies** in Maharashtra, Gujarat, Goa, Dadra and Nagar Haveli, and Daman and Diu. Shah said a large number of such societies operate in the region and that different areas had been asking for an office of this kind. Until now the registrar's regulatory work for them was handled from elsewhere; the release does not say where. For a society with branches in more than one state, a regional office means filings, complaints and inspections can be dealt with closer to home.

## Bharat Taxi begins

The cab service is built on the cooperative model. Shah said drivers of many private taxi companies had for years surrendered a part of their earnings as commission and in many cases faced exploitation. In Bharat Taxi, he said, **drivers, called Sarathis, become owners** of the enterprise, and their full earnings are credited directly to their bank accounts with no commission deducted.

He asked that the service be extended to **Nagpur, Nashik, Kolhapur and Solapur** after Pune, and said it would spread across the country. The release does not give a date for any of these cities, a fleet size, or the fares.

## What the Centre says it has done for the sector

Shah linked the revival to what he called a series of steps since the Ministry of Cooperation was set up. He cited **relief of about ₹15,000 crore to cooperative sugar mills** and income tax relief of about **₹3,000 crore a year**, along with measures to strengthen primary agricultural credit societies (PACS), district cooperatives and dairies. These are the minister's figures from the release; no breakdown was published with them.

He also said a circular economy in the dairy and sugar sectors would give farmers extra income from cattle dung and other by-products, and would reduce imports by making more use of what sugar mills produce. In its wider debate on the sector, the Centre recently formed a panel under former NITI Aayog member Ramesh Chand to look at [sugarcane growers' price demands](/news/centre-sets-up-committee-sugarcane-farmers-sugar-sector).

## The political part of the speech

Shah's remarks at the same function on Rahul Gandhi's Bharat Summit speech were reported separately in our [earlier story](/news/amit-shah-pune-rahul-gandhi-bharat-summit-defaming-india). The PIB release records him saying that all parties, in government or opposition, have a responsibility to protect the country's reputation and democratic institutions on international platforms. Congress had not replied by the time this report was written.

## Why it matters and what next

For cooperative sugar mills, which have to buy crushing, power and ethanol equipment, a cooperative-owned manufacturer is meant to keep the machinery profit within the sector. For Maharashtra's cooperatives, the registrar's office brings regulation to Pune. For drivers, the test of Bharat Taxi will be whether the no-commission promise holds as the fleet grows.

The next markers are concrete. NHEC's liabilities are to be rescheduled, construction has to begin on the 110-acre site, and the first sugar mills are due to roll out before **December 2027**. Whether the service reaches Nagpur, Nashik, Kolhapur and Solapur, and how soon, has not been announced.
