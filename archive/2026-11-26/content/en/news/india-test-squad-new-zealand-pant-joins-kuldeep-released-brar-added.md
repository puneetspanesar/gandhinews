The BCCI has **cleared Rishabh Pant to join India's Test squad** in Wellington, added fast bowler Gurnoor Brar to the group for the two-Test series against New Zealand and released left-arm wrist-spinner Kuldeep Yadav, the board said on Saturday, November 14, 2026. The first Test begins on **November 19 at the Basin Reserve**. This report is as of 6 am IST on Sunday, November 15.

Pant, the wicketkeeper-batter, had been named on October 16 only subject to fitness. The BCCI medical team has now signed him off and he is with the squad in New Zealand.

## The squad as it stands

Shubman Gill leads the 16-man side with KL Rahul as vice-captain. The other 14 are Yashasvi Jaiswal, Devdutt Padikkal, Pant, Dhruv Jurel, Ravindra Jadeja, Anshul Kamboj, Jasprit Bumrah, Prasidh Krishna, Akash Deep, Sai Sudharsan, Nitish Kumar Reddy, Washington Sundar, Mohammed Siraj and Gurnoor Brar. Pant and Jurel are the two wicketkeepers.

Brar takes the place Kuldeep leaves. The BCCI said the Men's Selection Committee "has added Gurnoor Brar to the Indian squad", and that he "will now link-up with the members of the Test squad and will not be available for the fifth ODI".

## Pant's road back

Pant hurt his side while captaining Rest of India at the Irani Cup in Srinagar from October 1 to 4. He began rehabilitation at the BCCI's Centre of Excellence in Bengaluru on **October 23**, and the process was extended after he missed the first two India A four-day games against New Zealand A. He was also unavailable for the third, played from November 7 to 10 at Lincoln, which ended in a draw after rain washed out the last day. That left the series between the two A sides level with all three matches drawn.

His arrival gives India a first-choice keeper in the camp with four days to go before the first Test, and keeps Jurel as cover.

## Brar called up mid-series

Brar has not played a Test, but he has been one of India's more productive bowlers in the one-day series. His **4 for 30 in the second ODI at Wellington** was his best return in the format, and it bowled New Zealand out for 134 before rain gave India a four-run win on DLS. India have since lost the fourth ODI by 244 runs and lead the series 2-1 going into the finale at Bay Oval, Mount Maunganui, on Sunday.

Brar leaves the ODI group before that match and heads straight to Wellington. The BCCI said "there will be no replacement for the final ODI", so India will pick from the players who remain in the Mount Maunganui party. The one-day squad is led by Shreyas Iyer, with Ishan Kishan as vice-captain, and includes Rohit Sharma, Virat Kohli, Ruturaj Gaikwad, Tilak Varma, Axar Patel, Naman Dhir, Auqib Nabi, Arshdeep Singh, Kumar Kushagra, Mohammed Siraj, Yash Thakur and Kuldeep Yadav.

## Why Kuldeep goes

The BCCI said Kuldeep "will be released from the Test squad in order to play the ongoing domestic cricket back in India". He stays in the ODI squad for Sunday's match and flies home afterwards. The board gave no other reason.

The decision fits the conditions India expect. The Tests are in Wellington and, according to reports, Christchurch from November 27, and the surfaces are expected to suit seam bowling, according to reports. With Bumrah, Siraj, Prasidh Krishna, Akash Deep and Kamboj in the party, plus Nitish Kumar Reddy as a seam-bowling all-rounder, India have little room for a second spinner beside Jadeja and Sundar, both of whom bat in the top eight.

Kuldeep had played two Tests this year and taken four wickets, according to figures reported on Saturday. He was also wicketless for 73 runs in the fourth ODI at Mount Maunganui, as reported.

## What happens next

India play the fifth ODI at Bay Oval on Sunday, with a 7:30 am IST start, and then turn to the Tests. The first is at the **Basin Reserve in Wellington from November 19**. The second is due on November 27, in Christchurch according to reports. Gill, who left the ODI leg early to prepare, will lead the side in both.
