The leaders of the Quad met in Manila on Tuesday, November 17, 2026, **the first Quad leaders' meeting in two years**, and agreed that the four countries will hold a **joint maritime exercise in 2027** using P-8 and P-3C patrol aircraft. Prime Minister Narendra Modi, US President Donald Trump, Australian Prime Minister Anthony Albanese and Japanese Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi met on the sidelines of the ASEAN-India and East Asia Summits, according to reports of the meeting and its joint statement. The joint statement describes the exercise as the first of its kind in a Quad context.

## What was said

Before the talks began Mr Trump called Mr Modi "a very tough man" and said the Japanese leader was "even tougher", which drew laughter. Mr Modi called the Quad "a constructive and trusted partnership" for India and said the meeting should set concrete goals for its next phase. "Manila and Quad have shared an age-old and special bond," he said, recalling that officials of the four countries first met in Manila in 2007 and that the dialogue was revived there in 2017. Foreign ministers have met five times since January 2025.

## What the joint statement says

As reported, the leaders **opposed any effort to alter the Indo-Pacific status quo by force or coercion**, warning that it could raise tensions and weaken regional peace. They voiced concern about economic coercion, in particular "arbitrary export restrictions", and committed to reduce dependence on single suppliers of critical minerals used in semiconductors, electric vehicles, batteries and defence technology. They backed freedom of navigation and safe maritime transit corridors, reaffirmed ASEAN unity and centrality, and condemned terrorism and violent extremism in all forms, including cross-border terrorism.


## The initiatives

The leaders announced or welcomed:

- an inaugural **Quad Business Leaders' Forum** in early 2027, which India will host, to promote commercial partnerships;
- progress on the **Quad AI Accelerator**, which supports access to trusted AI systems, technical assistance and deployment;
- an Indo-Pacific Maritime Surveillance Collaboration to improve information-sharing and maritime domain awareness;
- the joint P-8 and P-3C exercise in 2027;
- closer cooperation on cybersecurity and protection of critical infrastructure;
- the Quad Partnership for Cable Connectivity and Resilience, with an expanded Australian contribution that includes electricity cables, alongside planned undersea cable projects in the Indian Ocean.

## How we got here

The last meeting of the four leaders was hosted by then President Joe Biden in Delaware on **September 21, 2024**. India was due to host the next summit but it did not take place, amid uncertainty over Mr Trump's travel. On Monday the White House confirmed Mr Trump would take part, with Assistant Press Secretary Olivia Wales saying he would join "a multilateral meeting with Quad leaders" on Tuesday evening. The groundwork included the San Francisco Declaration on protecting AI and critical-technology supply chains, which India joined; our report on the [Pax Silica declaration](/en/news/pax-silica-san-francisco-declaration-india-signs-ai-supply-chains) has the detail. Earlier on Tuesday Mr Modi addressed the ASEAN-India and East Asia Summits, covered in [our report from Manila](/en/news/modi-asean-india-summit-manila-november-16-18).


## Why it matters

For India the practical points are the business forum it will host, the critical-minerals language that bears on electronics and battery supply chains, and a dated operational commitment rather than another statement. Reports say it gives New Delhi room to deepen ties in technology, manufacturing and resource security while keeping its strategic autonomy. A separate Modi-Trump bilateral was not reported, and the two governments' trade differences remain on a separate track, set out in [our report on the trade talks](/en/news/india-us-trade-talks-plateau-russia-oil-tariff-law).

## What happens next

The Business Leaders' Forum is planned for early 2027 in India, and the joint exercise follows in 2027; no dates have been announced for either. Mr Trump goes on to the APEC meetings in China this week, and Mr Modi was due back in India on Wednesday, November 18.
