Haryana Chief Minister **Nayab Singh Saini said in Patiala on Saturday, November 14**, that the people of Punjab have decided to elect a Bharatiya Janata Party government under Prime Minister Narendra Modi in the near future. Speaking to reporters in the Punjab town, he said party membership in the state was rising: "A large number of people are joining the BJP."

Saini's remarks came as the BJP prepares a **Scheduled Caste outreach campaign** that runs from Punjab to Uttar Pradesh, the two states where the community and the following of Sant Ravidas carry weight in elections due in early 2027.

## What Saini said

According to reports of his comments, Saini turned his fire on both of Punjab's other big parties. "The Congress has betrayed the people of Punjab," he said, and argued that the ruling Aam Aadmi Party had **betrayed voters even more**. Jobs were the main example he gave: both parties, he said, had failed to deliver on the promises they made at election time.

He did not, in the remarks reported so far, name a seat target or a possible alliance partner. The BJP's phrase for the model he invoked, a "double-engine" government, means the same party in power in the state and at the Centre. Haryana, which Saini leads, is one of the states the party points to when it makes that argument.

## The yatra from Jalandhar

The party's next big move is the **Samrasta Sankalp Yatra**. BJP national president Nitin Nabin will flag it off from Jalandhar on **November 18**, and it will run through to Varanasi, where it ends on **December 3**. The timing is deliberate: the campaign coincides with the **650th birth anniversary of Sant Ravidas**, who is revered by a large section of Dalit voters in Punjab and in eastern Uttar Pradesh. Varanasi is his birthplace.

Jalandhar sits in the Doaba belt, where the Ravidassia community has long had political influence. Nabin has been taking the party's message to voters in other states in recent weeks; on November 4 he was due to lead a foot march in Yogi Adityanath's home town of Gorakhpur, as we reported at the time.

## How the Punjab contest stands

The Punjab Assembly's term ends on March 16, 2027, and the Election Commission is expected to hold the poll with four other states in February and March, as we reported in October.

AAP is further along. It announced its first 40 candidates on October 31 and **31 more on November 12**, taking it to 71 of the 117 seats. The second list dropped agriculture minister Gurmeet Singh Khuddian and replaced sitting MLAs in at least five other constituencies, while giving tickets to two of the party's Lok Sabha members. Chief Minister Bhagwant Mann announced the list in Delhi.

The BJP has been adding to its ranks in Punjab at the same time. Earlier this month, three AAP legislators, including former IPS officer Kunwar Vijay Pratap Singh, joined the party in New Delhi after Kunwar Vijay Pratap resigned his seat. That defection left Amritsar North vacant; AAP has named Karamjit Singh Rintu as its candidate there.

## Why the statement matters

A Haryana chief minister campaigning in Punjab is routine for the BJP, which sends leaders from neighbouring states to build momentum before elections. What Saturday's remarks do is set the line the party will take into the yatra: that Punjab's voters are already moving its way, and that both AAP and Congress have failed them on jobs.

That claim is a political prediction and has not been tested at the ballot box. The BJP has traditionally been the junior partner in Punjab politics, in alliance with the Shiromani Akali Dal, and reviving that alliance has been discussed publicly; Saturday's remarks, as reported, made no mention of it.

For voters, the first sign will be the yatra itself. Its route through Jalandhar and onward into Uttar Pradesh means Punjab's Dalit electorate will be the first audience for the BJP's pitch.

## What happens next

Nabin flags off the Samrasta Sankalp Yatra from Jalandhar on **November 18**, and it reaches Varanasi on December 3. AAP still has 46 seats to announce. The Election Commission has not announced a schedule for the 2027 polls.
