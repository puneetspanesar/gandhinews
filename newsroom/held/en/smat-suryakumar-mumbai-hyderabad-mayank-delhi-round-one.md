Suryakumar Yadav set the tone for Mumbai's Syed Mushtaq Ali Trophy campaign with an **unbeaten 60 at a strike rate of 285.71**, which is 60 off 21 balls. Mumbai, whom he captains, chased down Hyderabad's 172 in **just 13.1 overs** and finished on 174 for 3 on Saturday, November 14, the opening day of the 38-team tournament.

The day belonged to the batters, with one big exception in Indore. At the Holkar Stadium, Delhi's **Mayank Yadav took 5 for 14 in three overs**, his first five-wicket haul in T20 cricket, as Jammu and Kashmir were bowled out for 140 chasing 244.

## Mumbai's chase

Hyderabad were bowled out for 172 in their 20 overs. Mumbai needed 173 and barely noticed the target. Suryakumar finished unbeaten on 60 and **Musheer Khan made 51**, so the two of them took the chase away from Hyderabad well before the 14th over.

It was the kind of start Mumbai wanted in Group D. As [we reported when the squads were named](/en/news/smat-2026-squads-captains-sooryavanshi-bihar-surya-mumbai-november-14), Suryakumar leads a Mumbai squad that also has Sarfaraz Khan, Shivam Dube and Shardul Thakur. The win came with nearly seven overs unused, which also helps net run rate if the group is tight at the end.

## Mayank Yadav's spell

Delhi batted first in Indore and made **243 for 6**. Himmat Singh scored an unbeaten 63 and Priyansh Arya added 58. Jammu and Kashmir started well and were **47 for 1 after four overs** in their chase, with Yawer Hassan going on to make 70.

Then Mayank came on. He bowled Shubham Khajuria, removed Vivrant Sharma, Abdul Samad and Rydham Sharma, and bowled Brijesh Sharma to finish with **5 for 14**. J&K lost their last nine wickets for 93 runs and were all out for 140, a defeat by **103 runs**. By the figures, it is the second-best return by a Delhi bowler in the tournament's history, behind Suyash Sharma's 5 for 13 against Madhya Pradesh in 2023, and he is only the third Delhi bowler to take five wickets in a SMAT match.

Mayank is one of several players using the tournament to stay in view, while India's selectors have already named the squad for the New Zealand Tests, as [we reported on Monday](/en/news/india-new-zealand-first-test-wellington-thursday-wtc-stakes-gill-latham).

## The rest of round one

All 19 matches had a result. Karnataka made 184 and beat Bengal, who finished on 165 for 9, by **19 runs**. Karun Nair scored 53 for Karnataka, and Sudip Kumar Gharami made 65 for Bengal in the chase. Bengal's squad includes Mohammed Shami.

**Chandigarh beat Baroda with one ball to spare**, 149 for 7 against 148 for 6, in a low-scoring finish in which Baroda's Krunal Pandya took 2 for 7 in four overs and also scored an unbeaten 40. Punjab chased 191 with 86 from Vihaan Malhotra, and Tamil Nadu beat Railways by six runs. Rajasthan overhauled Uttar Pradesh's 171 for 6 with Abhijeet Tomar's 63. As [we reported on Sunday](/en/news/sooryavanshi-bihar-lose-saurashtra-youngest-captain-smat-day-one), Gujarat's 250 for 9 was the highest total of the day, and Vaibhav Sooryavanshi lost his first match as Bihar captain.

## Why it matters

The tournament is a final chance to impress IPL scouts before the auction, so uncapped players and veterans alike are playing for attention. Each team in the Elite groups plays **seven league matches in 13 days**, so one big result, whether a 13.1-over chase or a 103-run win, shapes the table early. For Mumbai and Delhi, a win in the first round of a short tournament counts for more than it would in a long league.

For Suryakumar, an unbeaten 60 at that strike rate is a strong start to a tournament in which he is also one of the veterans in focus.

## What happens next

The league stage runs until **November 26**, the Super League from **November 30 to December 4**, and the final is on **December 6**. Mumbai and Delhi go into round two as winners.
