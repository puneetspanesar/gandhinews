Leader of Opposition Rahul Gandhi on Friday asked Home Minister Amit Shah to persuade Prime Minister Narendra Modi to accept **an open debate** with him, and said he would take part on any platform, at any time.

In a post on X addressed to Shah, Gandhi wrote: "Amit Shah ji, don't confuse Modi ji." He added: "Just convince him to have an open debate with me. Don't be afraid." He had earlier run a poll on social media asking young Indians whether they wanted to see the debate, and tagged Rajnath Singh, Nitin Gadkari, Shivraj Singh Chouhan and Yogi Adityanath, asking them to persuade the Prime Minister.

## Shah's reply

Speaking at a rally in Karnal, Haryana, part of the BJP's "Save Democracy, Remove Anarchy" campaign, Shah **told Gandhi to take his concerns to Parliament**. "Come and discuss them inside Parliament," he said, adding: "But you won't be able to handle the answers; as always, you will just run away."

On Friday Shah also described the Congress "Vote Bachao" yatra as more like a "Ghuspatiya Bachao Yatra", framing the protests as a campaign to shield infiltrators.

## The SIR dispute

The exchange is part of the row over the **Special Intensive Revision of electoral rolls**. Opposition parties have protested in several states, demanding that the exercise be cancelled and that Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar resign. On Thursday, Siddaramaiah and D K Shivakumar led an INDIA bloc protest at Freedom Park in Bengaluru.

On Friday the Election Commission wrote to the Chief Electoral Officers of Delhi, Maharashtra, Haryana, Andhra Pradesh, Jharkhand, Telangana and Nagaland that **voters marked Absent, Shifted, Dead or Duplicate** whose enumeration forms were not received will get another chance to seek inclusion in the final rolls. Booth-wise lists will be displayed at polling stations and local offices and put on the CEO websites, and Booth Level Officers will visit homes.

## What next

Modi has not responded to the challenge in the statements available so far. A Cockroach Janta Party protest against SIR is planned in Kochi on Saturday, and the Samajwadi Party is due to release its "Jan-Man Manifesto" on November 26.
