India have done the hard part in Wellington. **New Zealand were bowled out for 134 in 41 overs** on Saturday in the rain-hit second ODI, leaving the visitors a target of 135 to go 2-0 up in the five-match series after their five-wicket win in Auckland.

Shubman Gill won the toss and chose to bowl after a wet outfield and about an hour of rain cut the game to 47 overs a side. "We will bowl first. No surprises, the conditions are overcast and the wicket was under covers," the India captain said. His counterpart Mitchell Santner had said he would have done the same: "Looks like a lovely day to bowl first, but we'll see how the wicket plays and put up a score."

## How the innings fell apart

The new-ball pair did the early damage. Auqib Nabi removed Tim Seifert for a duck and Will Young for 6, **leaving New Zealand 11 for 2**. Anshul Kamboj then had Henry Nicholls for 11 to make it 23 for 3.

Gurnoor Brar did the rest of the middle-order work, **finishing with 4 for 30 in 10 overs**. He took Tom Latham and Glenn Phillips in quick succession and later Santner and Nathan Smith. Axar Patel dismissed Daryl Mitchell and Kyle Jamieson for 2 for 28, and Kamboj ended the innings by getting Blair Tickner for 2, finishing with 2 for 25.

Mitchell's **55 off 104 balls** was a lone hand, and Santner's 31 off 38 was the only other score of note. Arshdeep Singh went wicketless but conceded just 16 runs in his 7 overs.

New Zealand played without Devon Conway, who is out of the series with concussion. The chase has Rohit Sharma, Gill and Virat Kohli, who made 74 in the first ODI, in the line-up.
