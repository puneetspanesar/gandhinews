Rajat Paliwal held the Services innings together at Palam on Monday, finishing **111 not out off 196 balls** as Delhi bowled the visitors out on the first day of their Ranji Trophy round four match.

The Services captain walked in with his side in trouble. Navdeep Saini had removed opener Shubham Rohilla for a duck off the second ball of the match, and Services slipped to 44 for four in the 18th over after choosing to bat at the Airforce Complex Ground.

Paliwal rebuilt first with Mohit Ahlawat, who hit three fours and two sixes in a brisk 33 off 49 balls, and then with Pulkit Narang, who made **a patient 30 off 89 balls**. The captain hit ten fours and kept the strike well enough to reach three figures as the lower order fell around him.

Delhi's seamers shared the work. **Aman Chaudhary took four for 79**, and Saini added two more wickets after his opening burst to finish with three for 61. Off-spinner Hrithik Shokeen took two for 61. Services were **all out in 88 overs**, leaving Paliwal stranded on 111.

With 88 overs gone, the first day was all but done when Services were dismissed. Delhi's task in the days ahead is to build a first-innings lead on a pitch where Paliwal showed that patience brings runs, against a Services attack that will want early wickets.

The match is scheduled to run until Thursday, 5 November.
