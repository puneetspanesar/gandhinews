BJP president Nitin Nabin will **lead two street marches on Monday**, in Bhagalpur in Bihar and in Kolkata, as the party widens its "Loktantra Bachao, Arajakta Hatao" (Save Democracy, Remove Anarchy) campaign against the INDIA bloc.

The Bhagalpur march is due to start **at Lajpat Park around 11 am** and end at Seva Jyoti Chowk at 12:30 pm. In the afternoon Nabin moves to Kolkata, where the march will leave College Street at 3 pm and reach Dharmatala at 5:30 pm. State BJP presidents, senior leaders and party workers are expected to walk with him.

The BJP calls the campaign a movement against the opposition's **"lies, deception, conspiracies and anarchy"**. It says its workers are going from village to village and house to house to counter the opposition's account of the electoral rolls.

The marches follow the INDIA bloc's rally at Ramlila Maidan in Delhi on Sunday, where opposition parties demanded the BJP's removal over alleged "vote chori". Rahul Gandhi told the crowd that Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar would one day be removed and **jailed for "treason"**.

Nabin led the first march of the campaign in Rohtak on Sunday. There he said the Congress "should have sought atonement" for the 1984 anti-Sikh riots on their anniversary instead of staging a protest, and accused the party of **"double standards"**. Union Home Minister Amit Shah takes the same campaign to Hyderabad on Monday afternoon.
