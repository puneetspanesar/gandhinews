Assam's government has put the toll in the head-on collision at Rongajan, in Golaghat district, at **23 dead**, and Chief Minister Himanta Biswa Sarma has said he will go to the site on **Saturday, November 14, 2026**. The vehicle, described in later reports as a Traveller, hit a container truck on National Highway 715 on Friday afternoon. Our [first report](/en/news/kaziranga-rongajan-minibus-container-truck-collision-23-dead) carried the early facts. This one covers what has come out since about 8 pm IST on November 13.

## The toll and the injured

Sarma's figure of **23** matches the count first given by a Golaghat magistrate. Some reports still carry 24, and none of the reports read had a final reconciled number, so the figure should be treated as the latest official count and not the last. The state government has said post-mortems will be carried out on Saturday, which is when a final list of the dead is likely to be settled.

On the injured, the picture is clearer. Many were taken first to **Kohora Model Hospital**, the nearest facility, and then to the hospital at **Bokakhat**. The seriously hurt were later moved on to **Jorhat Medical College and Hospital**. No report read this evening gave a fresh count of the injured or a breakdown of who is critical, and earlier figures of between 11 and 20 had not been revised.

## Who the victims were

Authorities had **not released the names** of the dead when the latest reports were filed. What is reported is that most of the passengers were **students**, and that two of the dead were women. The vehicle was travelling from Bokakhat towards Nagaon, which fits the account of college students heading home. Until the district administration publishes a list, families are depending on the hospitals and the police for identification, and Saturday's post-mortems will be the step that turns unnamed bodies into names.

## What the Chief Minister has said

Sarma wrote on X that a "very tragic accident took place in Kaziranga" and told officials to ensure the **best possible treatment** for the injured. He said the government would "definitely conduct a thorough investigation into what caused the accident and how it happened", and that he would take stock of the situation personally. Cabinet minister Atul Bora, the local MLA, is at the site overseeing relief and rescue work, and a second minister, Ranoj Pegu, was sent there as well.

## Ex gratia

A published report headline says Prime Minister Narendra Modi has announced **ex gratia** for the families of the dead and the injured. We could not open that report or the Prime Minister's Office post, and no report we could read gave a figure, so no amount is stated here. The state government has said the affected families will get support from Assam, but no state compensation figure had been announced either. Both amounts should come in the Prime Minister's Office and Chief Minister's posts or a PIB release; we will report them once they are confirmed.

## The police case and speed on NH 715

Police have launched an investigation, and reports say it is **not yet clear** whether speeding, visibility or something else caused the collision. Earlier police and ministerial statements named speed and a breach of the restrictions near the park, and the minibus was reported to carry 12 earlier challans. What the later reports do not contain is the case itself: **no report read gave the sections invoked**, an arrest or the status of the container truck driver, so we do not state them.

Nor is there any confirmed new enforcement on the highway. No report read this evening mentioned a speed-camera drive, checkpoints or a change to the limits on the stretch through the park. Whether the state announces one is a fair test of the promise of a thorough investigation, and Sarma's visit on Saturday is the first chance to say so.

## Why it matters

The highway runs along the edge of Kaziranga National Park, where speed limits apply because the road crosses animal corridors, yet the vehicle's record of repeated challans shows that the penalties had not stopped it from being on the road. For families, the questions are practical: names, bodies released after post-mortem, treatment bills for the injured at Jorhat, and compensation. For drivers on NH 715, the question is whether enforcement changes.

## What happens next

On **Saturday, November 14**, post-mortems are due, Sarma is expected at the site in Golaghat district, and the identities of the dead should become clear. The ex gratia amounts, the police case and any enforcement steps on NH 715 are the three things still to be confirmed.
