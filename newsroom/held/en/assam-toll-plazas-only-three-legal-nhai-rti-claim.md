## What we found

The claim is misleading. An RTI reply from the National Highways Authority of India (NHAI), dated November 11, 2026, did list **only three toll plazas: Madanpur, Nazirakhat and Raha**. An Assam youth group, Asomiya Yuva Mancha, held it up at a press meet as proof that every other toll plaza in the state is unauthorised and demanded that those without proof of legality be removed.

The reply cannot carry that weight. It answered for **NHAI's Guwahati Project Implementation Unit (PIU) only**, and NHAI runs four such units in Assam. On November 26 NHAI's regional office in Guwahati issued a clarification calling the "only three" impression "factually incorrect" and naming the other plazas.

## The evidence

### 1. What the RTI reply covered

Three regional reports of November 19 describe the same reply: it named Madanpur, Nazirakhat and Raha, said no plazas were under construction in the Guwahati PIU area, pointed the applicant to the Rajmargyatra portal for toll rates and listed amenities such as ambulances and patrol vehicles. **All three plazas fall under the Guwahati unit.** Two of the reports themselves noted that the reply did not say these were the only authorised plazas in Assam.

### 2. What NHAI says

NHAI's clarification, issued through the Press Information Bureau in Guwahati, says the RTI information "pertains exclusively to the toll plazas under the jurisdiction of the Guwahati Project Implementation Unit". It lists **four PIUs in Assam: Guwahati, Kaziranga, Bongaigaon and Silchar**, and says it "would not be appropriate" to draw conclusions about all plazas in Assam from one unit's three. It then names the plazas: Telipara, Patgaon, Dahalpara, Galia, Madanpur, Nazirakhat, Raha, Rangamati, Sulung, Ratoa, Mikirati, Manderdisa and Balachera.

### 3. The group's own list does not add up

The group named ten "other" toll points, among them Madanpur in Kamrup. But **Madanpur is one of the three plazas in the RTI reply**, so the group's list counts it on both sides. The other names on its list, such as Rangamati, Sulung, Balachera and Manderdisa, all appear in NHAI's list of 13.

### 4. What we could not check

Whether each plaza has a valid fee notification is a separate question, and one the RTI reply does not touch. We could not open NHAI's toll information records for each plaza, so this check does not rate the legality of any particular plaza. It rates only the inference drawn from the RTI reply, which does not hold.

## Why this claim spread

Tolls are a live grievance in Assam. Two new plazas, at Rangamati in Golaghat and Sulung on the Guwahati to Tezpur route, began collecting in January 2026, and the group told reporters that travellers to upper Assam now pay more in tolls than in fuel. **A one-line reading of a real government document** fitted that anger, and it travelled faster than the reply's narrow scope.
