India's young bowlers made light work of Nepal in their Men's Under-19 Asia Cup opener at the Oman Cricket Academy in Al Amerat on Friday, **bowling them out for 128** in 42.3 overs. Captain Lakshya Raichandani had won the toss and chosen to field, and the decision paid off as Nepal's batting never settled.

Nepal, captained by Bipin Sharma, **could not bat out their 50 overs**, falling with more than seven overs unused. Arjun Rajput ended the innings in the 43rd over, leaving India a modest chase on a ground where every match of the tournament is being played.

India need **129 runs to win**, and the required rate of around two and a half an over gives them all the time they need. Raichandani opened the reply himself alongside Aaryavir Sehwag, and the pair came through the opening overs without losing a wicket. The chase was under way when this report was written.

## What comes next

India, the competition's most successful side with **eight titles**, are in Group A with Nepal, Pakistan and the UAE. The top two from each group reach the semi-finals on November 12, and the final is on November 14.

Next up on Sunday are **defending champions Pakistan**, who beat India by 191 runs in last year's final and opened their own defence against the UAE on the next ground on Friday. India close the group against the UAE on November 10.

The squad, picked by the BCCI on October 30, also includes Anvay Dravid, son of Rahul Dravid, and vice-captain Yashbardhan Singh Chauhan.
