India **chose to bowl first** against Nepal on Friday as their Men's Under-19 Asia Cup campaign got under way at Al Amerat in Oman, sending Nepal in to bat in the 50-over Group A match that began at **10:30 am IST**.

The decision puts the onus on Lakshya Raichandani's bowlers to set the tone for the tournament. India, the competition's most successful side with **eight titles**, will then chase whatever target Nepal set on the Ministry Turf 1 ground at the Oman Cricket Academy.

Raichandani leads a 15-member squad named by the BCCI on October 30, with Yashbardhan Singh Chauhan as vice-captain. The batting has drawn most of the attention before the tournament, with **Aaryavir Sehwag and Anvay Dravid** arriving on the back of heavy scoring in the Vinoo Mankad Trophy. Among the bowlers, Ishan Sood took 13 wickets in eight games in that competition.


Nepal are led by **Bipin Sharma**. They also play the UAE and Pakistan in Group A, and a strong total here would give them a real chance of pushing for one of the two semi-final places.

## What comes next

For India, this is the first of three group games. They face **defending champions Pakistan on Sunday**, November 8, a rematch of last year's final, which Pakistan won by 191 runs. India close the group against the UAE on November 10. The top two in each group reach the semi-finals on November 12, and the final is on November 14.

The match is being shown in India on the Sony Sports Network and streamed on SonyLIV.
