Maaya Rajeshwaran Revathi's remarkable week at the Chennai Open ended in the quarter-finals on Friday, when the American **Robin Montgomery won 6-3, 6-3** on Centre Court.

The 17-year-old wild card, ranked 669th in the world when the tournament began, had become the first Indian woman since Sania Mirza in 2012 to reach the last eight of a WTA Tour-level singles event. She is expected to climb into the **top 500** after the week.

## How the match went

Maaya made the better start, breaking Montgomery in the fifth game of the first set. The American broke straight back, held serve to lead 4-3 and then took the set. Maaya's first serve let her down at the key moments, and **three double faults** handed Montgomery chances in the second set, which the American also won.

Montgomery, a left-hander and a former top-100 player, is ranked 151st. She now plays the top seed and defending champion **Janice Tjen** of Indonesia in the semi-finals. Tjen came from a set down to beat sixth seed Polina Kudermetova in her quarter-final.


## Indian hopes that remain

Maaya is not done in Chennai. She plays doubles with **Shrivalli Bhamidipaty**, and the pair are in the quarter-finals. Vaishnavi Adkar and Rutuja Bhosale are also through to the doubles last eight.

The other Indian singles players went out earlier. Vaishnavi Adkar lost in the second round to seventh seed Anastasia Zakharova, and Sahaja Yamalapalli lost her first-round match to Switzerland's Simona Waltert.

## What the week means

Maaya arrived in Chennai off a breakthrough October, when she won her first professional singles title at a W35 event in Lagos, Portugal. The quarter-final run, with wins over Katarzyna Kawa and the qualifier Weronika Falkowska on the way, is expected to take her into the top 500.

The Chennai Open, a WTA 250 event at the SDAT Tennis Stadium in Nungambakkam, ends on Sunday, November 8.
