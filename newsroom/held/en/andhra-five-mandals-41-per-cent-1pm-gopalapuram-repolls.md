Voting in the five Andhra Pradesh mandals that Cyclone Murasu pushed back by a day, and at seven booths where Thursday's poll was spoiled, **reached 41.74% by 1 pm** on Friday, November 6, 2026, with no trouble reported through the morning.

Turnout across the **211 polling stations** voting on Friday rose steadily from 12.56% at 9 am to 26.95% at 11 am, according to State Election Commission figures, before jumping almost 15 points in the next two hours. Polling ends at 5 pm, and anyone in the queue by then will be allowed to vote.

The five mandals were dropped from Thursday's statewide vote after Cyclone Murasu's rain flooded the Godavari belt, and between them they elect **5 ZPTC and 60 MPTC members**. The seven repoll booths, spread over five districts, were added after the commission cancelled Thursday's voting there over rigging, violence and a seized ballot box.

## Gopalapuram leads, Eluru slower

Among the cyclone-hit areas, **Gopalapuram in East Godavari polled 49.9%** by 1 pm. The mandal has 18 MPTC seats and a ZPTC seat spread over 64 polling stations.

The Eluru district mandals, where floods from the cyclone overflowed reservoirs and cut roads earlier in the week, were slower, at **32.23% by 1 pm**. In Velerupadu, close to the Godavari, officials had put turnout at 23% by 11 am across 22 polling stations, with voters lining up for seven MPTC seats and one ZPTC seat.

The four Eluru mandals, Polavaram, Buttayagudem, Jeelugumilli and Velerupadu, have 1,30,996 voters between them. Buttayagudem alone has 42,962 voters and 49 polling stations, and **44 candidates are in the race** for its 15 MPTC seats.

## Repoll booths

Two of the repoll areas ran well ahead of the rest. Kosigi and Arlabanda in Kurnool, where the commission ordered fresh voting after rigging complaints, **recorded the highest turnout at 53.35%** by 1 pm. Pullalacheruvu and Gangavaram in Markapuram district, where a ballot box was seized on Thursday, stood at 51.19%.

Krosuru and Andukuru in Palnadu polled 39.37%, and Kudair and Marutla in Anantapur, where ballot papers were torn during Thursday's clashes, 39.29%. **Ponnam in Srikakulam was lowest at 26.82%**; voting there was cancelled on Thursday after ink stains were found on ballot papers.

## What happens next

Friday's votes join Thursday's, when the commission put statewide turnout at **67.24%, or 1.5 crore voters**. Eluru Collector K Vetriselvi said on Thursday night that preparations for counting were complete. Votes from all of Andhra Pradesh will be **counted on November 7**, and elections for mandal parishad presidents and zilla parishad chairpersons follow on November 11 and 12.
