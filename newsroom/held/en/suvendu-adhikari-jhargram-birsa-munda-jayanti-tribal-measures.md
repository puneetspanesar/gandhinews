West Bengal Chief Minister **Suvendu Adhikari gave land titles to 770 tribal people** at a government programme in Shilda, Jhargram district, on Sunday, and announced a tribal university for the district. The event marked Janjatiya Gaurav Diwas, the 151st birth anniversary of Birsa Munda.

At the same programme he laid the foundation stones of **137 projects worth ₹274.43 crore** and inaugurated 51 completed projects worth ₹47.59 crore, about ₹322 crore in all. The foundation stones include 73 roads, nine school development works and eight hostels. The inaugurations include 13 roads, 10 anganwadi centres and six school development works.

## What was announced

The land titles are forest rights pattas. Data has been collected to extend the process to **1,237 identified forest villages**, the chief minister said. No date for those villages was given.

Adhikari said every resident of Jhargram would get a concrete roof. "We will not build any more mud houses in Jhargram," he said. He also said the government is working to bring tap water to every household in the district.

On jobs, he said: "It is my duty to ensure that educated youths get jobs as per their eligibility." A proposed tribal university in Jhargram and improvements to the district's government hospitals were also part of the announcements. No cost, site or timeline was given for either.

Members of the **Sabar community** will receive benefits under the state's welfare schemes for Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Groups, Adhikari said, even though the national programme for such groups does not cover them. All eligible beneficiaries of the Lokprasar Shilpi allowance are to be enrolled by December 31, and the allowance will be raised in the next state Budget, he said. He also spoke of felicitating 8,000 fishermen across the state.


## Fake certificates

Adhikari said **73 people have been apprehended** for obtaining caste certificates by falsely claiming tribal status, and that the previous Trinamool Congress government had issued Scheduled Tribe certificates to people who were not tribal. "We will ensure that such do not recur," he said. The allegation about the earlier government is the chief minister's own; the party has not been heard on it in the reports available.

On language, culture and land, he told the gathering: "We will fulfil your aspirations concerning language, culture and land rights."

## The political message

Much of the speech was aimed at the opposition. Adhikari accused former Chief Minister Mamata Banerjee of engaging with tribal women mainly for photographs. He said the opposition had voted against Droupadi Murmu in the presidential election, which he used to contrast with Prime Minister Narendra Modi, who he said had begun Janjatiya Gaurav Diwas in 2021. He said his ministry had more tribal ministers than any state government since Independence.

He thanked voters for electing BJP candidates in 16 Assembly constituencies reserved for Scheduled Tribes. "You know who your friends are and who your foes are," he told the crowd.

## How we got here

Jhargram has been a repeated stop for the chief minister since he took office as the ninth chief minister of West Bengal. On October 23, at Kalajhariya block, where Lodhas and Shabars are the main tribal groups, he announced a **₹342 crore special package** for the district.

That package promised foodgrain kits of rice, pulses, oil and soybeans for about 25,000 tribal families in Jhargram and a further 45,000 in areas next to Midnapore district. It also promised Scheduled Tribe scholarships of ₹800 a month for 2 lakh students and 125 days of guaranteed work under an Adivasi Mahila Samridhi Yojana, to be launched soon. At that event he said improving tribal livelihoods was one of his top priorities.

## Why it matters

Jhargram lies in Jangalmahal, the western belt of Bengal where Scheduled Tribe communities such as the Lodha and Sabar live, and where forest land rights, housing and caste certificates are everyday concerns. A title under the forest rights law puts a family's claim to the land it already uses on paper.

The certificate issue is also about who gets reserved jobs and seats. Adhikari said he had 73 people held and that no further fake certificates would be allowed, though the reports do not say under which charges.

## What happens next

The next fixed date is December 31, the enrolment deadline for the Lokprasar Shilpi allowance, which is due to rise in the next state Budget. The tribal university, the extension of titles to 1,237 forest villages and the housing and water pledges have no dates yet. Whether the Trinamool Congress will respond to the charges about tribal certificates and the presidential vote has not been stated.
