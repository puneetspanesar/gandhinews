The investigation into the sting videos that have shaken Karnataka politics has reached the door of Union minister **H D Kumaraswamy**, with Bengaluru police saying on Sunday that a phone at the centre of the case was handed over at Kushak Road in New Delhi, where the JD(S) leader has his official residence.

Bengaluru City Police Commissioner Seemant Kumar Singh said Ritesh Pandey, who was arrested in Delhi last week, and a second man, Vinod Sharma, bought the handset together. "Ritesh and Vinod Sharma had gone to a mobile shop in Karol Bagh in Delhi, purchased a mobile," he told reporters. "This particular mobile, after purchase, both Ritesh Pandey and Vinod Sharma (went) to a place in Kushak road in New Delhi."

Police say the phone was given there to a third man, Prajwal Prasad. "We have got technical evidence in terms of CCTV and other technical details, **financial transactions between Prajwal Prasad and Ritesh Pandey**," Singh said. Notices have been issued to Sharma and Prasad. They have not replied, are treated as absconding, and were reported to have applied for anticipatory bail.

Karnataka Home Minister Priyank Kharge went further. "All technical findings are pointing to the involvement of his (Kumaraswamy) people," he said. "As I said, irrefutable evidence is available. They cannot deny it all. Picture abhi baaki hai."

## How the row began

The case grew out of Kumaraswamy's own claim in October that Siddaramaiah gave up the chief minister's chair after **D K Shivakumar threatened to release sting videos**. Shivakumar, now Chief Minister, answered that "hired" people had tried a sting on him too, pointing to a businessman wearing camera glasses at a meeting in his residence.
