P V Sindhu ended a week of near-misses with a title. The Indian **beat Denmark's Line Kjaersfeldt 21-12, 21-14** in the women's singles final of the Hylo Open, a Super 500 event in Saarbrücken, Germany, on Sunday. The match lasted 39 minutes.

It is Sindhu's **second BWF World Tour title of the season**, after the Japan Open in July. Seven days earlier she had won the first game of the French Open final in Paris before world No. 1 An Se Young came back to beat her.

## How the final went

Kjaersfeldt stayed level until 5-5 in the first game, but Sindhu, seeded second, **led 11-8 at the interval** and pulled clear from there. The Dane closed a 7-3 gap to 7-6 early in the second game. Sindhu went into the break 11-7 up, stretched it to 19-11 and closed the match as net errors crept into Kjaersfeldt's game.

Sindhu came into the final with **a 6-1 head-to-head lead**. Kjaersfeldt's only win had come at the 2025 Sudirman Cup Finals.

"I took the lead from the beginning, and I didn't give her a chance," Sindhu said. "We've played a lot of times, and for me, it was important that I focus on every point."

## A clean week

Sindhu **did not drop a game** all tournament. In the semi-final on Saturday she beat 19-year-old compatriot Anmol Kharb 21-13, 21-16.

The world No. 8 earned 9,200 ranking points, which **keep her on course for the World Tour Finals** at the end of the season. "This gives me a lot of boost and a lot of confidence," she said. "I'm happy that things are going according to plan."

Asked whether she was back to her best, Sindhu replied: "Definitely yes." She added that there was still room to improve. "It's been an amazing week, and I think it's time to go back and celebrate now."

Lakshya Sen was due to play Indonesia's Jonatan Christie in the men's singles final later on Sunday.
