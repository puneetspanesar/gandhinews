Data Patterns, the Chennai-based maker of defence electronics, on Friday reported a **standalone net profit of ₹21.97 crore** for July to September, the second quarter of the 2026-27 financial year. The board approved the unaudited results at a meeting that ran from 2:30 pm to 5:30 pm, and the figures were filed with the stock exchanges in the evening.

Revenue from operations **rose to ₹133.25 crore** in the quarter. That is about 15% more than the roughly ₹116 crore the company booked in April to June, going by its half-year figures. Total income, which includes other income, was ₹141.44 crore.

The extra sales did not lift profit. Total expenses came to ₹111.91 crore, and profit before tax was ₹29.53 crore, so the bottom line was **almost unchanged from the ₹22.06 crore** of the June quarter.

## The half year

For the six months to September 30, Data Patterns earned **₹44.03 crore in net profit** on revenue from operations of ₹249.28 crore and total income of ₹264.78 crore. Profit before tax for the half was ₹59.01 crore.

The company's consolidated numbers, which include its subsidiary, were nearly identical: revenue of ₹133.25 crore and a net profit of ₹21.82 crore for the quarter, and ₹43.88 crore for the half year.

Data Patterns builds radars, electronic warfare systems and avionics for India's armed forces and defence public sector firms, and its results tend to swing with the timing of large deliveries. The company did not announce a dividend with the results.

Its auditor, Deloitte Haskins & Sells, said in its limited review that **nothing had come to its attention** suggesting the statements were materially misstated.
