Prime Minister Narendra Modi, President Droupadi Murmu and Vice President C. P. Radhakrishnan paid floral tributes to Bhagwan Birsa Munda at the Parliament complex in New Delhi on the morning of Sunday, November 15, 2026, his **151st birth anniversary**. Lok Sabha Speaker Om Birla and Union Law Minister Arjun Ram Meghwal joined them as tribal dancers performed. The day is observed as Janjatiya Gaurav Diwas, and it is also Jharkhand's 26th Foundation Day, because the state was carved out of Bihar on Birsa Munda's birth anniversary in 2000.

Mr Modi followed the tribute with **two posts on X**. On Birsa Munda he wrote that he bowed "with deepest reverence on behalf of all the people of our country", and that "the indomitable courage, struggle and sacrifice of Dharti Aaba will continue to inspire every generation" of the country. He said his government was "leaving no stone unturned to ensure dignity, opportunity and prosperity" for tribal brothers and sisters, and asked people to come together to honour the contribution of tribal communities to nation-building.

## Greetings to Jharkhand

In a separate post for the state's Foundation Day, the Prime Minister called Jharkhand **"a unique blend of tribal pride, cultural diversity and natural wealth"**. He pointed to its forests, waterfalls and historic sites, and to its potential in minerals, energy, handicrafts and tourism.

Ms Murmu, herself from the Santhal community, said the state has "a distinctive identity rooted in its rich tribal culture", and named Birsa Munda and Sidho-Kanhu as its best-known sons. On Janjatiya Gaurav Diwas she wrote that the day "is an opportunity to remember our rich tribal heritage", and urged citizens to **preserve that heritage while moving forward on the path of inclusive development**. She hoped the people of Jharkhand would carry the state to new heights.

Mr Birla spoke of the state's "natural splendour, abundant mineral wealth, rich forests and a distinctive tribal cultural heritage" and hoped for progress in education, health, employment and infrastructure, with equal participation across society. Madhya Pradesh Chief Minister Mohan Yadav and Jharkhand Governor Santosh Kumar Gangwar also sent greetings, and the Jharkhand Chief Minister's Office posted its own message paying respects to Birsa Munda. In the state, a "Run for Cleanliness" began from Bapu Vatika at Morhabadi in Ranchi.

## Who Birsa Munda was

Born in 1875 in what is now Jharkhand, Birsa Munda led the Ulgulan, the "Great Tumult", against British rule and against land policies that stripped tribal families of their forests and fields. His slogan, "Abua Dishum, Abua Raj", means **"our land, our rule"**. He was arrested and died in British custody on June 9, 1900, at the age of 25. Tribal communities know him as Dharti Aaba, Father of the Earth, and the Centre set aside November 15 as Janjatiya Gaurav Diwas in his honour.

## The 11 am event at Gadchiroli

The tributes in Delhi are the first half of the Prime Minister's day. At **11 am** he is due to join the Janjatiya Gaurav Diwas programme at Gadchiroli in Maharashtra by video conference. As we reported from the Prime Minister's Office statement of November 14, he will [inaugurate 173 projects and lay foundation stones for 73 more](/en/news/modi-janjatiya-gaurav-diwas-gadchiroli-246-tribal-projects-3000-crore), together worth about ₹3,000 crore. The inaugurations include 38 Eklavya Model Residential Schools and 100 Multi-Purpose Centres under PM-JANMAN, the Tribal Research Institute building in Itanagar and a museum to the Mizo freedom fighter Ropuiliani in Aizawl.

The event closes the Janjatiya Gaurav Pakhwada, the fortnight of tribal outreach that began on November 1. Maharashtra is marking its own Bhagwan Birsa Munda Gaurav Week until November 18.

## How it compares with last year

The government has used the day for large announcements before. At Dediapada in Gujarat on November 15, 2025, the 150th anniversary, the Prime Minister inaugurated and laid foundation stones for works **worth over ₹9,700 crore**, according to the Press Information Bureau. This year's package is roughly a third of that in rupee terms, with 38 Eklavya schools against 42 and 100 Multi-Purpose Centres against 228 a year ago.

The scheme behind much of the list, the Dharti Aaba Janjatiya Gram Utkarsh Abhiyan, was launched at Hazaribagh in Jharkhand on October 2, 2024, with an outlay of ₹79,156 crore over five years for about 63,843 tribal-majority villages. Those are the government's own figures, and how many of the new schools have teachers and students in place is not part of the Prime Minister's Office statement.

## Why it matters

For tribal families, the test of the day is delivery: residential schools that open with staff, service centres that function in the habitation, and the land and forest rights that were Birsa Munda's own cause. For Jharkhand, the anniversary and the Foundation Day fall together every year, and this year the greetings from Delhi come while the state government has [moved its own main celebration to November 28](/en/news/jharkhand-26th-foundation-day-main-event-moved-nov-28-chhath-khelgaon).

## What happens next

Mr Modi's address from Gadchiroli is due at **11 am on Sunday**. His next scheduled engagement is the [ASEAN-India summit in Manila](/en/news/modi-asean-india-summit-manila-november-16-18) from November 16 to 18. What he says on the package, the schools and the centres will be reported once the address is delivered.
