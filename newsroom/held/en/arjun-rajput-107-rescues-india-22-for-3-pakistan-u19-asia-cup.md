Arjun Rajput hit **107 off 85 balls** to drag India Under-19 out of trouble against Pakistan in the Asia Cup Group A match at Al Amerat, Oman, on Sunday, November 8. India, who chose to bat first, were **22 for 3** early on and 42 for 4 at one stage before the 19-year-old left-hander turned the innings round.

Pakistan's new-ball bowlers had done the damage. **Ali Raza removed captain Lakshya Raichandani for a duck** off the second ball and then VK Vineeth for 8, and Rizwanullah dismissed Yashbardhan Singh Chauhan for 1. Aaryavir Sehwag and Anvay Dravid also fell, and India were 104 for 5 after 26 overs.

## Rajput's counter-attack

Rajput reached his fifty off 43 balls and his **maiden youth ODI century in 80 balls**, with 11 fours and 5 sixes in all. He was out with India on 170 for 7 in the 35th over, having scored a big share of the runs on the board. The tail then took the total close to 200: by about 44 overs India were **around 200 for 9**. The final total was not confirmed at the time of writing.

Raza and Niqab Shafiq took three wickets each for Pakistan. Raza's figures were 3 for 36 in 9.4 overs, Shafiq's 3 for 37 in nine.

## Who is Arjun Rajput

Rajput is from Jalandhar in Punjab and trained at the Harbhajan Singh Institute of Cricket. His father, Hoti Ram, ran a chhole-kulche cart to pay for his children's education. This season he captained Punjab U19 in the Vinoo Mankad Trophy and scored **695 runs in eight matches** at an average of 139, including a century in the quarter-final against Chandigarh and an unbeaten 55 off 33 balls in the final against Mumbai. He also took 3 for 8 against Nepal in India's first Asia Cup match.

## What it means for the group

India and Pakistan both won their openers and each has two points. Only the top two teams in the group reach the semi-finals, so the result of Pakistan's chase will go a long way towards settling Group A. India play the UAE on November 10 to finish their group games.

Pakistan had not begun their innings when this report was written, around 2 pm IST.
