India are Asian champions again. Harmanpreet Singh's side came from a goal down to **beat Japan 4-1 in the men's Asian Champions Trophy final** in Mohali on Thursday night, scoring four times in the last 20 minutes to win the title for a record sixth time.

It is their **third title in a row**, after 2023 and 2024, and it ends a tournament in which they won every match. The scoreline matched their league win over Japan in Jalandhar on October 30.

## Japan strike first

Japan, still chasing a first title, made the better start. In the first quarter **Kazumasa Matsumoto ran into the Indian circle**, got past Jugraj Singh and Jarmanpreet Singh and beat the goalkeeper with a reverse flick from a tight angle.

India looked rattled, repeatedly giving the ball away under Japanese pressure. A referral for a penalty corner was turned down, Hardik Singh had a shot saved, and Japan's defence held firm to **lead 1-0 at half-time**. Even India's first penalty corner, in the 33rd minute, came to nothing when Harmanpreet's drag flick went straight at the Japanese goalkeeper.

## How the match turned

The equaliser came from a set piece. From back-to-back penalty corners, Harmanpreet chose a variation instead of his usual flick, and **Shilanand Lakra slotted in the equaliser**. The goal stood after a video referral for a dangerous ball.

India then took over. **Dilpreet Singh put them ahead** with a turn and shot after a driving run by Hardik Singh. Jugraj Singh then **drag-flicked a penalty corner high into the net** for 3-1, with Abhishek making the injection. Vivek Sagar Prasad finished the job, reacting first to a rebound at the edge of the circle and firing low through the goalkeeper's legs.

## A night for the hosts

Punjab Chief Minister Bhagwant Mann met the players on the turf before push-off. The final was also set to be **Nilakanta Sharma's last appearance** in an India shirt.

Two of the scorers carried their semi-final form into the title match. Lakra and Dilpreet had **each scored twice in the 8-1 win** over South Korea on Wednesday, when Harmanpreet, Sukhjeet Singh, Angad Bir Singh and Vivek Sagar Prasad also found the net. India had won all five of their league games before that.

Japan reached the final through a shoot-out win over Malaysia after a goalless semi-final. Runners-up in 2013 and 2021, they are **still waiting for a first title**.
