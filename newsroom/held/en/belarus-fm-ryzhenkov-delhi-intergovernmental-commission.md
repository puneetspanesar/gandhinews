Belarus Foreign Minister Maxim Ryzhenkov is in New Delhi on an official visit that runs to November 18, and on Monday he **co-chairs the 12th Belarus-India Intergovernmental Commission** with Minister of State for Commerce and Industry Jitin Prasada at Vanijya Bhavan. The commission covers trade, the economy, science, technology and culture. No outcome of Monday's meeting had been released when this report was written.

Ryzhenkov arrived on Sunday, November 15, and Ministry of External Affairs spokesperson Randhir Jaiswal confirmed it in a post on X: Ryzhenkov "has arrived in New Delhi on an official visit." The ministry had published the programme two days earlier, on Friday, November 13.

## What the visit includes

The schedule has three fixed points after the commission. Later on Monday, Ryzhenkov joins a **roundtable with members of the Confederation of Indian Industry** at the India Habitat Centre. The sectors listed for that session are manufacturing, industrial technology, engineering, pharmaceuticals and agriculture.

On Tuesday, November 17, he meets Minister of State for External Affairs Kirti Vardhan Singh to review bilateral ties and exchange views on regional and international issues. He leaves India on Wednesday, November 18.

## The commission and the roadmap

An intergovernmental commission is the standing forum where the two governments review the agreements they already have and decide where to add more. For Monday, the stated priorities are **expanding trade in goods**, improving investment, strengthening industrial partnerships and working together on scientific research and technology. Both sides also want to shape a cooperation roadmap for **2026 to 2030**.

The Belarus Foreign Ministry has already given its own reading of where things stand. In a post on X it spoke of "strong momentum in Belarus-India relations", and said bilateral trade has **crossed $1 billion**, work on the 2026 to 2030 roadmap has begun, Belarus has introduced e-visas for Indian nationals and new direct flight connections are planned. It gave no breakdown of the trade figure by year or by product.

## How we got here

The latest visit follows a meeting between Ryzhenkov and External Affairs Minister S Jaishankar on the sidelines of the BRICS summit in September. The two are reported to have discussed trade, investment, connectivity, tourism and people-to-people exchanges.

The relationship is old. India was among the first countries to recognise Belarus as an independent state, in 1991, and formal diplomatic relations began in 1992, when India opened its mission in Minsk. India's first resident ambassador reached Minsk in January 1993, and Belarus opened its embassy in New Delhi in 1998. Besides the commission, the two sides talk through Foreign Office Consultations and a Joint Commission on Military Technical Cooperation, along with other thematic forums. They also sit together in BRICS and the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation.

Air links are recent. Belavia started direct Minsk to Delhi flights in **August 2023**, and the Belarusian side now says more are planned.

## Why it matters at home

For Indian families, the most direct link is education. According to the External Affairs Ministry's figures cited in reports, **1,276 Indians are registered as temporary residents in Belarus, 846 of them students**, most of them studying medicine at state medical universities. Another 99 Indians are registered as permanent residents. Easier visas and direct flights bear on those students and on their parents' costs and travel.

For industry, the CII roundtable puts Belarus's foreign minister in front of Indian manufacturing, engineering, pharmaceutical and agriculture businesses. The roadmap under discussion is meant to set the direction for those sectors up to 2030.

## What happens next

What the commission agreed, and whether the 2026 to 2030 roadmap was settled or left for a later round, will be known once the two governments publish their account of the meeting. Ryzhenkov's meeting with Kirti Vardhan Singh on Tuesday is the last scheduled engagement before his departure on November 18. Until then, the only official statements on the table are the arrival post and the programme.
