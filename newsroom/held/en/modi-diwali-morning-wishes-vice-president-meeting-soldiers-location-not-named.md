Prime Minister Narendra Modi wished the country a happy Diwali on Sunday morning, and has said he will once more spend the festival among soldiers.

His post at **7:05 am** read: "Diwali wishes to everyone! Boundless Diwali wishes to my family members across the country." At 7:15 am he posted that he had met Vice-President C P Radhakrishnan and exchanged greetings, in the words "Met Vice President Thiru CP Radhakrishnan Ji and exchanged Diwali greetings."

## The soldiers' Diwali

In a video message on Saturday, November 7, Modi said he would **continue his tradition of celebrating with soldiers**. "This time also, I will continue the tradition," he said. He did not name the military station or border post.

Over the years the Prime Minister has marked the festival at Siachen base camp in 2014, Gurez in 2017, Longewala in Jaisalmer in 2020 and Kargil in 2022. No location for this year has been announced in anything published so far.

## Message of the festival

In the video he described Diwali as a festival that reflects the country's **resolve against darkness**, and wished everyone happiness, peace and prosperity. A separate written message, published on the government's Jan Sampark portal on the eve of Diwali, spoke of the victory of light over darkness and asked citizens to work towards a Viksit Bharat.

Elsewhere on Sunday, Defence Minister Rajnath Singh marked Diwali eve with troops in north Bengal and is due in Sikkim, and Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath was due in Ayodhya for morning prayers. This report will be updated if the Prime Minister's location is announced.
