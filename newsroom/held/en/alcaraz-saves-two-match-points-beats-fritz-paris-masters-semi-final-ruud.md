Carlos Alcaraz **saved two match points** against Taylor Fritz on Friday night and won 6-7 (10), 7-5, 6-3 on Court Central to reach the first Paris Masters semi-final of his career. The match lasted just under three hours.

Fritz took a 22-point first-set tie-break 12-10 after saving three set points. In the second set Fritz led 5-3, and **the first match point came with Alcaraz serving at 3-5**, after a crosscourt forehand return winner. Alcaraz charged the net, saved it and held. Serving for the match at 5-4, Fritz earned a second match point after a volley exchange, and Alcaraz answered with a passing-shot winner, broke back and won the last two games of the set.

## A decider that tilted

The third set belonged to the Spaniard. He broke early and led 5-2, and although he double-faulted at 5-3, **he won four straight points** to close on his first match point with an ace and a serve followed by a forehand. He made 11 unforced errors in the set, three fewer than in the second.

"Today's match reminds me a little bit of the French Open final last year," Alcaraz said on court, referring to his 2025 final against Jannik Sinner. "The feeling I have today is unmatched."

Alcaraz now leads Fritz 7-1 in their meetings. He won Vienna last week and has **won seven matches in a row**. He is the first Spaniard in the Paris semi-finals since Rafael Nadal in 2020. For Fritz the defeat ended his bid for a place at the ATP Finals in Turin.

## Ruud next

Alcaraz's opponent on Saturday is Casper Ruud, who beat lucky loser Nuno Borges 7-6 (5), 6-4 in one hour 34 minutes. Borges had held all 25 of his service games in the main draw before the match. **Ruud won the tie-break**, saved both break points he faced in the second set and broke once, with his only chance of the match. Alcaraz leads Ruud 6-1 in their meetings.


The other semi-final is between Luca Van Assche and Ben Shelton.
