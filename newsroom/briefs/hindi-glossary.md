# Gandhi News Hindi style and glossary (V1)

## Register
- Natural Hindi news register, as in quality Hindi newspapers and BBC Hindi: clear, readable, not Sanskritised. Use English loanwords that Hindi readers actually use (रेपो रेट, जीडीपी, जीएसटी, बिल is fine in casual use but prefer विधेयक in Parliament stories).
- Adapt, don't translate word for word. Rewrite headlines, standfirsts and SEO descriptions for Hindi readers and Hindi search terms. Keep every fact, figure, date, attribution and qualification exactly as in English. Never add or drop facts.
- No em dashes (—). Use commas, पूर्ण विराम (।), colons or brackets.
- Numbers: Western digits (7.8%, 2026). Lakh/crore: ₹2.04 लाख करोड़, 2.06 करोड़ नाम. Dates: 11 अक्टूबर 2026. Times: सुबह 9:30 बजे.
- Quotes: translate faithfully. If the original remark was made in Hindi and the Hindi wording is in a source, use that wording. Keep the "Source:" line, as "स्रोत:".
- Names: standard Hindi spellings (नरेंद्र मोदी, निर्मला सीतारमण, राहुल गांधी, संजय मल्होत्रा, ज्ञानेश कुमार, अरविंद केजरीवाल, जयराम रमेश, चंद्रबाबू नायडू, ममता बनर्जी, सुवेंदु अधिकारी, विजय).
- Parties: भाजपा, कांग्रेस, तृणमूल कांग्रेस (टीएमसी), आम आदमी पार्टी (आप), समाजवादी पार्टी (सपा), टीवीके, एआईएनआरसी, शिवसेना (यूबीटी), अकाली दल, वाईएसआरसीपी, टीडीपी, सीपीआई(एम).
