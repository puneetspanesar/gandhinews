When we get something wrong, we fix it on the story and say what changed and when. Every correction is also listed below, newest first.

## Ask for a correction

If you think something we published is wrong, write to puneet@thar.co with the link to the story, what you think is wrong and, if you can, a source that shows it. We will reply, and if we were wrong we will correct the story and log it here.

Clarifications that do not change a fact, such as fixing a typing error, are made without a log entry.
