Puneet Panesar is the founder and Editor-in-Chief of Gandhi News. He publishes it through Thar Media and Publications, which he owns outright.

## What the Editor-in-Chief does

- Decides what Gandhi News covers, which stories lead the homepage and how each story is framed.
- Sets our [editorial standards](/standards/) and the rules every story, chart and translation must follow.
- Agrees the position taken in each editorial with the desk.
- Signs off every correction, and answers complaints as our [Grievance Officer](/grievance/).

Stories on Gandhi News carry "From the editorial desk" because they are produced by the desk as a whole, with help from AI tools as described in [how we use AI](/ai-policy/). The Editor-in-Chief is responsible for everything we publish, and for putting it right when we get something wrong.

## Background

Puneet Panesar has worked in digital media, content production and marketing for more than a decade. Before Gandhi News he founded and ran a production studio for video and digital content, and before that a consulting firm that planned social media and marketing for companies. He studied business administration at the Symbiosis Institute of Computer Studies and Research. He is on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/panesarpuneet/).

## Independence

Gandhi News is published by Thar Media and Publications, which Puneet Panesar owns. It is not part of any media group or political organisation, and it is not linked to any political family or party that shares its name. It is paid for entirely by its owner. Our [ownership and funding](/ownership/) page sets this out in full.
