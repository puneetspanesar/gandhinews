Puneet Panesar is the founder, publisher and Editor-in-Chief of Gandhi News. He owns the publication outright.

## What the Editor-in-Chief does

- Decides what Gandhi News covers, which stories lead the homepage and how each story is framed.
- Sets our [editorial standards](/standards/) and the rules every story, chart and translation must follow.
- Agrees the position taken in each editorial with the desk.
- Signs off every correction, and answers complaints as our [Grievance Officer](/grievance/).

Stories on Gandhi News carry "From the editorial desk" because they are produced by the desk as a whole, with help from AI tools as described in [how we use AI](/ai-policy/). The Editor-in-Chief is responsible for everything we publish, and for putting it right when we get something wrong.

## Independence

Gandhi News is not part of any company, media group or political organisation, and it is not linked to any political family or party that shares its name. It is paid for entirely by its owner. Our [ownership and funding](/ownership/) page sets this out in full.
