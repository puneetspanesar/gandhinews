Like many newsrooms, we use AI tools to help with research, drafting and translation. They let us follow more of the news, and publish it in English and Hindi as it happens.

## Where they help

- **Research:** finding and reading official documents, data releases and reports.
- **Drafting:** first versions of stories, explainers and summaries, built from sources we have gathered.
- **Translation:** our Hindi editions.
- **Pictures:** finding licensed photographs of the people and places in the news.

## What does not change

- Every story is held to our [editorial standards](/standards/) and lists its sources.
- Facts, figures and quotes come from those sources. We do not use AI to invent quotes, sources or people.
- We do not present AI-generated images as photographs.
- Our [Editor-in-Chief](/editor/) is responsible for everything we publish and reviews our coverage.

## When we get it wrong

Mistakes are corrected in public under our [corrections policy](/corrections/), whatever their cause. To report one, write to puneet@thar.co.
