AI assists our research, drafting and translation. Editors check and approve everything we publish.

## What AI helps with

- **Research:** finding and summarising official documents, data releases and reports, so an editor can go to the original.
- **Drafting:** first drafts of stories, explainers and summaries, written from sources we have gathered.
- **Translation:** first drafts of our Hindi versions, which a Hindi editor then checks and rewrites where needed.
- **Checking:** spotting numbers or names that do not match their source.

## What an editor always does

- Checks every fact, figure, date and quote against its source before publication.
- Decides what we cover, what the story says and how it is framed.
- Approves every story, headline, chart and translation before it goes live.

## What we do not do

- We do not publish anything an editor has not read and approved.
- We do not use AI to invent quotes, sources or people.
- We do not present AI-generated images as photographs. Any illustration made with AI is labelled.
- Editorials are the editorial desk's own view. AI may help with drafting, but the position is decided by people.

## When AI gets it wrong

The responsibility is ours, not the tool's. Mistakes are corrected under our [corrections policy](/corrections/), like any other error.
