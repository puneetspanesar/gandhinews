Gandhi News is produced by an AI-assisted editorial desk. AI systems do much of the research, writing and translation, working to written rules set by our [Editor-in-Chief](/editor/). Stories are published by the desk as they are ready, and the Editor-in-Chief reviews what has been published and corrects it where needed. The responsibility for everything we publish is ours.

## What AI does

- **Research:** finds and reads official documents, data releases and reports, and the coverage of other news organisations, so each story can be built from its sources.
- **Writing:** drafts news stories, explainers and summaries from those sources, in our style.
- **Translation:** writes the Hindi version of every story, keeping every fact, figure and quote of the English one.
- **Pictures:** finds licensed photos of the people and places in each story.

## The checks every story goes through

- Every story lists its sources at the end, with links where they exist.
- Facts, figures, dates and quotes are taken from those sources. Numbers in our data pages come only from sources named on the page.
- Every picture must show the actual person or place in the story. A story without a correct picture is held back.
- Stories that cannot be checked against a reliable source are not published.

## What the Editor-in-Chief does

- Sets what we cover and the rules every story, chart, picture and translation must follow, in our [editorial standards](/standards/).
- Reviews published stories and corrects or removes anything that falls short.
- Decides every correction and answers every complaint.

## What we do not do

- We do not use AI to invent quotes, sources or people.
- We do not present AI-generated images as photographs. Any illustration made with AI is labelled.
- We do not put a person's name on a story they did not write. Stories carry "From the editorial desk".

## When we get it wrong

The responsibility is ours, not the tool's. Mistakes are corrected in public under our [corrections policy](/corrections/), like any other error. To report one, write to puneet@thar.co.
