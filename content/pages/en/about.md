Gandhi News is an independent Indian news site that covers politics, policy, the economy and Parliament, in English and Hindi.

We are named after Mahatma Gandhi, for whom truth came before everything else. Our promise is in the seal on every page: सत्य, truth. Our motto is "Truth is the only side we take".

## What we publish

- **News:** what happened, with the documents and data behind it.
- **Explained:** plain answers to the questions behind the news.
- **Fact check:** viral claims checked against the record, rated on a [four-point scale](/fact-check/methodology/).
- **Trackers:** data pages we keep up to date, such as [Bills in Parliament](/en/tracker/bills-in-parliament-tracker) and the [economy dashboard](/en/tracker/economy-dashboard). The data behind each one can be downloaded.
- **Opinion:** the view of the editorial desk, always labelled and kept apart from our reporting.

Every story appears in English and in Hindi. The Hindi version is adapted for Hindi readers and keeps every fact, figure and quote of the English one.

## How we work

Every story is credited to the editorial desk rather than to one person. Each report lists its sources at the end, with links where they exist. We prefer primary records: Acts, gazette notifications, court orders, official data and Parliament's own papers.

Our [editorial standards](/standards/) set out the rules we follow. When we get something wrong we correct it in public, on the story and in our [corrections log](/corrections/). We explain [how we use AI](/ai-policy/) and who [owns and funds](/ownership/) us.
