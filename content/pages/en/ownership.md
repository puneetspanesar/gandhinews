Readers should know who owns a news organisation and who pays for it. This page sets that out.

## Who owns Gandhi News

Gandhi News is published by Thar Media and Publications, which also publishes the finance news site [FinanceBharat](https://financebharat.com/). Thar Media and Publications is owned by Puneet Panesar, an individual based in India. It is not part of any media group or political organisation.

Publisher: Thar Media and Publications. Owner: Puneet Panesar (100%).

## Who funds us

Gandhi News is paid for entirely by its owner. It has no investors, grants or donors. This is correct as of November 20, 2026, and we will update this page if that changes.

## How we make money

Gandhi News does not earn any money yet. It may carry advertising in future. If it does, ads will always be clearly labelled and kept apart from the news.

## How we keep that separate from the news

Owners, funders and advertisers have no say over what we report or how. If we report on any of them, we say so in the story. Our [editorial standards](/standards/) apply to them as to everyone else.
