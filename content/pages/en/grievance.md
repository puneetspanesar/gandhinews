Gandhi News publishes news and current affairs content in India. Part III of the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021 asks such publishers to follow a Code of Ethics and to run a grievance redressal process. This page explains ours.

## Grievance Officer

- Name: Puneet Singh
- Designation: Editor and Publisher
- Email: puneet@thar.co
- Phone: +91 77609 06195

## How to make a complaint

Write to the Grievance Officer by email or post. Please include:

1. your name and a way to contact you;
2. the link to the content, and its title and date;
3. what you think is wrong, and which part of the Code of Ethics you believe it breaks;
4. any document or source that supports your complaint.

## What happens next

- We acknowledge your complaint within 24 hours of receiving it.
- We look into it and send you our decision within 15 days.
- If we find we were wrong, we correct the content and log it on our [corrections page](/corrections/).

## If you are not satisfied

If you do not agree with our decision, or do not hear from us within 15 days, you can take it further under the oversight mechanism the Rules set up, run by the Ministry of Information and Broadcasting.

## Other ways to reach us

For corrections and general questions, the quickest route is our [contact page](/contact/).
