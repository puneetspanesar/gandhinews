## What we found

The claim is **misleading**. The figure behind it is real as the CRPF reports it: the force's Director General, **G P Singh**, said on November 2 that 12 months had passed without an **operational fatality**, the first such stretch in 47 years. But Prime Minister **Narendra Modi** put it more broadly when he spoke to security forces at Karanpur in Bastar on Diwali, November 8. As reported, he said that for the first time in 47 years not a single CRPF personnel had lost his life on duty over the past year. Personnel did die while on duty in that period, in ways the CRPF does not count as operational.

## The claim and where it came from

The CRPF chief called the stretch a "significant milestone in the history of CRPF". In his words, "12 consecutive months (One Year) have passed without any Operational fatality in the force," with the last operational fatality on **October 30, 2025**, and the previous such period ending in **January 1979**. Reports of the post say the two men lost on October 30, 2025, were Inspector Kousal Kumar Mishra and Head Constable Mahendra Lashkar, killed in a blast during an area-domination exercise in West Singhbhum, Jharkhand.

Six days later the Prime Minister took the point into his Diwali speech. The text released by the Press Information Bureau, as our earlier report records, has him saying no CRPF jawan died on duty in the past year. The wording changed in transit: "operational" became "on duty".

## The evidence

### 1. No source defines "operational"

None of the reports of the Director General's post defines an operational fatality. One states only that **suicides are not counted** as deaths during official operations. The term therefore appears to cover deaths in action or in an operation, not every death of a serving jawan.

### 2. A sub-inspector died at his post on November 5

Sub-Inspector **Sudhir Swami**, 31, of the 225 Battalion, was posted at Gate 2 of Lok Bhavan, the Governor's residence in Aizawl, Mizoram. Police said he shot himself with his service rifle at about 10:30 am on Thursday, November 5, three days before the speech. The motive is not known and an unnatural death case has been registered. He was on duty, at his post, so on the Prime Minister's wording the claim does not hold. On the Director General's wording it does, since this was not an operation.

### 3. Three CoBRA jawans died in a road accident in Chhattisgarh

On **February 14**, a vehicle carrying CRPF personnel from Jagdalpur to Raipur collided with a car near Khapri in Dhamtari district, Chhattisgarh. Three jawans of the 201 CoBRA battalion and the car's driver died. The report does not say whether they were on an operation, so we do not count this as a death in the field. It does show that the force lost personnel in the region in the very year the claim covers.

### 4. A wider count of deaths exists, but not an official one

One analysis of paramilitary deaths this year found **at least 26 CRPF suicides** so far in 2026 against zero operational deaths. It is that outlet's own tally, not a figure from the Home Ministry or the CRPF, and we do not rely on it for the rating. It is a reason to ask for the official numbers.

## Why this claim spread

The speech was built around the declaration that the country is free of Maoist violence, and a year without a single operational death is the most striking figure that supports it. Dropping the word "operational" turns a precise statistic into a promise that no jawan died at work, which is a different and easily disproved statement.

## What is fair to say

The CRPF's own claim is a real record and nothing found contradicts it: no operational fatality since October 30, 2025. What is not supported is the wider wording, that no jawan died on duty. The Home Ministry or the CRPF could settle it by publishing deaths for the year by cause. If the Prime Minister's text shows he used the narrower word, this rating will be revisited.
