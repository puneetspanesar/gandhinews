## What we found

The Prime Minister's claim is **misleading: it is worded more broadly than the record behind it**. At the CoBRA base in Karanpur, Bastar, on November 8, Narendra Modi said that "for the first time in 47 years, no CRPF jawan lost his life while on duty during the past year." The force's own milestone, announced a week earlier by Director General G P Singh, is that it went **12 months without an operational fatality**. Deaths on duty that were not in operations are a separate count, and several were reported.

## The evidence

### 1. What the Prime Minister said

The PIB text of the address has Modi saying the record came from government policy and the forces' courage and restraint, and he repeated the point. He used the words "on duty", not "in operations". The claim sat inside a longer passage declaring every district free of Naxalism.

### 2. What the CRPF said

On November 1, Director General Singh posted that the force had completed a year with **no operational fatality**, calling it a significant milestone. Reports of the post give the last operational deaths as October 30, 2025, when two personnel were killed by a blast in West Singhbhum, Jharkhand, and put the previous such stretch as ending in January 1979. We could not open the post itself, so these details are the DG's claim as reported, not a document we have read. We also found **no published CRPF series** against which to test "47 years".

### 3. Deaths on duty in the same year

Three CRPF personnel were reported to have died on duty in 2026, none in an operation:

- **April 7:** Constable Abdul Gani was found shot with his service rifle at a camp in Poonch, Jammu and Kashmir, while on sentry duty. Officials said an accidental discharge and suicide were both open.
- **May 5:** Head Constable Surinder Singh collapsed on duty at the Zainapora camp in Shopian and was brought dead to hospital.
- **November 5:** Jawan Sudhir Swami, posted at the Governor's residence in Aizawl, allegedly shot himself with his service rifle while on duty. Police registered an unnatural death case.

These are not combat losses, and the CRPF's claim does not cover them. Read literally, however, "no jawan lost his life while on duty" does not hold.

### 4. The Bijapur deaths do not count against the force

On December 3, 2025, three personnel died in an encounter in Bijapur, Chhattisgarh. All three belonged to the District Reserve Guard, the state police unit, though CRPF and CoBRA units were in the joint team. That is consistent with the CRPF's record.

## Why this claim spread

The line was the emotional centre of a Diwali speech in uniform, released by PIB the same afternoon, and it fits the government's case that the Maoist campaign has ended. "First in 47 years" is easy to repeat. The Prime Minister did not say the figure came from the CRPF, and the force's own wording was left out. If the CRPF publishes its data or the Home Ministry answers in Parliament, this rating will be revisited.
