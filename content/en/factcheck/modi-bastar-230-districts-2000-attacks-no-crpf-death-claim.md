## What we found

The claim is **mostly true**. The Prime Minister's "as many as two thousand" attacks in one year sits close to the Home Ministry's own count of **1,936 incidents in 2010**, and the government says no district has been officially affected by left-wing extremism since March-April 2026. But his figure of **more than 230 districts** in nearly 20 states does not appear in any official series we could read. The ministry's count peaked at **126 districts**.

## The claim

Speaking to CRPF, CoBRA and state police personnel at Karanpur in Bastar on November 8, Modi said: "There was a time when more than 230 districts across nearly 20 states of the country were affected by Naxalism." He added: "There were as many as two thousand Naxalite attacks in a single year." He called it the first Diwali when every district is free of Naxalism, and said no CRPF jawan had died on duty in the past year.

## The evidence

### 1. Attacks in a year: close

PIB's August 1, 2026 release says violence peaked in 2010 with **1,936 incidents**. The Home Ministry told the Rajya Sabha on December 4, 2024 that incidents in 2023 were 73% below 2010. "As many as two thousand" is a fair round figure for the peak, though "attacks" is looser than "incidents".

### 2. Districts: the official peak is 126

The ministry's reply of December 4, 2024 lists 126 affected districts before April 2018, then 90, 70 and 38 (April 2024). PIB says 126 districts were affected in 2014 and 35 were "most affected". No source we read gives 230. The ministry may once have used a wider measure; the speech does not say which it used.

### 3. "Every district free": official, with a footnote

PIB says the number of affected districts fell to zero by March-April 2026 and that India became "effectively free" of left-wing extremism on March 31, 2026. The same PIB research page also says "just 2 districts" in one place. Violence has not stopped: PIB records **33 incidents and 11 deaths in 2026 up to July 29**.

### 4. No CRPF death on duty: partly confirmed

CRPF Director General G P Singh said on July 1 that the force had **zero operational deaths from January 1 to June 30, 2026**. The report did not mention 47 years and did not say how accidental deaths are counted. The Home Ministry's figure of 32 security personnel killed in 2025 up to December 1 covers all forces and does not contradict a CRPF-only claim for a later period.

## Why this claim spread

The address was made on Diwali, released by PIB the same afternoon, and paired with Modi's attack on "intellectual Naxalites", which drew a sharp reply from the Congress. Rahul Gandhi then renewed his debate challenge over the same speech. The headline numbers travelled with the politics.

## What would change this rating

If the Home Ministry publishes a series with 230 districts, or the CRPF confirms a full 12 months without an operational death, this rating will be revisited.
