## What we found

The claim is **misleading**. After a telephone call with Prime Minister Narendra Modi on Monday, November 9, 2026, Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy wrote on X: "India is ready to mediate in establishing food corridors, and we are counting on these efforts to continue."

India's own account of the call is narrower. The Prime Minister's Office said Modi reaffirmed India's human-centric approach, stressed that the conflict must be resolved through dialogue and diplomacy, and conveyed India's **readiness to contribute to all efforts** aimed at a peaceful resolution. Modi's post on X spoke of contributing "in every possible way towards a peaceful resolution". **Neither mentions mediation, food corridors, grain or the Black Sea.** Indian officials had not confirmed the offer by the evening of November 10.

## The evidence

### 1. What India said

The PMO page for the call, dated November 9, says Zelenskyy briefed Modi on recent developments in the conflict, that Modi spoke of dialogue and diplomacy and of India's readiness to contribute to peace efforts, and that the two leaders discussed ways to deepen bilateral cooperation. The public broadcaster's summary of the statement, put out at 8:21 pm that day, matches it. A word search of both finds no reference to mediation or to shipping.

### 2. What Zelenskyy said

His post, as quoted, opens by saying he had briefed Modi on the latest diplomatic developments and that Kyiv was "actively preparing on every item on the agenda" for the talks in Abu Dhabi. The food corridor sentence follows. It is Zelenskyy's description of the call, and it is **not attributed to Modi** or to any Indian statement.

### 3. Is it plausible?

The claim is not baseless context. In early October, Ukraine's foreign minister, Andrii Sybiha, called India's truce proposal the most comprehensive of those before Kyiv, and reports describe its parts as including uninterrupted grain and energy shipments through Black Sea ports. India has not published that proposal. A general willingness to help on shipping may be what Zelenskyy means. But "mediate" is a specific role, and **India has not said it has taken it on**.

### 4. What is missing

No Ministry of External Affairs statement or briefing on the point was reachable for this check. If Delhi confirms the offer, the rating would need to change. Until then, the only official Indian record of the call is the PMO statement, and it is general.

## Why this claim spread

The call came a day before the United States, Russia and Ukraine opened talks in Abu Dhabi, and Black Sea grain is one of the issues Kyiv wants settled. A line saying that India is ready to mediate is **easy to carry as news**, and it was reported in India the same evening. Readers should treat it as Kyiv's account of the call until Delhi says the same.
