**The government has published 2027's holiday calendar for its offices.** In an order dated July 16, 2026, the Department of Personnel and Training (DoPT) listed **17 gazetted holidays** for central government administrative offices in Delhi and New Delhi. The list also sets the pattern most central offices in the rest of the country follow, and is the first one people look for when planning leave and travel.

## What are the 17 holidays in 2027?

**Republic Day on a Tuesday, Diwali on a Friday and Christmas on a Saturday.** The full list:

## How many holidays fall on weekends?

**Five of the 17.** Independence Day and Milad-un-Nabi both fall on Sunday, August 15; Gandhi Jayanti (October 2), Dussehra (October 9) and Christmas (December 25) are on Saturdays; and Guru Nanak Jayanti (November 14) is on a Sunday. Under the rules, **a holiday that falls on a weekly off is not replaced** by another day.

**Four three-day weekends, and two more with one day's leave.** For offices that work Monday to Friday:

## What about holidays in other states?

**Central offices outside Delhi follow a 14 plus three formula.** They must observe 14 compulsory holidays, including Republic Day, Independence Day, Gandhi Jayanti, Christmas, Diwali and Id-ul-Fitr. They then add **three holidays chosen from a list of 12 optional ones**, which includes occasions such as Holi, Janmashtami, Ram Navami, Maha Shivratri, Onam, Pongal, Rath Yatra and an additional day for Dussehra.

The three are chosen by the Central Government Employees Welfare Coordination Committee in each state capital, and the choice then applies across the state. In Delhi, DoPT makes the choice itself. Where a state observes Naraka Chaturdasi as its Diwali holiday, central offices there may do the same.

## What are restricted holidays?

**Extra days each employee can choose.** Every central government employee may take **two restricted holidays** a year from a separate list of festivals and occasions. The nine optional holidays not picked as a state's three variable holidays are added to that state's restricted list, and state capitals can add local occasions.

## Can the dates change?

**Yes, for festivals that depend on the moon.** The dates for Id-ul-Fitr, Id-ul-Zuha, Muharram and Milad-un-Nabi may be changed if the moon is sighted on a different day, and any change is announced through the Press Information Bureau, television, radio and newspapers.

## Does this list apply to banks and schools?

**No.** This is the list for central government offices. Banks follow holidays declared by the Reserve Bank of India for each state under the Negotiable Instruments Act, schools follow their state or board calendars, and state government offices follow lists notified by each state. RBI's bank holiday calendar for 2027 had not been published at the time of writing.
