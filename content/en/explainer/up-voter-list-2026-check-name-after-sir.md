**Uttar Pradesh has a new electoral roll, and it is very different from the old one.** The Special Intensive Revision (SIR), a house-to-house check of every voter in the state, ran for 166 days from October 27, 2025, to April 10, 2026. When the final roll was published, it listed **13,39,84,792 voters**. Before the exercise began, the roll had about 15.44 crore names. With the state due to vote for a new assembly in early 2027, every voter should check that their name is on it.

## How many names were removed, and how many added?

**About 2.04 crore names came off, and 84 lakh were added back after the draft.** The draft roll published on January 6, 2026, had 12,55,56,025 voters. Over the next three months, officials sent notices from January 14, held hearings from January 31 to March 27, and took applications from people who had been left out. By the final roll, the electorate had grown by **84,28,767**, split almost equally between men and women.

The final roll has 7,30,71,071 men, 6,09,09,525 women and 4,206 third-gender voters. The number of women per 1,000 men on the roll rose from 824 in the draft to 834. First-time voters aged 18 and 19 rose from 3,33,981 to 17,63,360.

## How do I check if my name is on the list?

**Online, in two minutes, on the Election Commission's portal.**

1. Go to **voters.eci.gov.in**, the Commission's voters' service portal.
2. Choose "Search in Electoral Roll".
3. Search by your **EPIC number** (the number on your voter ID card), or by your name, date of birth, state and district.
4. If you are listed, the result shows your assembly constituency, part number and serial number, which you will need on polling day.

You can also search through the Commission's Voter Helpline app, or call the voter helpline on 1950. Your booth-level officer (BLO), who visits homes in your area, can check the printed roll for you.

## My name is missing. What do I do?

**Apply to be added on Form 6, online or through your BLO.** Enrolment stays open, so a voter who was removed during the revision, or who has turned 18, can still get on the roll for 2027. Every application is verified in the field before a name is added. Keep proof of age and address ready.

The formal appeal window against decisions taken during the revision has passed: a first appeal had to be filed with the district magistrate within 15 days of the final roll, and a second appeal with the Chief Electoral Officer within 30 days after that. A fresh Form 6 is now the way back onto the roll.

In early November, the Election Commission told Uttar Pradesh officials to **re-examine rejected Form 6 applications** so that eligible people are not left out, to run enrolment drives in Classes 11 and 12 and in colleges, and to recheck all new entries made since the revision ended.

## Which districts lost the most names?

**Mostly big cities.** Lucknow recorded the largest number of deletions, 9,14,185, followed by Prayagraj, Kanpur Nagar, Agra and Ghaziabad. Many of the same districts also saw large additions after the draft: Prayagraj added 3,29,421 names and Lucknow 2,85,961 between January and April, and the Sahibabad seat in Ghaziabad added the most of any constituency, 82,898.

## Why were so many names removed?

**Because the revision checked every entry against what was found on the ground.** Officials flagged about 1.04 crore electors as "non-mapped", meaning they could not be linked to earlier records, and 2.22 crore cases with logical discrepancies, such as mismatched details. In such a revision, entries of voters found to have died, moved away permanently or been enrolled twice are removed. The state's Chief Electoral Officer, Navdeep Rinwa, said **no name was deleted without due process**, and that a name missing from the final list was the result of either a Form 6 application or a decision by an electoral registration officer after a hearing.

> No voter's name was deleted without due process.
>
> Source: Navdeep Rinwa, Chief Electoral Officer, Uttar Pradesh, April 10, 2026

## Why is the roll a political issue?

**Because the opposition says the revision went wrong.** Samajwadi Party chief Akhilesh Yadav has accused the BJP of trying to game the rolls, and the SP sent 85 of the 107 memoranda parties submitted to the Chief Electoral Officer during the exercise; the BJP sent 10 and the Congress 9. The CEO said every complaint was addressed. Parties also deployed booth-level agents in large numbers: 1,61,581 for the BJP, 1,57,631 for the SP and 1,54,224 for the BSP.

## When will the roll be frozen for the 2027 election?

**Close to polling day.** Under the law, names can be added up to the last date for nominations in each constituency, but it is safest to apply well before the schedule comes out, which is expected after mid-December 2026. Voter information slips will be handed out five days before polling, and booth-level officers must record each one they deliver.
