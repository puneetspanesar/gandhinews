**The next PM-KISAN payment has not been scheduled yet.** The 24th instalment of the Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi was released on **October 30, 2026**, at an event in Bhildi in Gujarat's Banaskantha district, sending ₹2,000 each to about 9.34 crore farmers, roughly ₹18,680 crore in all. The government has not announced when the 25th will come.

## What is PM-KISAN?

**A direct cash transfer of ₹6,000 a year to farmer families.** Launched in February 2019, the scheme pays eligible farmers three instalments of ₹2,000 a year by direct benefit transfer into their bank accounts, to help with seeds, fertiliser, equipment and other farm costs. The money is released centrally, usually at an event addressed by the Prime Minister.

By June 2026, total payments since the scheme began had crossed **₹4.46 lakh crore**; the 24th instalment took the total to about ₹4.65 lakh crore.

## When will the 25th instalment come?

**Probably around February or March 2027, but no date is official.** The last four instalments have come about four months apart:

## How do I check my PM-KISAN status?

**On pmkisan.gov.in, in two places.**

1. **Know Your Status:** enter your registration number to see your eligibility, whether your land records are seeded, whether Aadhaar is linked, your e-KYC status, and your payment history.
2. **Beneficiary list:** under "Farmers Corner", choose your state, district, block and village to see whether your name is on the list.

If something is wrong, the PM-KISAN helplines are 155261 and 1800115526, and the email address is pmkisan-ict@gov.in.

## How do I complete e-KYC?

**Three ways, one of them from home.** e-KYC is mandatory for every registered farmer. You can complete it:

- with a **one-time password** on the PM-KISAN portal, using the mobile number linked to your Aadhaar;
- with **face authentication** on the PM-KISAN mobile app, which needs no OTP or fingerprint device; or
- with **biometric verification** at a Common Service Centre.

## What is the Farmer ID, and do I need one?

**A digital farmer identity being built under AgriStack, now required in several states.** It records a farmer's land, crop and other details so that benefits reach the right person. According to reports in May 2026, it had been made mandatory for PM-KISAN in 14 states: Andhra Pradesh, Assam, Bihar, Chhattisgarh, Gujarat, Karnataka, Kerala, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Odisha, Rajasthan, Tamil Nadu, Telangana and Uttar Pradesh. It is especially required for new farmers. You need your Aadhaar, an Aadhaar-linked mobile number and land documents such as khasra or jamabandi, and you can register on your state's agriculture portal or the AgriStack platform.

## Why do payments get held up?

**Usually because of paperwork, sometimes because of eligibility checks.** The most common reasons are incomplete e-KYC, an Aadhaar number not seeded to the bank account, land records not verified, or a missing Farmer ID where it is required.

There are also eligibility rules. Farmers who acquired land ownership after February 1, 2019, are not eligible. Where more than one member of the same family, such as a husband and wife, or a parent and adult or minor child, has been receiving the benefit, the payments are **held back until physical verification** is complete. The 24th instalment went to about 9.34 crore farmers, around 10 lakh fewer than the 23rd, according to reports on the day.
