**Booking a reserved train ticket in India works differently from a year or two ago.** Since 2025, Indian Railways has shortened the booking window, made Aadhaar verification central to online booking, tightened Tatkal rules and moved the chart earlier. Here is how the system works as of 2026.

## How far ahead can I book?

**60 days, not counting the day of travel.** The advance reservation period opens 60 days before the journey. For a train on May 1, booking opens on March 1. For most trains, booking on the opening day starts at 8 am.

## Do I need Aadhaar to book?

**On the first day, yes, if you book online.** In stages since late 2025, the Railways has reserved the opening day of online booking for users whose IRCTC accounts are authenticated with Aadhaar:

**Since January 12, 2026, only Aadhaar-authenticated users can book general reserved tickets online on the first day** of the 60-day window, up to midnight. The rule applies only to the IRCTC website and app; bookings at railway reservation counters are unaffected. Authorised ticketing agents also face a 10-minute halt on the opening day.

> Aadhaar verification of users is an important step which has been taken to make the system more robust.
>
> Source: Ministry of Railways, January 2026

## What are the Tatkal rules?

**OTP verification, and no agents in the first half hour.** Tatkal tickets, sold the day before the journey at a higher fare, need **Aadhaar-linked OTP verification** since July 15, 2025: the OTP goes to the mobile number linked to the passenger's Aadhaar, whether you book on IRCTC, at a counter or through an agent. Authorised agents cannot book Tatkal tickets in the first 30 minutes of the window.

## When is the reservation chart prepared?

**Much earlier than it used to be.** The chart tells waitlisted passengers whether their ticket is confirmed. It used to be prepared about four hours before departure. In July 2025 the Railways moved it to about eight hours, and the latest timings, notified in January 2026, are:

An earlier chart gives a passenger whose ticket is still waitlisted more time to find another way to travel.

## Did fares go up?

**Slightly, by distance.** Sleeper, ordinary first class and general fares on non-suburban trains went up by one paisa a kilometre, and mail and express fares, AC and non-AC, by two paise a kilometre. Distance-based surcharges range from ₹5 for 216 to 750 km to ₹20 for 1,751 to 2,250 km.

## What should I do before my next booking?

**Authenticate your IRCTC account with Aadhaar, and keep your Aadhaar-linked phone handy.** Without Aadhaar authentication you cannot book online on the opening day, and you cannot get the OTP needed for Tatkal. If you book at a counter, the opening-day rule does not apply, but the Tatkal OTP rule does. Because the Railways has revised these rules several times, check the latest notices on IRCTC before an important journey.
