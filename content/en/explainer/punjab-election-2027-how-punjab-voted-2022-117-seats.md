**Punjab's assembly has 117 seats, and a party needs 59 to govern.** The next election is due before the current House's term ends in March 2027. The Election Commission has not announced dates, but polling is expected in February or March alongside Uttar Pradesh, Uttarakhand, Goa and Manipur.

The Aam Aadmi Party goes in defending its 2022 landslide. Its rivals go in with the 2024 Lok Sabha results, which showed how quickly that lead can shrink.

## How did Punjab vote in 2022?

**AAP won 92 of 117 seats, a landslide.** It took 42.01% of the vote, almost double the Congress's 22.98%. The Congress, which had governed the state for the previous five years, was reduced to 18 seats. The Shiromani Akali Dal, contesting for the first time without the BJP since 1997, won only 3, its worst result, and the BJP won 2. The BSP, the Akali Dal's partner that year, won 1, and an independent won 1.

Turnout was **71.95%**. Bhagwant Mann, AAP's chief ministerial candidate, won Dhuri by 58,206 votes, and was sworn in on March 16, 2022.

## Why do Malwa, Majha and Doaba matter?

**Because Malwa alone holds more than half the seats.** Punjab is usually read as three regions. Malwa, south of the Sutlej, has **69 of the 117 seats** and includes Ludhiana, Patiala, Bathinda and Sangrur. Majha, the border belt around Amritsar and Gurdaspur, has 25. Doaba, the land between the Beas and the Sutlej around Jalandhar and Hoshiarpur, has 23.

In 2022 AAP won **66 of Malwa's 69 seats**, which by itself was more than a majority. Its lead was narrower elsewhere: 16 of 25 in Majha and 10 of 23 in Doaba, where the Congress held on to 9. Any party hoping to win in 2027 has to break AAP's hold on Malwa.

## What changed in the 2024 Lok Sabha election?

**AAP's lead disappeared.** In the 2024 general election the Congress took 26.31% of Punjab's vote and AAP 26.03%, a gap of less than a third of a percentage point. The BJP polled 18.56%, and the Akali Dal 13.41%.

Lok Sabha votes do not translate neatly into assembly seats, and AAP's leaders argue that state elections are fought on state issues. But the numbers matter for one reason above all: **the BJP and Akali Dal together polled 31.97%**, more than either of the two leading parties. That is why talks on reviving their alliance, which broke in 2020, have become the biggest open question of the 2027 campaign.

## What has happened to the Mann government since 2022?

**It has delivered on some promises and lost several ministers.** The government's flagship moves include the "Yudh Nashian Virudh" (war against drugs) campaign launched by Punjab Police on March 1, 2025, and the ₹1,000 monthly allowance for women, which began paying out on July 1, 2026, four years after it was first promised.

It has also been hit by **resignations and arrests**. Health Minister Vijay Singla was sacked over corruption allegations in May 2022, Transport Minister Laljit Singh Bhullar resigned in March 2026, and the Enforcement Directorate arrested minister Sanjeev Arora in a money-laundering case on May 9, 2026. In late April 2026, seven of AAP's Rajya Sabha MPs from the state joined the BJP. In September 2025, floods across all 23 districts led the state to declare itself disaster-hit, and relief is now a campaign issue.

## Who are the main contenders?

**Four parties, and possibly an alliance of two.** AAP will run on Bhagwant Mann's record and is seeking a second term. The Congress, the main opposition party in the assembly with Partap Singh Bajwa as Leader of the Opposition, changed its state chief on October 2, 2026, appointing **Pargat Singh** in place of Amrinder Singh Raja Warring.

The Akali Dal is led by Sukhbir Singh Badal, who announced in December 2025 that he would contest from Gidderbaha, a seat long associated with his family. The BJP has said publicly that it is preparing to contest all 117 seats, while talks with the Akali Dal on a seat-sharing deal have been reported to be in their final stages.

## What issues will decide the vote?

**Drugs, money and floods, in roughly that order.** The government says its anti-drug drive has produced record arrests; the opposition says the drug trade continues. The women's allowance is AAP's main welfare pitch, while its opponents point to Punjab's debt, which reached ₹4.13 lakh crore in 2025-26. Farmers hit by the 2025 floods want compensation and stronger embankments. Corruption cases against ministers give the opposition its sharpest line of attack.

## What does it take to win?

**Malwa, and a vote share big enough to beat a divided field.** In 2022 AAP's 42% produced 92 seats because the rest of the vote was split three ways. If the BJP and Akali Dal fight together, the contest becomes a three-way one between AAP, the Congress and the alliance, and a party needs a larger share to win. If they fight apart, a party could win a majority on a much smaller share, as AAP did in 2022. Either way, the result will turn on whether AAP holds the southern Malwa belt that gave it its majority five years ago.
