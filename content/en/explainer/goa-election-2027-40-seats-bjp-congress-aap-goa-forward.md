**Goa elects a new 40-member assembly in early 2027.** It is the smallest of the five states going to the polls, but its politics are among the most fluid. Its MLAs have changed sides in large numbers after each of the last two elections, the opposition has split into rival camps, and a court fight over seats for Scheduled Tribes could redraw the map before the vote.

## How did Goa vote in 2022?

**The BJP won 20 of 40 seats, one short of a majority.** It polled 33.31%, up slightly on 2017, and gained seven seats, while the Congress fell six seats to 11 with 23.46%. Independents won 3, the Maharashtrawadi Gomantak Party and AAP 2 each, and the Revolutionary Goans Party and Goa Forward Party 1 each. The BJP **kept power with the support of independents**, and Pramod Sawant continued as Chief Minister.

The Revolutionary Goans Party, a new regional outfit, drew 9.81% of the vote, more than AAP or the MGP, though it won only one seat.

## Why do defections matter so much in Goa?

**Because they have repeatedly turned opposition wins into BJP strength.** Ten Congress MLAs moved to the BJP in 2019, and eight more legislators joined the ruling party in 2022, months after the election. In a House of 40, those numbers decide who governs. Opposition parties now cite this record against each other: in October 2026, AAP mocked the Congress with the slogan "Ek Kachori Do Samosa, Is Congress Ka Kya Bharosa" (what trust can you place in this Congress), and the Congress's working president Sunil Kawthankar said the jibe "speaks volumes about the Delhi-based party's intentions".

## Who is allied with whom for 2027?

**The opposition has split into two camps.** On September 13, 2026, AAP's national convenor Arvind Kejriwal announced a **"Goa First" alliance** between AAP and the Goa Forward Party, led by former Deputy Chief Minister Vijai Sardesai, the party's only MLA, who represents Fatorda. Delhi's former Chief Minister Atishi is in charge of AAP's Goa campaign.

> The Goa First alliance is loyal to the people of Goa. We will fight this election and we will win it on our own strength.
>
> Source: Arvind Kejriwal, AAP national convenor, September 13, 2026

The Congress, the main opposition party, had been in talks with Goa Forward and said it wanted a united front against the BJP; its state president Girish Chodankar said on the eve of the announcement that talks were continuing. AAP's Goa chief Valmiki Naik had said in June that AAP was open to an alliance with the Congress and wanted it to lead. Since September, the Congress has run its own campaign, including a 27-day "Goy Rakhon Yatra" that ended with a rally addressed by Rahul Gandhi in Margao on October 18.

The BJP, in power since 2012, will seek a fourth consecutive term under Sawant. AAP, which first contested Goa in 2017 and won nothing, holds the Benaulim and Velim seats it won in 2022.

## What is the fight over tribal seats?

**Whether four seats can be reserved for Scheduled Tribes before the vote.** Goa's main tribal communities, the Gawda, Kunbi and Velip, were added to the Scheduled Tribe list only in 2003, after the 2001 Census on which the current constituency map is based. As a result, **none of Goa's 40 seats is reserved for them**, even though they are about a tenth of the population.

A 2025 Act of Parliament lets the Census Commissioner estimate their share of the population so that the Election Commission can reserve seats, likely four, before 2027. On October 29, 2026, the Bombay High Court at Goa restrained the Commissioner from notifying that estimate while it hears a challenge to the law, and the state government said it would appeal in the Supreme Court. If the stay holds, the 2027 election will be fought on the existing map.

## What is the campaign about so far?

**Change after a long BJP run, and trust.** AAP's Valmiki Naik has framed the contest as a chance to change governance after "15 years of BJP rule in the state", and has proposed a common minimum programme drafted after consulting citizens. The opposition parties also attack each other over the defections that followed the last two elections. The national row over the Election Commission and the roll revision has reached Goa too: at his Margao rally, Rahul Gandhi accused the Prime Minister, the Home Minister and the Chief Election Commissioner of planning "vote theft".

## What will decide the result?

**Whether the anti-BJP vote stays divided.** In 2022 the BJP won half the seats with a third of the votes because the opposition was split between the Congress, AAP, the RGP, the MGP, Goa Forward and the Trinamool Congress. For 2027 the opposition is again divided into at least two camps, and in a 40-seat House every split helps the party with the largest single base.
