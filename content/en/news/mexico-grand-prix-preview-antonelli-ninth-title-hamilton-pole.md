Kimi Antonelli lines up fourth for the Mexico City Grand Prix knowing that **ninth place or better will make him world champion**, whatever Lewis Hamilton or George Russell do ahead of him.

The Mercedes driver arrives with **a 123-point lead over Russell**, his team-mate. The 71-lap race at the Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez starts at 2 pm local time, which is **1:30 am IST on Monday**. A title there would make Antonelli Formula One's youngest champion.


Hamilton starts from his **first pole as a Ferrari driver**, nine thousandths of a second quicker than Russell. Charles Leclerc completes the second row alongside Antonelli, who moved up a place on the grid after Max Verstappen took a **one-place penalty** for colliding with Liam Lawson in Friday's second practice. Verstappen starts fifth with Lando Norris sixth.

Lawson's weekend went from bad to worse. The stewards **excluded him from qualifying** after finding that the electrical power from his car's MGU-K dropped away faster than the rules allow on his fastest Q2 lap. He starts from the back, behind Valtteri Bottas, who carries a 50-place grid penalty.

The order further back was also shuffled, with Pierre Gasly and Nico Hülkenberg moving into the top ten. Oscar Piastri starts a lowly 12th. For Antonelli, though, the sum is simple: **stay out of trouble and finish in the top nine**.
