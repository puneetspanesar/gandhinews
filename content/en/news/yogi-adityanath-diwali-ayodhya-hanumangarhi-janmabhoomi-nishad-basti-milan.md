Chief Minister **Yogi Adityanath will spend Diwali morning in Ayodhya** on Sunday, with prayers at two of the city's main shrines, a festive meeting in a Nishad settlement and a round of calls on saints. The programme, published a day ahead, starts early.

## Prayers and the Nishad basti

He is due to offer **darshan and puja at Hanumangarhi at 7:50 am**, followed by darshan at the Shri Ram Janmabhoomi temple at 8:05 am. At 8:20 am he is scheduled to attend a **Deepawali milan in Nishad basti**, near Ramghat Halt. A visit to Ashok Singhal Ward follows, for which no time has been given.

## Meetings with saints

From 9 am the Chief Minister is to call on saints in turn: Digambar Akhara at 9:00 am, Bada Bhaktmal at 9:10 am, Maniram Das Chhawni at 9:20 am, and a meeting and discussion at Karsevakpuram at 9:30 am.

He is then scheduled to attend a public programme at Ram Ki Paidi from 10:05 to 10:35 am, and to **leave from the Ramkatha Park helipad at 10:40 am**.

## After Deepotsav

The visit is the second day of a two-day stay. On Saturday evening he performed the aarti at the 10th Deepotsav on the Saryu, where more than 30 lakh diyas were lit, with Vice-President C P Radhakrishnan present.

Times are as announced and may shift on the day. This report will be updated once the events have taken place.
