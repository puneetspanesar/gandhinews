Australia go into the second Test against South Africa at St George's Park on Sunday still unsure whether Cameron Green or Josh Inglis will be fit, and with their batting order open to change.

Green missed the series opener in Durban with an abdominal injury, a soft-tissue problem picked up after he was struck by a full toss in an ODI before the Tests. He has been sprinting but not batting. "Cam hasn't started batting yet," coach Andrew McDonald said. "He's got a few things to go through in the next three, four days."

McDonald said Green was "progressing well" but would not confirm his return. Even if he plays, he will not bowl on this tour.

That matters for Cooper Connolly, who missed the first Test with a head cold. His spin bowling gives Australia a bowling option Green cannot provide, and he is under consideration for Gqeberha.

Inglis, the reserve wicketkeeper, injured a finger in last month's ODI series and is wearing a splint. He batted in the nets during a lunch break in Durban, which McDonald said "went well", and he will have bat-against-ball sessions in Gqeberha before a call is made.

## Where Renshaw bats

Matt Renshaw was the standout batter in Durban, batting at No. 5 partly because the selectors liked his game against spin. Whether he opens in Gqeberha is undecided. "We're really open-minded to what that means," McDonald said.

He also backed Nic Maddinson, who struggled in the first innings in Durban before an attacking 48 in the second. "I think Nic Maddinson is a very mature player who will assess the conditions," McDonald said. Beau Webster's place is also under discussion.

Australia lead the three-match series 1-0 after winning by 214 runs. South Africa have already named their side for Gqeberha, dropping Tristan Stubbs and recalling Matthew Breetzke. The third Test is in Cape Town from October 27 to 31.
