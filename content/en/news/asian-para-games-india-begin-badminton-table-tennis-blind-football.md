India's athletes begin competing at the fifth Asian Para Games on Friday, two days before the opening ceremony in Nagoya, with para badminton, para table tennis and blind football starting early in Japan's Aichi prefecture.

The badminton hall is where India's day is busiest. Indian players are down for 26 pool matches, starting at 5:30 am IST, and the first-day line-up includes some of the country's best-known para shuttlers. Suhas Yathiraj plays Bangladesh's J. Dhar in men's SL4 singles at 7:05 am IST, Manasi Joshi faces China's L. Yuemei in women's SL3 at 9:30 am, and Krishna Nagar meets Chinese Taipei's J. Lin in men's SH6 at 12:30 pm.

There is an all-Indian men's doubles SL3-SU5 match in the afternoon, with Nitesh and Ruthick up against Suhas and Abhijeet Sakhuja. In mixed doubles, Manisha and Pramod take on a Chinese pair.

In para table tennis, Bhavina Patel starts her women's Class 4-5 singles group against Kuwait's A. Alrasheedi at 7:10 am, at the same time as Baby Sahana Ravi and Subham Wadhwa begin their campaigns.

The men's blind football team opens against South Korea at 9 am.

Bhavina and Bhagat will carry India's flag at the opening ceremony on October 18. India have sent 168 athletes across 11 sports to the Games, which formally run from October 18 to 24.

> "It is a very proud moment for me to be chosen as India's flag bearer."
>
> Source: Bhavina Patel, para table tennis player, October 7, 2026

Bhagat has six Asian Para Games medals, two of them gold, and Bhavina has a silver and a bronze.
