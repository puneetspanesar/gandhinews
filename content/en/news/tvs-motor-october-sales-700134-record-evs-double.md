TVS Motor Company **sold 7,00,134 vehicles in October**, 29% more than the 5,43,557 it sold in the same month last year, as festive demand at home and a surge in exports lifted every part of its business.

Two-wheelers made up the bulk of the total at **6,70,679 units, up 28%**. Within that, domestic two-wheeler sales rose 22% to 5,14,764, a sharp step up from the 4,21,631 the company sold in Indian showrooms a year ago.

## Electric and export growth

The fastest growth came from electric vehicles. TVS **sold 71,382 electric vehicles**, up 120% from 32,387 last October, making EVs roughly one in ten of everything it sold in the month.

Overseas markets also pulled hard. Two-wheeler exports **rose 51% to 1,55,915 units**, which means more than a fifth of the company's two-wheelers went abroad.

The three-wheeler business, still small next to scooters and motorcycles, **grew 60% to 29,455 units**, from 18,407 a year earlier.

## The wider picture

The numbers add to a strong opening day for October auto sales. Maruti Suzuki, Hyundai and Mahindra all reported record months earlier on Sunday, helped by festive buying.

For TVS, the October total is a big jump on its own earlier peak: a year ago, 5,43,557 units was described by the company as its highest monthly sales. November figures will show whether demand holds once the festive season is over.
