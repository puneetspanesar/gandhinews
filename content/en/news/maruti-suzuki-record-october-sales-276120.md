Maruti Suzuki sold **276,120 vehicles in October**, the highest monthly total in its history, the company said in a stock exchange filing on Sunday. That is 25% more than the 220,894 it sold in October 2025, a month that itself had been a record.

Sales in India, counting passenger cars, utility vehicles, vans and light commercial vehicles, reached an **all-time high of 216,789 units**, up about 20% from 180,675 a year earlier. Passenger vehicles alone came to 212,384, a big jump from the roughly 1.69 lakh the company sold in September.

Small and mid-size passenger cars accounted for 100,877 units, while **utility vehicles like the Brezza** and Grand Vitara added 97,787. The two are now almost level, a sign of how far Indian buyers have moved towards SUVs.

## Exports keep climbing

The sharper rise came from overseas. Maruti **shipped 49,872 vehicles abroad**, about 59% more than the 31,304 of last October. Sales to other carmakers, mainly Toyota, which sells rebadged Maruti models, were 9,459 units.

From April to October, Maruti has sold **16.55 lakh vehicles**, up about 27% from 13 lakh a year earlier. The numbers top a strong day for the industry: Hyundai, Tata Motors and Mahindra also reported their October figures on Sunday, with Hyundai posting a record. The October totals will be the first test for Maruti shares when markets reopen on Monday.
