Six people died during Andhra Pradesh's mandal and zilla parishad elections on Thursday, November 5, 2026: **four voters and two officials on election duty**, according to district accounts gathered on Friday as polling went on in five mandals hit by Cyclone Murasu.

The two officials both collapsed while at work. Haritha Goud, 58, a secondary grade teacher from Rayadurg serving as an assistant polling officer at a booth in Kadadarakunta in Anantapur district, **developed breathing trouble in the afternoon**. She was taken to the primary health centre in Kudair and died during treatment.

Geddada Namashivaya, 61, a head constable at the Palakollu police station in West Godavari district, was on duty at Chandaparru. He **collapsed and died while handing over ballot boxes** at the mandal parishad office after polling closed.

Chief Minister **N Chandrababu Naidu and Deputy Chief Minister Pawan Kalyan** offered condolences over the teacher's death and promised her family the government's full support.

## Voters who died

Two elderly voters died shortly after casting their ballots: Chitta Tulasi Reddy, 63, of Repudi in Phirangipuram mandal of Guntur district, and Khagga Tirupati Rao, 65, of Vatticherukuru. In Aryapeta in Yalamanchili mandal of West Godavari, Eda David, 50, **collapsed near a polling station** and died of a heart attack.

Bhaskar Goud, 43, who works in Sangareddy district of Telangana, had travelled home to Chennampalli in Kurnool district's Tuggali mandal to vote. He was riding a two-wheeler towards Gooty when a car hit an electricity pole, which **fell on him and a friend**. He died on the spot.

## Turnout and Friday's polling

The State Election Commission has put Thursday's turnout at **67.24%, or 1,50,84,014 of 2,24,32,460 voters**, for 7,767 MPTC and 582 ZPTC seats. On Friday, voting for 5 ZPTC and 60 MPTC seats was under way from 7 am to 5 pm in Gopalapuram in East Godavari and in Buttayagudem, Jeelugumilli, Polavaram and Velerupadu in Eluru district, alongside repolls at seven booths. Votes from across the state will be **counted on November 7**.
