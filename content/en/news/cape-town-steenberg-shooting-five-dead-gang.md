Gunmen burst into a home in Cape Town on Saturday night and opened fire on a group of people who were drinking inside, killing five and wounding three in what police believe was a gang attack.

The shooting happened at about 8 pm at a house in Blode Street, near the neighbourhoods of Steenberg and Lavender Hill on the Cape Flats. Police said an unknown number of attackers entered the house and shot eight people, all aged between 21 and 33.

Four men and a woman were declared dead at the scene. Another man and two women were taken to hospital with gunshot wounds.

"The incident is believed to be linked to gang violence," Western Cape police said on Sunday. "Anti-gang unit detectives are hard at work in search of the perpetrators of the act."

No arrests had been made by Sunday morning, and police did not say what might have prompted the attack.

The Cape Flats, a sprawl of working-class townships south-east of the city centre, have for years been at the heart of Cape Town's gang conflicts.

Mass shootings are a recurring feature of crime in South Africa. Earlier this month, a gunman killed at least four people in a shooting after a memorial service in the Johannesburg area.
