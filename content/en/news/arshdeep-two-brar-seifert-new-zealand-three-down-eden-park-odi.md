India's new-ball pair made the first hour count at Eden Park. **Arshdeep Singh took both early wickets** in the powerplay, and Gurnoor Brar then removed the dangerous Tim Seifert, leaving New Zealand three down in the first of five ODIs in Auckland.

Shubman Gill chose to bowl after a rain delay that pushed the toss back by about 20 minutes. Arshdeep struck with the third ball of his third over, when **Will Young was caught by Shreyas Iyer** at square leg with New Zealand on 22. He came back to have **Henry Nicholls caught by Naman Dhir** for 9.

## Seifert falls after a quick start

Seifert had kept New Zealand moving. He was 22 not out when the hosts reached **44 for 2 after nine overs**, with Daryl Mitchell on 4, and he hit Brar for six soon after. Brar had the last word: Seifert picked out **Ruturaj Gaikwad**, giving the young seamer his first wicket. Live listings had Brar on **1 for 19** with Mitchell on 19 and Tom Latham on 3 at the crease.

Anshul Kamboj shared the new ball duties with the two left-armers as India rotated their seamers on a ground with short straight boundaries.

## What comes next

New Zealand still have most of their 50 overs to bat, and Mitchell is the wicket India want most. Gill's side chose to chase because teams batting second have won more often at Eden Park. Wellington hosts the second ODI on November 7.
