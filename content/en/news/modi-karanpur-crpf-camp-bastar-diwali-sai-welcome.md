Prime Minister Narendra Modi is spending Diwali with security personnel in **Bastar, Chhattisgarh**, and the **Karanpur CRPF camp** has been reported as the venue. His Diwali programme with the jawans was under way on Sunday, November 8, 2026, though no official timetable had been released by late morning.

Before leaving, Modi wrote on X in Hindi that he was going to Bastar to celebrate Diwali with the "brave security forces". "Where Naxalites wreaked havoc until yesterday, **development is in bloom today**," he said. In a second post he paid tribute to the sacrifice and dedication of the soldiers.

## Welcome in Raipur and Bastar

Chief Minister Vishnu Deo Sai welcomed the Prime Minister on X and thanked him for his attention to the state. He credited Modi's leadership with taking Bastar from "the darkness of fear and violence" towards "**a new light of peace, trust and development**", and extended Diwali greetings on behalf of the people of Chhattisgarh.

Deputy Chief Minister Vijay Sharma said: "Welcome to the Prime Minister in a Naxal-free Bastar."


## A first in Chhattisgarh

The visit is Modi's **13th consecutive Diwali with security personnel** since he became Prime Minister in 2014, and his first in Chhattisgarh. He spent last year's festival aboard INS Vikrant off Goa and 2024 in Kutch, Gujarat. Jammu and Kashmir has hosted him most often, three times.

The trip was closely held: even some senior state leaders and officials were said to lack details of the programme. Security agencies were on alert and the Bastar administration was preparing the schedule.

Bastar was for decades the centre of the Maoist insurgency. The state government says roads, schools and basic services are now reaching remote villages, and the CRPF has reported 12 consecutive months without an operational fatality.

What the Prime Minister says to the troops at the camp will be added when his address is available.
