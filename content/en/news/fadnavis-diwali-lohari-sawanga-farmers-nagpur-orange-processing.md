Maharashtra Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis spent Diwali on Sunday with farmers in **Lohari Sawanga village in Nagpur district**, staying at the home of Arunabai and Rambhau Balpande. Prime Minister Narendra Modi praised the gesture.

"Today, I had the good fortune to celebrate Diwali with fellow farmers," Fadnavis wrote on X. At a village meeting held after a prayer gathering organised by the Gurudev Seva Mandal in the Gram Panchayat hall, he said: "This has been a year of challenges. The rains remained elusive until the very end."

## What he announced

Fadnavis heard farmers' problems and discussed how to protect the **rabi crop in a drought year**. He said borewells could be dug wherever possible. For the Katol and Narkhed belt, known for oranges and sweet limes, he announced a **major processing facility under the MIHAN project** in Nagpur, meant to ease export difficulties that have hurt farm incomes.

The Chief Minister said the state had put **₹5,000 into the bank accounts of about 1.5 crore farmers** so they could celebrate Diwali, with deposits still going on. He also cited the earlier farm loan waivers. The state treats the drought as a natural calamity, with crop losses and shortages of fodder and drinking water in parts of Maharashtra.

## The Prime Minister's reaction

Modi called the visit "very touching". "It underlines the spirit of compassion with which the NDA Government in Maharashtra is working for the farmers," he said.

Revenue Minister and Nagpur Guardian Minister Chandrashekhar Bawankule, MLAs Charansingh Thakur, Ashish Deshmukh and Rajiv Potdar, former MLA Sudhakar Kohale, District Collector Kumar Ashirwad and Zilla Parishad CEO Vinayak Mahamuni were present.
