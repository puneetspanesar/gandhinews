Indian shares **kept climbing through the morning** on Monday, with the Sensex stretching its gain past 1,000 points as Reliance Industries, metal stocks and returning foreign money powered a broad rally.

The Sensex rose **as much as 1,030 points, or 1.38%, to 75,489.70**, its third straight day of gains, while the Nifty 50 climbed up to 278 points, or 1.20%, to 23,330.70. Both indices had opened higher at 9:15 am and added to the gains in the first hour. The Nifty Midcap 100 and Smallcap 100 each rose about 0.9%.

## Reliance does the heavy lifting

Reliance Industries, **up nearly 7%** after better-than-expected quarterly results, accounted for about 400 points, or roughly a quarter, of the Sensex rise. Delhivery jumped about 9%, and Muthoot Finance recovered about 9% from a 52-week low.

All sectoral indices except pharma traded higher. The **Nifty Metal index led with about 1.5%**, and FMCG, PSU Bank and realty each gained more than 1%. The IT index rose about 0.8%.

The mood was helped by cheaper oil. Brent crude fell about 3% to around $110 a barrel, from a peak near $118 last week, as talks to end the US-Iran conflict continued. Foreign portfolio investors **bought ₹6,300 crore of shares** in the cash market last week after weeks of selling.

## A turn in the market?

"There are clear indications of a turnaround in the Indian market assisted by positive external and domestic cues," said V K Vijayakumar, chief investment strategist at Geojit Investments. "The weakening trend in AI trade is beneficial for India, which is widely regarded as a counter AI play."

Shrikant Chouhan, head of equity research at Kotak Neo, advised buying on dips and booking profits on rallies, with **support for the Nifty at 22,800** and then near 22,600.
