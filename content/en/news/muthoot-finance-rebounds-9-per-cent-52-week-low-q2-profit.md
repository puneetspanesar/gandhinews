Shares of Muthoot Finance **swung sharply on Monday morning**, sliding to a 52-week low soon after the open and then bouncing nearly 9%, as the market digested the gold lender's September-quarter results.

The stock fell to **₹2,305 in early trade** on the National Stock Exchange, against Friday's close of ₹2,409, before climbing to a day's high of ₹2,508, about 8.8% above the low. At 9:55 am it was up 2.6% at ₹2,471. Even after the bounce, the shares were nearly 40% below their 52-week high of ₹4,149.50, reached in January.

## Strong numbers, worried investors

The results, announced over the weekend, were strong. **Standalone net profit rose 27% to ₹2,977 crore** from ₹2,345 crore a year earlier, total income rose 37% to ₹8,842 crore and net interest income grew 34% to ₹5,364 crore. Consolidated gold loan assets under management grew 46% to ₹1.93 lakh crore.

"We are also revising our gold loan growth guidance for FY27 upward by an additional 10%," the company's management said, taking the target to **25% from 15%**. Muthoot added 262 branches in the first half and expects a similar number in the second.

Brokerages were split. Equirus Securities **upgraded the stock to "Add"** from "Reduce" with a target of ₹2,920, citing valuation comfort after a steep fall over six months, though it raised its credit cost estimate and flagged rising delinquencies. Nuvama kept a "Hold" rating and cut its target to ₹2,675 from ₹3,100, naming asset quality as the thing to watch.
