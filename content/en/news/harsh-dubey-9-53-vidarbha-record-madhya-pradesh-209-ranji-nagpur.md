Harsh Dubey wrote his name into Vidarbha's record books in Nagpur on Monday, taking **nine of the ten Madhya Pradesh wickets** as the visitors were bowled out for 209 on the first day of their Ranji Trophy match.

His figures of **9 for 53 are the best innings return** by a Vidarbha bowler in the Ranji Trophy, and no Vidarbha bowler had taken nine wickets in an innings in the competition before. The left-arm spinner bowled 23.1 overs in a long, patient spell and wore the Madhya Pradesh batting down one wicket at a time.

The one wicket that did not go to Dubey came early. **Nachiket Bhute removed Yash Dubey**, the Madhya Pradesh top-order batter, before the spinner took over and claimed every wicket that followed.

Madhya Pradesh never built the partnership they needed against him, and **209 was all they could manage**.

The spell adds to an outstanding first-class record. Dubey took **69 wickets at an average of 16.99** in the 2024-25 season, and he made his India ODI debut in June this year and took four wickets in two matches. He is also on the books of Sunrisers Hyderabad in the IPL.

Vidarbha now have three days to turn his effort into a first-innings lead. The four-day match runs until Thursday.
