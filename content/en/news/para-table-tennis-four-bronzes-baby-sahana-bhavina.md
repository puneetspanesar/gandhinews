India's women para table tennis players came away from Nagoya with four bronze medals on Tuesday, and one of them made history. Baby Sahana Ravi's bronze in the women's singles class 9 was India's first ever medal in the standing category of para table tennis at the Asian Para Games.

All four reached the semi-finals and all four lost there, three of them to Chinese or Korean opponents, without taking a game.

Bhavina Patel, the Tokyo Paralympics silver medallist, was beaten 11-7, 11-5, 11-7 by China's Zhang Bian in the class 4-5 semi-final. Sonalben Patel went down 11-7, 11-5, 11-6 to South Korea's Yoon Jiyu in class 3.

Baby Sahana lost 11-8, 11-2, 11-4 to China's Mao Jingdian, and Prachi Pandey was beaten 11-9, 11-2, 11-8 by Iraq's Najlah Al-Dayyeni in class 6.

In para table tennis, losing semi-finalists both receive bronze, so the four results still added four medals to India's tally on a busy morning that also brought Deepthi Jeevanji's 400m gold and silvers in shooting and powerlifting.
