The rally on Dalal Street **held into the afternoon** on Thursday. In afternoon trade the **Sensex was up 1,644 points at 78,945** and the Nifty 50 up 467 points at 24,428, both about 2% higher.

The gains had come early. By 10 am the Sensex was up 1,552.83 points, or 2.01%, at 78,854.23, and the Nifty 50 had gained 427.80 points, or 1.79%, to 24,388.40.

The Sensex opened 1,329 points higher at 78,630 and kept climbing, on course for a sixth straight day of gains. Buying came from almost every corner. Every Sensex stock except Bharti Airtel traded higher in the opening minutes, with Maruti, Asian Paints, Bajaj Finance and M&M **up 3 to 4%**. Airtel slipped slightly ahead of its September-quarter results, due later on Thursday.


## ₹500 lakh crore and counting

The broad buying lifted the total value of companies listed on the BSE **past ₹500 lakh crore**. It stood at ₹500.82 lakh crore shortly before 10 am, up about ₹6.4 lakh crore from ₹494.42 lakh crore at Wednesday's close.

Bank Nifty rose 1.71% to 56,867.80. Cement, financial services and realty stocks led the sector gains, while pharma and healthcare were the only sectors in the red. The Nifty Midcap 100 and Smallcap 100 indices rose more than 1% each.

## Results move single stocks

Quarterly numbers drove some of the biggest single moves. Lenskart Solutions **jumped 10.6% to ₹762.75**, a 52-week high, after its quarterly profit attributable to shareholders more than doubled, rising 122.1% to ₹227.1 crore. Nykaa's parent FSN E-Commerce went the other way, falling 3.3% to ₹343.70 even though its quarterly profit tripled to ₹101 crore.


The mood was set overnight on Wall Street, where the Dow rose 3.03% after the Democrats' win in the US midterm elections raised hopes of a quicker end to the West Asia conflict and pushed crude lower. Titan and Bharti Airtel were among the stocks traders watched through the day.
