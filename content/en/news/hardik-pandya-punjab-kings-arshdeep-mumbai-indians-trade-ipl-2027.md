Mumbai Indians and Punjab Kings on Wednesday, November 11, 2026, officially confirmed a player trade that sends **Hardik Pandya to Punjab Kings and Arshdeep Singh to Mumbai Indians** ahead of IPL 2027. The swap ends Pandya's second spell at Mumbai, which began in 2024 when he took over the captaincy from Rohit Sharma, and brings the left-arm fast bowler Arshdeep to Mumbai after seven years in Punjab colours.

Neither franchise's statement gives financial terms. Reports on the deal put **Pandya's new fee at ₹18 crore, up from ₹16.35 crore**, and Arshdeep's at the ₹18 crore Punjab paid for him at the 2025 mega auction. Those figures are not confirmed by the teams.

## What the franchises said

Mumbai's announcement thanked Pandya for his contribution and recalled that he joined the franchise in 2015 and won four IPL titles with it. Akash Ambani said: "I have great respect for him, both as a cricketer and as a person." Of Arshdeep he said: "We welcome him along with the MI Paltan as he takes the field in Blue and Gold." Mumbai described the bowler as one of the world's leading fast bowlers and said he would add strength and versatility to an attack that already has Jasprit Bumrah.

On the captaincy, Mumbai said only that they **will evaluate their options and announce a decision "at the appropriate time"**. Neither franchise has said who will lead the side in 2027.

Punjab's chief executive Satish Menon said: "We are delighted to welcome Hardik to the Punjab Kings family." He said the franchise wants a team that can compete at the highest level and bring the trophy home to Punjab. Of Arshdeep he said: "We thank him for everything and wish him continued success."

## Two players, two records

Punjab's statement puts Pandya's IPL record at **162 matches, 2,955 runs and 82 wickets** since his debut in 2015. He played for Mumbai until 2021, moved to Gujarat Titans for 2022 and won the title in the franchise's debut season, then led them to the 2023 final. He returned to Mumbai before the 2024 season as captain.

Mumbai's results under him were mixed. They finished last in 2024, reached Qualifier 2 in 2025 and were ninth in 2026, missing the playoffs in two of his three seasons. Reports say Pandya missed matches in the last season with back spasms, a leg strain and a shin injury.

Arshdeep made his IPL debut for Punjab in 2019 and has played **96 matches for the franchise, taking 111 wickets**. Punjab signed him at the 2025 mega auction for ₹18 crore.

## How the deal came together

Reports on November 3 spoke of advanced talks between the two franchises, and by November 6 neither had confirmed anything. Reports then said both teams had submitted paperwork to the BCCI with the players' consent. The confirmation on November 11 came six days before the retention deadline.

## What happens next

The retention deadline, which the BCCI extended by two days, is **November 17, 2026**, and the IPL 2027 mini-auction is on **December 16, 2026, in Goa**. Both franchises still have to announce who they keep and release. Mumbai must also settle their captaincy, and Punjab have not said what role Pandya will have in their leadership group. Until the teams or the league say more, the question of who leads each side in 2027 stays open.
