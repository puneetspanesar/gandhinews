Bihar **beat Railways by four wickets with 10 balls to spare** in the Syed Mushtaq Ali Trophy at Indore on Monday, November 16, chasing 138 and finishing on 141 for 6 in 18.2 overs. It was Bihar's first win of the tournament, two days after they lost their opening match to Saurashtra, and it came without their captain: Vaibhav Sooryavanshi, 15, did not play.

## Bihar's bowlers set it up

Railways, in Elite Group B with Bihar, made **137 for 9 in their 20 overs**. Jayant Lakhan top-scored with 47 off 44 balls, and Zubair Ali (26 off 18) and Ravi Singh (21 off 11) gave the innings its late push, but the rest of the order added little and the total stayed short of what a T20 side expects on a good surface.

The wickets were shared. **Prashant Singh and Suraj Kashyap took three each**, both for 28 runs in four overs. Mohammad Saif was the most economical of the bowlers, taking two for 17 in his four, and Toufik Uddin Mondal had one for seven in a single over. Kashyap, who also captained the side, was named **player of the match** for his spell.

## Loharuka's 29-ball fifty

Bihar's chase was built around AA Loharuka, who hit **58 off 29 balls**, the only half-century of the match. Tarun Kumar Singh made 20 off 18 and R Pratap Singh 18 off 23, but it was Loharuka's burst at the top that took the asking rate out of the game. Bihar lost six wickets on the way, which kept Railways in it for a while, and finished with 10 balls in hand.

## Why Sooryavanshi was not there

Sooryavanshi captained Bihar on his debut in the role on Saturday, November 14, and scored 18 off 12 balls in a five-wicket defeat by Saurashtra, which we [reported](/en/news/sooryavanshi-bihar-lose-saurashtra-youngest-captain-smat-day-one). Before the tournament, Bihar had said he would lead the side for its first two matches, as our [squad report](/en/news/smat-2026-squads-captains-sooryavanshi-bihar-surya-mumbai-november-14) noted.

According to reports, the reason he missed the Railways game is that he has been **picked in the India A squad for the Asia Cup Rising Stars**, which starts on November 19. Kashyap led Bihar in his place. The reports do not say whether Sooryavanshi will return to the Bihar side after that tournament.

## The rest of Monday's results

Elsewhere in the competition, **Vidarbha beat Odisha by eight wickets** at Kolkata, reaching 162 for 2 in 17.4 overs after Odisha made 161 for 8. At Mohali, **Himachal Pradesh beat Andhra by eight runs**, making 158 for 9 and then holding Andhra to 150 for 8. At Mumbai, Rajasthan made 166 and bowled Jharkhand out for 111 in 17.5 overs, a **55-run win**.

In the Plate group at Dimapur, Nagaland made 205 for 5 and beat Manipur by 80 runs, and Sikkim chased 70 for the loss of one wicket in 8.2 overs after bowling Arunachal Pradesh out for 69.

## What the result means

Each Elite team plays seven league matches in 13 days, from November 14 to November 26, so one defeat is not fatal but a second can be. Bihar are now level on a win and a loss. Their group also includes Delhi and Tamil Nadu, whose squads we described before the tournament, which means the next few matches carry more weight than this one.

For Railways, the problem was the batting: 137 was the best the side could do after nine wickets, and only one batter reached 30.

## What happens next

The league stage ends on November 26, the Super League begins on November 30 and the final is on December 6. Bihar's next fixture and the confirmation of Sooryavanshi's availability had not been announced in the reports read for this story.
