India's Under-19 bowlers made a tight start to their Asia Cup campaign in Al Amerat on Friday, with **Nepal 27 for 2 after 8.2 overs** at about 11:10 am IST. Captain Lakshya Raichandani won the toss and **chose to bowl first** in the 50-over Group A match, which began at 10:30 am IST.

## Sood does the early damage

**Ishan Sood took both wickets** to fall. He had Prasiddha Jaishi out with the score on 3 in the third over, and then had Suryanshu Koirala caught by Arjun Rajput for 5, leaving Nepal 18 for 2 in the fifth over. Sood's first three overs cost **only six runs**.

Kavya Patel backed him up with two maidens in his first two overs. Niraj Kumar Yadav kept Nepal going with 15 off 26 balls, including three fours, while Nischal Chhetri was yet to score after 10 balls.

Sood came into the tournament in form, having taken **13 wickets in eight games** in the Vinoo Mankad Trophy.

## What comes next

India will chase whatever Nepal set later on Friday, with Aaryavir Sehwag and Anvay Dravid among the batters in the squad. Raichandani's side then face **defending champions Pakistan on Sunday**, November 8, a rematch of last year's final, which Pakistan won by 191 runs. India close the group against the UAE on November 10.

The top two teams from each group go through to the semi-finals on November 12, and the final is on November 14.
