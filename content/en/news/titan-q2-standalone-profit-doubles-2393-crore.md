Titan Company on Thursday reported that its **standalone net profit more than doubled** in the July-September quarter, rising 113.66% to ₹2,393 crore from ₹1,120 crore a year earlier.

The Tata group company, which sells jewellery under the Tanishq brand along with watches and eyewear, released the results after the market closed. Its standalone revenue rose **10.09% to ₹20,614 crore**, from ₹18,725 crore in the same quarter last year.

The gap between the two growth rates stands out. **Profit grew more than ten times as fast as revenue**, and the first set of figures did not set out what drove the jump, whether stronger margins on jewellery or a one-time gain. Segment-wise numbers for jewellery, watches and eyewear were not part of the headline results.

The September quarter included the run-up to the festive season, when jewellery buying usually picks up, though Diwali itself fell in the following quarter.

Titan's shares had **gained 1.53% to ₹4,827.20** in morning trade ahead of the numbers. They will react to the results when trading opens on Friday.

Titan was among a crowded slate of companies reporting on Thursday. ONGC, Glenmark Pharma and Bharti Airtel also announced September quarter results during the afternoon and evening.
