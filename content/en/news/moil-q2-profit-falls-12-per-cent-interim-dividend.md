MOIL, the state-run manganese ore miner, reported a **12.3% fall in consolidated net profit** for the July to September quarter on Monday, to ₹61.77 crore from ₹70.44 crore a year earlier.

The drop came even as the company brought in more money. **Revenue from operations rose 10.9%** to ₹369.13 crore from ₹332.74 crore, but costs climbed faster. Total expenses went up to ₹307.89 crore from ₹273.27 crore, and profit before tax slipped to ₹77.13 crore from ₹89.76 crore.

Earnings per share came in at ₹3.04, against ₹3.46 in the same quarter last year.

## Dividend and a GIFT City arm

Despite the weaker quarter, the board declared an **interim dividend of ₹2.20 a share** for 2026-27, or 22% of the ₹10 face value. Shareholders on the register on November 6 will receive it.

The board also approved setting up **a wholly owned subsidiary at GIFT City** in Gujarat, to be called MOIL International IFSC Limited.

The numbers came on a busy results day. **Petronet LNG and Marico** reported earlier in the afternoon, and Container Corporation of India and ITC were among those due to follow in the evening.
