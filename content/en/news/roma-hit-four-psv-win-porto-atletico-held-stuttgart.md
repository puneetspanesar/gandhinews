Roma were the most comfortable winners on Tuesday's Champions League night away from the headline games, beating Slovan Bratislava 4-0 at home.

Mario Hermoso opened the scoring in the 22nd minute and Nahuel Molina added a second in the 39th. Niccolo Pisilli made it three eight minutes after half-time and Donyell Malen converted a penalty to finish the job.

In Portugal, PSV Eindhoven took all three points from Porto. Guus Til scored the only goal of a tight game.

Atletico Madrid had to settle for a point in Germany. Jamie Leweling put Stuttgart ahead around the 39th minute, and Julian Alvarez equalised a minute after the restart to make it 1-1.

## Napoli outlast Bodo/Glimt

Napoli and Bodo/Glimt shared a lively game in Naples, which the Italian side won. Jens Petter Hauge scored for the Norwegians in the 27th minute to make it 1-1, Scott McTominay put Napoli back in front before half-time, and after Bodo/Glimt levelled again through Helmersen, McTominay struck a second in the 75th minute. Napoli scored again to make sure of the win.

## The early games

Borussia Dortmund won 2-1 at Sabah in Azerbaijan, with Serhou Guirassy scoring his 23rd goal in his 25th start of the season.

In Istanbul, Fenerbahce made a flying start against Slavia Prague, with Oguz Aydin and Mason Greenwood scoring in the first 11 minutes. Slavia fought back, and the game finished 2-2.

The rest of matchday three is played on Wednesday night, India time, headed by Bayern Munich against Arsenal.
