India go into the fourth one-day international against New Zealand at Bay Oval, Mount Maunganui, on Friday, November 13, with a **2-0 lead in the five-match series** and a new captain. The match starts at 3 pm local time, 7:30 am IST, according to reports. One more India win would take the series with a game to spare.

Shreyas Iyer takes over from Shubman Gill for the last two ODIs, as the selectors planned when they named the squads on October 16. Ishan Kishan is his deputy. Gill, KL Rahul and fast bowler Anshul Kamboj were picked only for the first three games and have **left for Wellington to join the Test camp**. Batting coach Sitanshu Kotak, bowling coach Morne Morkel and other staff have gone with them, while head coach Gautam Gambhir stays for the one-dayers.

## An India side with new faces

Tilak Varma, wicketkeeper Kumar Kushagra and fast bowler Yash Thakur have been added to the ODI group. Rohit Sharma and Virat Kohli stay on, and the rest of the squad includes Ruturaj Gaikwad, Axar Patel, Naman Dhir, Arshdeep Singh, Gurnoor Brar, Auqib Nabi and Mohammed Siraj. One preview expects Kishan to open with Rohit, Gaikwad to take the place Rahul would have had, and **Tilak to come in at number five**. Those are guesses: India have not named their eleven, and the toss will settle it.

Siraj has not bowled on this tour after missing the opener while on India A duty, and Nitish Kumar Reddy, who played the first ODI, has been called to the Test camp. Kotak has said Nabi, Gurnoor and Arshdeep may stay back to help the Test bowlers, "Gurnoor, maybe Nabi" in his words on November 9, so the bowling line-up on Friday is the least certain part of the side.

## New Zealand short of players too

The home side is thinner still. Tom Latham, Daryl Mitchell, Henry Nicholls, Glenn Phillips and Nathan Smith have left for their own Test camp, **fast bowler Lockie Ferguson is out of the series** with a calf injury, and Blair Tickner is serving a seven-day stand-down after being struck on the helmet by a Kamboj bouncer. Kristian Clarke has been added as cover. Matt Henry has been cleared of concussion and Kyle Jamieson has been kept back from the Test group, which gives Mitchell Santner's side some pace.

A predicted New Zealand side has Finn Allen, Will Young, Tim Seifert, Nick Kelly, Mitch Hay, Michael Bracewell and Dean Foxcroft around Santner, with Jamieson, Henry and Jacob Duffy to bowl.


## The pitch and the weather

Bay Oval is described in previews as the **flattest of the three grounds** used so far in the series, and the forecast is better than it was in Wellington or Hamilton. The second ODI was decided by four runs on DLS and the first by five wickets on DLS, both in rain-hit games, and the third was washed out. Friday offers the first realistic chance of a full 50 overs a side.

## What is at stake

New Zealand must win both remaining games to level the series at 2-2. For India, reports say a series win would be **their first in New Zealand in seven years**. The result also decides how much the batters and bowlers heading to Wellington can rest: the two-Test series begins on November 19, and the squad that stays at Bay Oval is a mix of white-ball regulars, fringe players and the two senior batters in Rohit and Kohli.

Former India players Ambati Rayudu and Murali Kartik have pushed for Kishan and Gaikwad to come straight into the eleven, with Rayudu wanting Kishan to open alongside Rohit. One preview argues that India should also use the match to look at **Siraj and Kuldeep Yadav**, who have sat out so far.


## What happens next

The fourth ODI starts at 7:30 am IST on Friday, November 13, 2026. The fifth and last is at the same ground on Sunday, November 15, 2026, and the Test series follows from November 19, 2026.
