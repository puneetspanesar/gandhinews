North Carolina pushed the **top-ranked team in the country to the final minute**, but Miami escaped Kenan Stadium with a 38-35 win on Saturday night to stay unbeaten.

The game swung back and forth all evening. Darian Mensah opened the scoring with a 2-yard pass to Malachi Toney at the end of a 13-play drive, and the Tar Heels answered twice through Jelani Thurman. A late Jake Weinberg field goal gave Miami a 17-14 lead at half-time, and North Carolina went ahead 21-17 on Billy Edwards Jr.'s first pass of the second half.

Demon June's 1-yard run put the home side 28-24 up with 11:46 left. Mark Fletcher Jr. replied with a 2-yard run on fourth-and-1, his **third touchdown of the night**, a score that also tied the school's career rushing touchdown record. Then Marquise Lightfoot **stripped Edwards of the ball**, Justin Scott recovered, and Mensah found Toney from 9 yards with 2:33 to play for a 38-28 lead.

Edwards was not finished. He completed 28 of 34 passes for 341 yards and four touchdowns, the last a 15-yard throw to Trech Kekahuna with 1:38 left, but Miami ran out the clock.

Mensah finished **22 of 29 for 274 yards** and three touchdowns. The win ended Miami's five-game losing run against North Carolina and its wait for a victory in Chapel Hill dating back to 2017.
