**Eight fishermen from Rameswaram** who spent a month in a Sri Lankan prison were back in Tamil Nadu on Friday, November 6, 2026, after flying home from Colombo overnight.

The men were part of a crew of nine who set out from Rameswaram on a mechanised boat on October 3. Around midnight, a Sri Lankan Navy patrol stopped them while they were fishing **near Neduntheevu, also called Delft Island**, and arrested them for fishing in Sri Lankan waters. The navy also seized the boat, its nets and the catch.

The nine were produced before a local court and sent to **prison in Jaffna**. The central and Tamil Nadu governments then took up their case with Sri Lankan officials.

On October 30 a Sri Lankan court **ordered eight of the nine released**. The boat's driver, who had been caught by the Sri Lankan Navy once before, was given a three-month prison sentence after this second arrest.

The eight were handed to Indian diplomats in Sri Lanka and stayed at a camp run by the Indian mission while officials issued them **emergency travel certificates**. They left Colombo late on Thursday night on a passenger flight and landed in Chennai early on Friday.

Officials of the Tamil Nadu fisheries department received them at the airport, and the state government arranged a vehicle to **take them home to Rameswaram**.

The arrests of Tamil Nadu fishermen in the narrow Palk Strait remain a regular friction point between the two countries, with boats from Rameswaram and the delta districts often stopped on the Sri Lankan side of the maritime boundary. The return comes in a hard week for the state's fishing families, after Cyclone Murasu left fishermen from Chennai's Kasimedu dead or missing off the Andhra coast.
