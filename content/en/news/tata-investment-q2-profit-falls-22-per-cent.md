Tata Investment Corporation, the Tata group's listed investment company, on Monday evening reported a **profit fall of about 22%** for the September quarter, as income from its holdings shrank.

Consolidated profit came to ₹115.92 crore, against ₹148.16 crore a year earlier. Consolidated revenue **fell about 21% to ₹122.30 crore**, from ₹153.98 crore. Earnings per share dropped to ₹2.29 from ₹2.93.

The parent company on its own fared better. Standalone profit **slipped only about 3% to ₹117.99 crore**, from ₹121.88 crore, on total income of ₹146.94 crore. Standalone earnings per share were ₹2.33, against ₹2.41.

For the first half of the financial year, the company earned a standalone profit of ₹281.88 crore and a consolidated profit of ₹259.43 crore. Its standalone **total assets stood at ₹34,575.99 crore** on September 30.

Tata Investment is registered with the Reserve Bank of India as a **middle-layer non-banking finance company**, which places it under closer regulatory watch than smaller lenders and investment firms.
