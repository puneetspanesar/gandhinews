Union minister Jitendra Singh said on Friday that Mission Karmayogi, the government's civil service training programme, marks a basic shift in governance "from a rule-based system to a role-based approach".

He was speaking in Lucknow at the third regional workshop of Sadhana Shrinkhla 2026, organised by the Capacity Building Commission with the Uttar Pradesh government. Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath attended. The two-day meeting, which ends on Saturday, brings together eight states and four Union Territories from the northern region to discuss turning government training institutes into modern, skills-based centres.

## Numbers from Uttar Pradesh

Singh, minister of state for personnel, praised the state's record. More than 19 lakh officials and employees in Uttar Pradesh have registered on the iGOT Karmayogi online platform, out of about 1.7 crore nationally, he said. He noted that courses on yoga and on artificial intelligence were both popular with learners in the state.

He said training institutions had to move beyond routine courses and become centres of excellence, and that success should be judged by results on the ground, not by the number of programmes run or officials trained.

## Reforms since 2014

The minister listed changes made since 2014, including ending the need for gazetted officers to attest documents and dropping interviews for some government jobs. He said annual complaints on the CPGRAMS grievance portal had grown from about two lakh in 2014 to about 25 lakh, with 95 to 97% settled within set time limits, which he read as a sign of growing public trust.

Also present were S Radha Chauhan, who chairs the Capacity Building Commission, and Uttar Pradesh Chief Secretary Shashi Prakash Goyal.
