Delhi Police have registered FIRs over the INDIA bloc's march to the Supreme Court on Monday, and Lok Sabha Leader of the Opposition Rahul Gandhi is among those named, police sources said on Tuesday.

The first reports, on Tuesday morning, put the number at two: one at Tilak Marg police station and one at Barakhamba Road. Later in the morning police sources gave a figure of four, with two at Tilak Marg and one each at Mandir Marg and Barakhamba. No police statement has been issued and the full list of those named has not been made public.

## What police allege

The cases are for alleged violation of prohibitory orders under Section 163 of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS), which lets authorities restrict gatherings where public order may be threatened. Police say the march had no permission.

Police also allege that protesters broke barricades on the way to the court, using a mini-truck to push through, and painted graffiti on roads in central Delhi. The Tilak Marg FIR relates to protesters who entered the Supreme Court premises and names INDIA bloc leaders, including Gandhi.

## The march

The "March for Democracy" set off from the Constitution Club on Monday afternoon and moved through Connaught Place and Mandi House before police halted it at a barricade on Bhagwan Dass Road. Gandhi, Priyanka Gandhi Vadra, Akhilesh Yadav and Tejashwi Yadav climbed on top of a vehicle to address supporters. The Congress put the turnout at 2.5 lakh.

The bloc wants Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar to resign over the Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of electoral rolls. That evening its lawyers filed a writ petition in the Supreme Court in the names of Gandhi and 10 other leaders, seeking a stay on the SIR.

## What the two sides say

Gandhi did not back down after the march. "We needed to show the government that the people of India are not going to be intimidated," he said on Monday. "You cannot beat us, threaten us, frighten us."

Congress president Mallikarjun Kharge wrote on X: "The fight against vote theft will continue. INDIA's battle will go on." No response from the Congress to the FIRs had been issued by late Tuesday morning.

The BJP has called the march political theatre. "This is baseless... They are lying. The Supreme Court has already ruled on the constitutionality of SIR," said national spokesperson Guru Prakash Paswan. Union minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan said: "To gain power, the Congress sometimes rely on the Left, sometimes on cockroaches," a reference to the Cockroach Janta Party.

## What comes next

Gandhi has invited opposition MPs, civil society groups and student organisations to his residence at 4 pm on Tuesday, with a press conference planned beforehand. The Supreme Court is due to hear petitions over last weekend's protest detentions the same day.
