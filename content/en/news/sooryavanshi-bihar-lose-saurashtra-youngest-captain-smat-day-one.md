A teenage captain's first match ended in defeat on the opening day of the Syed Mushtaq Ali Trophy. **Saurashtra beat Bihar by five wickets** at the Holkar Stadium in Indore on Saturday, November 14, chasing 138 in **16.3 overs with 21 balls to spare**.

Vaibhav Sooryavanshi, who is **15 years and 232 days old**, opened the batting and scored 18 off 12 balls with two fours and a six in his first match in charge of a senior side. Left-arm seamer Chetan Sakariya had him out in the fifth over. According to reports, Sooryavanshi is now the youngest captain in men's T20 cricket, going past Croatia's Zach Vukusic.

## How the match went

Bihar chose to bat and finished on **137 for 9** in their 20 overs. **Prithvi Raj top-scored with 38 off 27 balls**, hitting four sixes, and Altamish Ashraf added 24 off 20. Saurashtra's bowlers shared the wickets around.

Sakariya, the Player of the Match, took **3 for 33**, and Prerak Mankad took 3 for 20 in his four overs. Captain Jaydev Unadkat and Krains Fuletra took a wicket each.

Saurashtra's reply was quick. **Harvansh Pangalia hit 33 off 11 balls** to take the chase away from Bihar early, and Harvik Desai added 21. Ruchit Ahir and Sidhant Rana were both unbeaten on 21 when the winning runs came, with more than three overs unused.

## What else happened on day one

The most runs came at Eden Gardens in Kolkata, where **Gujarat made 250 for 9** and beat Odisha by 21 runs. Khilan Patel hit 84 off 29 balls and Urvil Patel 62 off 30. Odisha chased hard and reached 229 for 8, with **Subhranshu Senapati scoring 87** and Kartik Biswal 55, but Arzan Nagwaswalla took 4 for 50 for Gujarat. The two sides scored **479 runs** between them.

Defending champions Jharkhand started with a six-wicket win over Mizoram. Mizoram were bowled out for 76, with Harsh Raj taking 3 for 16, and Utkarsh Singh's unbeaten 45 off 26 balls got Jharkhand home in 8.3 overs.

Andhra beat Tripura by 43 runs. Andhra's 141 for 9 owed most to an unbeaten 50 off 33 balls from SNDV Prasad, and Tripura were bowled out for 98 in 17.2 overs, with Cheepurapalli Stephen and KV Sasikanth taking three wickets each.

Reports also say Mumbai, led by Suryakumar Yadav, beat Hyderabad by seven wickets in Kolkata. The details of that match were not available when this was written.

## How we got here

The tournament opened with 38 teams, 32 of them in four Elite groups of eight, and Bihar were put in Elite Group B with Delhi, Haryana, Jammu and Kashmir, Punjab, Railways, Saurashtra and Tamil Nadu. As we reported when the squads were named, the Bihar Cricket Association gave Sooryavanshi the captaincy for the first two matches, and Suraj Kashyap is his deputy. [Our preview](/en/news/smat-2026-squads-captains-sooryavanshi-bihar-surya-mumbai-november-14) has the squads and the tournament structure.

Sooryavanshi arrived in Indore with a large reputation: he scored **175 off 80 balls in the Under-19 World Cup final** and was the leading run-getter of IPL 2026 with 776 runs. He has also played T20Is for India in New Zealand, as [we covered earlier](/en/news/india-new-zealand-second-t20i-sooryavanshi-kishan-preview). On Saturday, a quick 18 was not enough.

## Why it matters

Each Elite team plays **seven league matches in 13 days**, so a defeat in the first game puts pressure on the rest of the group stage. Bihar's group still has Delhi, Haryana, Jammu and Kashmir, Punjab, Railways and Tamil Nadu to come, and the squad named for the first two matches is led by a 15-year-old. The captaincy was handed to him for only two matches, so the next game is his second and last in the role for now, as the squad announcement stands.

For the selectors, the tournament is a short window to see form. Players such as Mohammed Shami for Bengal and Ishant Sharma and Harshit Rana for Delhi, named in the preview, are using it to stay in view.

## What happens next

According to reports, Bihar play **Railways on Monday, November 16**, in what is the second match of Sooryavanshi's stint as captain. The league stage ends on **November 26**, the Super League runs from November 30 to December 4 and the final is on **December 6**.
