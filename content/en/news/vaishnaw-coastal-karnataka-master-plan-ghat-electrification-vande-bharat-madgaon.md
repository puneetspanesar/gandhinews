Railway Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw announced in Mangaluru on Friday, November 13, 2026, that the Centre will prepare **an integrated master plan for coastal Karnataka**, and said work is under way to double the Mangaluru-Bengaluru rail line as part of it. He spoke hours after Prime Minister Narendra Modi dedicated the electrified Ghat section to the nation and flagged off the new Bengaluru-Mangaluru-Madgaon Vande Bharat Express.

Vaishnaw described the region as one with "great economic potential" and called the electrification of the Ghat section a feat completed "despite all odds". The master plan, he said, would be built around better rail connectivity. No cost, timeline or agency for the plan was announced.

## What the Prime Minister dedicated

The Press Information Bureau's release for the Karnataka visit, issued on November 11, lists the railway items. The **55 km Sakleshpur-Subrahmanya Road Ghat section** was dedicated, and the release says "the entire Bengaluru-Mangaluru rail route is now fully electrified".  The Ghat stretch runs through the Western Ghats between Sakleshpur and Subrahmanya Road.

Four redeveloped Amrit Bharat stations were also inaugurated: Chamarajanagar, Chikkamagaluru, Sakleshpur and Subrahmanya Road. The Prime Minister dedicated the **45 km Bagalkot-Wandal doubling section**, part of the 284 km Gadag-Hotgi doubling project, which also supports coal supply to the NTPC plant at Kudgi.

## The new Vande Bharat

The Yesvantpur-Madgaon Vande Bharat Express links Bengaluru, Mangaluru and Margao. According to South Western Railway, two inaugural special services ran on November 13 and **regular operations begin on November 15**. According to reports, the trains are numbered 26655 from Yesvantpur and 26656 from Madgaon, run six days a week (26655 skips Wednesdays, 26656 skips Thursdays) and take about 13 hours 20 minutes.

The stops are Shravanabelagola, Hassan, Sakleshpur, Subrahmanya Road, Kabaka Puttur, Mangaluru Junction, Udupi, Kundapura, Byndoor, Murdeshwar, Gokarna Road and Karwar, before the train reaches Madgaon. The service puts Subrahmanya, Udupi, Murdeshwar, Gokarna and Karwar on one Vande Bharat chair car line from Bengaluru. Departure times and fares were not in the material available at the time of writing.

## Why it matters

With the Ghat section electrified, the whole Bengaluru-Mangaluru route is now under overhead wires, which is what allows a Vande Bharat to run end to end. Doubling the Mangaluru-Bengaluru line, which Vaishnaw says is being worked on, would be the larger change, since it would add track capacity on the route.

Until a document, a budget head or a Railway Board sanction appears, the master plan remains an announcement, and approvals and costs will be the first things to watch.

## What next

Regular Vande Bharat service starts on Sunday, November 15. The Prime Minister's visit continues in Gujarat on November 14. For the master plan, the next marker would be a formal Railway Ministry or Railway Board statement on scope and funding.
