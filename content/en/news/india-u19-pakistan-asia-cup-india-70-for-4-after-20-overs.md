India U19 were **70 for 4 after 20 overs** against Pakistan U19 in the Asia Cup Group A match at Al Amerat, Oman, on Sunday, with Pakistan yet to bat. India chose to bat after winning the toss.

The innings began badly. Captain **Lakshya Raichandani was out for 0** in the first over, and VK Vineeth followed at 21 for 2 in the ninth over. Yashbardhan Singh Chauhan went at 22 for 3 in the 12th, and the stand-out opener of the Nepal game, Aaryavir Sehwag, fell at 42 for 4 in the 17th over for 22 off 40 balls, caught by Mohammad Sayyam off Niqab Shafiq.

Pakistan's Ali Raza has taken **two wickets** and Shafiq one. Arjun Rajput and Anvay Dravid were at the crease, Rajput having hit four fours and a six on his way to a score of about 30, and the pair had added 24 off 18 balls at the point of the latest scorecard.

## Handshake skipped at the toss

Raichandani did not shake hands with Pakistan captain Farhan Yousuf at the toss. The two exchanged no greetings, in line with India's no-handshake stance against Pakistan.

## What is at stake

Both teams started the tournament with wins, India beating Nepal by 10 wickets and Pakistan beating the UAE by nine wickets, so each has two points. **Only the top two in the group** reach the semi-finals, which makes the result important for the group. India's last group match is against the UAE on November 10.

This report will be updated when the innings ends. The final result was not available at the time of writing.
