Switzerland's Naima Karamoko and Simona Waltert won the **Chennai Open doubles title on Sunday**, beating Vaishnavi Adkar and Rutuja Bhosale in straight sets in the final at the SDAT Tennis Stadium, with the **second set going 6-0**. The match was scheduled for 4 pm IST, and the result was in by the evening.

Match reports differ on the first set, giving it as 6-2 in one and 6-3 in another. Both agree that the Indians did not win a game in the second.

## How the final went

The Swiss pair settled first. Waltert's baseline play, especially off the backhand, and Karamoko's work at the net gave them an early break and a **4-0 lead** in the opening set. The Indians broke back in the fifth game, but the Swiss closed the set out.

The second set was one-sided. Bhosale lost her serve early after a double fault and a run of errors, and Adkar dropped hers at a second break point. Karamoko was particularly effective at the net, and the Indians struggled to get past her. They also made many unforced errors and could not convert the key points in the closer games.

## A first title for Karamoko

For Karamoko it is a **first WTA title** and her first WTA final. The 29-year-old from Geneva is Switzerland's top doubles player at No. 99 and no longer holds a singles ranking; her career-high in singles was 730th, in August 2024. She has been named in Switzerland's Billie Jean King Cup team, which plays in Thailand in about two weeks, her first time at the event.

Waltert, 25, from Graubünden, won her second WTA doubles title. The first came in Lausanne in 2021 with Susan Bandecchi. At No. 133 in the individual rankings she was the highest-ranked of the four finalists.

## How both pairs got here

The Swiss pair won their semi-final on Saturday, November 7, **6-4, 6-4** against Isabelle Haverlag and Panna Udvardy, without dropping a set in that match. Our report on the [Indian pair's semi-final](/en/news/adkar-bhosale-first-all-indian-pair-wta-tour-doubles-final-chennai-open) records that Adkar and Bhosale beat second seeds Weronika Falkowska and Alana Smith **6-2, 2-6, 10-8** in a super tie-break, with Adkar needing a medical timeout for her strapped left thigh during the second set.

## What the week meant for Indian doubles

Adkar and Bhosale remain the **first all-Indian pair to reach a WTA Tour-level doubles final**, a milestone for Indian women's doubles. The earlier near-misses were pairings with a foreign partner on one side: Shikha Uberoi and her sister Neha reached finals in 2005, but Neha played for the United States.

Below the main tour, Ankita Raina and Karman Thandi won a WTA 125 doubles title in 2018, and Prarthna Thombare has reached three WTA 125 finals with the Dutch player Arianne Hartono. A WTA 250 final, played at home, is a step above those results.

The tournament was held at the SDAT Tennis Stadium from November 2 to 8. The singles title was to be decided between top seed and defending champion Janice Tjen of Indonesia and third seed Magda Linette of Poland, and that result was not available at the time of writing.

## What happens next

Switzerland's Billie Jean King Cup team, with Karamoko in the squad, plays in Thailand in about two weeks. No date has been announced for the next tournament for Adkar and Bhosale in the sources available.
