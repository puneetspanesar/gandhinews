**India won the toss and chose to bowl** in a rain-delayed second ODI at Wellington on Saturday, and the new ball did the early damage. New Zealand were **29 for 3 after 10 overs** at about 9:30 am IST, in a game reduced to 47 overs a side.

The toss was held late after a continuous downpour and a wet outfield forced inspections. Skipper Shubman Gill sent New Zealand in, with India leading the five-match series 1-0 after their rain-affected win at Auckland.

## The early wickets

Arshdeep Singh set the tone with an opening spell of **4 overs, 1 maiden, 4 runs**, giving the openers nothing to hit. **Auqib Nabi then struck twice.** Tim Seifert fell for a duck in the second over, caught by Anshul Kamboj off Nabi. Will Young made 6 off 17 balls before KL Rahul took the catch behind the stumps off Nabi in the sixth over.

Kamboj added the third, bowling Henry Nicholls for 11 off 26 balls in the ninth over. India also let one go: Gill was reported to have dropped a chance offered by Daryl Mitchell, who stayed in.

## The teams

India: Rohit Sharma, Shubman Gill (captain), Virat Kohli, Shreyas Iyer, KL Rahul (wicketkeeper), Axar Patel, Naman Dhir, Anshul Kamboj, Auqib Nabi, Gurnoor Brar and Arshdeep Singh.

New Zealand, led by Mitchell Santner, are without Devon Conway, who was ruled out of the rest of the series with concussion. Their side: Will Young, Tim Seifert, Henry Nicholls, Daryl Mitchell, Tom Latham (wicketkeeper), Glenn Phillips, Santner, Nathan Smith, Kyle Jamieson, Jacob Duffy and Blair Tickner.

## What next

New Zealand need a partnership through the middle overs to reach a competitive total, with Mitchell, Latham and Phillips still to come. This story will be updated as the innings goes on. All scores here are as at about 9:30 am IST on November 7, 2026.
