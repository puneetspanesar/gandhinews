Union Law and Justice Minister Arjun Ram Meghwal **celebrated Diwali with Border Security Force personnel in Bikaner**, Rajasthan, on Sunday, the day of the festival.

He spent the morning with the soldiers, exchanged festive greetings with the jawans and praised their **vigilance, dedication and service** in guarding the borders during the festive season.

"It is a pleasure to celebrate Diwali with our soldiers, who are family to us," Meghwal said.

He added that the jawans are away from their loved ones, and that celebrating together makes it feel like **one big family**. "My wishes to everyone," he said.

## Diwali with the forces

Meghwal's visit is one of several that ministers and chief ministers have planned with the armed and border forces this Diwali. Defence Minister Rajnath Singh visited the Bagdogra Air Force Station on Saturday, the eve of the festival, and met Air Warriors, praising the Air Force's vigilance, capabilities and readiness.

West Bengal Chief Minister Suvendu Adhikari is due to visit a BSF border outpost at Hingalganj in North 24 Parganas on Sunday, with the state's Director General of Police, Siddh Nath Gupta. He has said he wants to extend "Diwali greetings to those who have left everything behind to guard our borders."

The reports do not name the unit or post Meghwal visited in Bikaner, or say how many personnel were present.
