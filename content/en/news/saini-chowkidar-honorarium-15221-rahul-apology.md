Haryana Chief Minister Nayab Singh Saini on Friday raised the monthly honorarium of the state's rural chowkidars to **₹15,221 from ₹11,000**, a rise of 38 per cent, and turned the same stage into an attack on Congress leader Rahul Gandhi.

The announcement came at the state-level **Rural Chowkidar Samman Samaroh** in Panchkula, held to honour the village watchmen. The raise covers 5,201 chowkidars across the state. For the first time, they will also be brought under **Employees' State Insurance and labour welfare** schemes, and their uniform, bicycle and other allowances will go up.

The government said the decisions take **effect immediately** and will add roughly ₹27 crore a year to the state's spending.

Saini then brought back the **"chowkidar chor hai" slogan** that Gandhi used against Prime Minister Narendra Modi, and demanded that he apologise publicly for what he called an insult to the men who guard villages. Chowkidars protect villages and public property, he said, often working through the night in hard conditions, and the slogan showed disrespect for them.

The Chief Minister also questioned whether Gandhi had the standing to make charges of corruption, pointing to the **big scandals of the Congress years** in power at the Centre.

For the watchmen, the gain is immediate: the higher honorarium and the new insurance cover apply from now on.
