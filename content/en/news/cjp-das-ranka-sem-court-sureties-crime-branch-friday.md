Cockroach Janta Party (CJP) leaders Saurav Das and Ashutosh Ranka were due back before a Special Executive Magistrate's court in Delhi at 3 pm on Friday, two days after they walked out of Tihar Jail, so that the court could verify the papers of the people who stood surety for them.

SEM Ritu Raj ordered their release on 21 October after they furnished personal and surety bonds of ₹50,000 each, valid until the inquiry ends, and gave written undertakings to keep the peace and not call protests at Jantar Mantar while it remains de-notified as a protest site. The court then directed the two and their sureties to return on Friday, and the investigating officer was asked to check the sureties.

Das's bond was furnished by a professor from Ghaziabad who runs an NGO. Ranka's wife, who is originally from Varanasi and lives in Bengaluru, travelled to Delhi to stand surety for him.

## The Crime Branch case

Their release has not ended the police case. Delhi Police say a group of 14 entered Jantar Mantar through private premises on 13 October, a day after the site was struck off the list of protest venues. Two cases alleging trespass, obstruction of public servants and breach of public order have been transferred to the Crime Branch.

The Crime Branch served the two fresh notices on Wednesday, soon after they secured bail, asking them to join the investigation at its Rajouri Garden office on Thursday. Das, Ranka and the party's Ajinkya Shinde did not go and sought more time. A CJP leader said the police were expected to issue fresh notices.

Das and Ranka were first sent to judicial custody on 14 October for five days, and the custody was extended by two days on 19 October.
