Indian shares **pushed past a milestone on Friday afternoon**. At 2 pm the Sensex was up 1,065.64 points, or 1.34%, at 80,375.65, well clear of the 80,000 mark it had been chasing all morning. The Nifty 50 was up 265.20 points, or 1.08%, at 24,801.80.

The rally gathered pace after lunch. At 1 pm the Sensex had been up 646.73 points at 79,956.74; **in the next hour it added about 419 points more**, its strongest stretch of the day, after a flat opening at 79,371.

## Banks in front

Banks did most of the lifting. **Yes Bank, ICICI Bank and HDFC Bank led the Nifty Bank index** around 2:10 pm, with most bank shares in the green. ICICI Bank had already touched a record earlier in the day. IT companies were also higher, with the Nifty IT index up about 1%.

Not everything rose. Property shares lagged the market, and **Anant Raj, Godrej Properties and Sobha** were the biggest losers in the Nifty Realty index by 1:20 pm.

## Nerves under the surface

The gains came with a warning sign. **The India VIX, which tracks expected swings in the Nifty, jumped 10.53% to 17.22** by 1:40 pm, a sign that traders were paying more to protect themselves even as prices climbed.

That unease comes from abroad. Overnight the Nasdaq Composite fell 5.52%, its biggest one-day drop in 19 months, in a sell-off of chip and artificial intelligence stocks, and Japan's Nikkei 225 and South Korea's Kospi both lost more than 5% on Friday. V K Vijayakumar, chief investment strategist at Geojit Investments, has argued that the tech rout in the US could end up helping India, as foreign investors keep buying Indian shares.

Trading closes at 3:30 pm.
