Hero MotoCorp, India's largest two-wheeler maker, **dispatched 7,34,403 vehicles in October**, 15.5% more than the 6,35,808 it sent out a year earlier, according to figures the company filed with the stock exchanges on Sunday evening.

Almost all of the growth came from the home market. **Domestic dispatches rose 16.6% to 7,05,084**, from 6,04,829 in October 2025, helped by festive demand for scooters and 100cc commuter motorcycles.

## Scooters lead the gains

The sharpest jump was in scooters. Hero shipped **1,04,603 scooters, up 60.8%** from 65,055 a year ago. Motorcycles, still the core of the business, grew a steadier 10.4% to 6,29,800.

Overseas business went the other way. **Exports fell 5.4% to 29,319 units**, from 30,979 a year earlier, and now make up only about 4% of the company's volume.

Hero's electric brand Vida dispatched 30,143 units, up 4.7% from September. The company said Vida's share of the electric two-wheeler market **rose to 13.6% from 11.1%**, and that its registrations on the government's Vahan portal grew 80% on a year earlier.

The dispatch figure is not the same as what buyers took home. Vahan data show **5,89,665 Hero vehicles registered** in October, about 1.45 lakh fewer than the company sent to dealers. October was also softer than September, when Hero dispatched 7,66,348 units, a fall of 4.2% month on month.

For the financial year so far, April to October, Hero has dispatched 42,79,878 vehicles, **up 15.9% on the same period last year**.
