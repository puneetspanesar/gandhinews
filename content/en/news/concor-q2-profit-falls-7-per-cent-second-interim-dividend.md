Container Corporation of India (CONCOR), the state-run operator of container trains and inland depots, reported a **6.8% fall in net profit** for the July to September quarter on Monday evening.

Profit came in at ₹353.10 crore, down from ₹378.70 crore in the same quarter last year. **Revenue barely moved**, rising 1.02% to ₹2,378.57 crore from ₹2,354.53 crore.

## A second payout this year

The board declared a **second interim dividend of ₹1.90 a share** on a face value of ₹5, which adds up to ₹144.71 crore. Shareholders on the register on **11 November** will be paid on or after 20 November.

CONCOR's numbers capped a long results day. Earlier, **Marico reported a 31% jump in profit** and Petronet LNG a 54% rise, while UPL and Escorts Kubota posted lower earnings. Thomas Cook (India) and ITC were among the companies reporting later in the evening.

Two consumer-facing companies **swung back into profit** during the day. Restaurant operator Devyani International reported a net profit of ₹3.46 crore against a loss of ₹23.94 crore a year earlier, and payments firm Mobikwik earned ₹11.83 crore after a loss of ₹28.61 crore.
