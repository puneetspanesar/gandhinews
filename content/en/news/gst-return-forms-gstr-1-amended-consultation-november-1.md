Revised versions of the monthly GST return forms GSTR-1, GSTR-1A and IFF will be put up for public comment on the MyGov portal from November 1, and will come into force on April 1, 2027, a senior tax official said on Thursday.

Gaurav Singh, Commissioner of the GST Policy Wing at the Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC), told reporters that comments will be taken until November 30. The changes are meant to make the sales return line up better with GSTR-3B, the monthly form on which businesses pay their tax.

The GST Council approved the enhancements at its 57th meeting on October 8. It also cleared an alternative way for businesses to amend their tax liability and input tax credit (ITC) in returns, which the government expects will reduce mismatches and the notices and intimations they trigger. Singh said notifications and circulars on the Council's decisions, already vetted by the law department, are due to be issued on Friday, October 23.

## Audits next

Singh said the department would also work on streamlining GST audits. A business that operates in several states now faces separate audits by each state's tax officers.

"With regard to audit, we need to have a look," he said, adding that the process should involve "no harassment" of taxpayers.

He said further GST reforms would gather pace once integrity problems in the input tax credit chain are resolved.
