The Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports has issued revised guidelines for its main funding scheme for national sports federations, with a total outlay of ₹7,386.74 crore for the five years from 2026-27 to 2030-31.

The Scheme of Assistance to National Sports Federations, which the government has approved for the 16th Finance Commission cycle, pays for much of what federations do, from athletes' boarding, lodging, kit and insurance to sports science support. The new guidelines widen what it can be used for.

## Money for big events

Federations bidding to host international competitions can now receive up to ₹50 crore per event. The amount will depend on the importance of the event and the number of countries and athletes taking part, and proposals will be assessed by a committee headed by the Secretary (Sports).

The same ceiling of ₹50 crore applies to the National Games, covering sports equipment, timing, scoring and results services, technical operations and the fees of technical officials.

## Training at home

The ministry also plans Olympic Training Centres, each dedicated to a single sport, with sports science equipment and specialist staff. The aim is to reduce elite athletes' reliance on training overseas. Athletes at these centres would get support for coaches and technical staff, specialised equipment and sports science services.

## Beyond India's borders

Several new strands look outward. The scheme will help indigenous sports seek demonstration-event status at major international competitions, set up federations abroad, and support international federations of indigenous sports that are based in India. It will also fund scholarships and subsidised access to Indian facilities for athletes and coaches from Global South partner countries, and events, conferences and research run by groups such as BRICS and BIMSTEC.

International and continental sports bodies recognised in India can be funded case by case, through federations or the national Olympic committee, on the recommendation of the Secretary-led committee.
