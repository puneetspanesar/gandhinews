Real Madrid pulled away from RB Leipzig late on to win 4-1 at the Santiago Bernabeu on Wednesday night, scoring three times in the last eight minutes of their third Champions League league-phase game.

Bernardo Silva gave Jose Mourinho's side the lead two minutes before half-time, and Arda Guler tapped in after a Kylian Mbappe header seven minutes after the break. Leipzig, coached by Martin Demichelis, had their chances: Thibaut Courtois kept out a volley from Romulo and a first-half effort from Christopher Nkunku.

The game opened up in the closing stages. Mbappe made it 3-0 in the 83rd minute from a pass by substitute Yan Diomande, and Tidiam Gomis pulled one back on the counter two minutes later as Courtois came off his line. Diomande then volleyed in a fourth from Brahim Diaz's pass in the 87th minute. Mbappe and Jude Bellingham were taken off just before the end.

## Around the grounds

Aston Villa kept up their perfect start, beating Viking 3-1 for nine points from three matches. Inter edged Shakhtar Donetsk 1-0 in Milan, with Augusto scoring the only goal.

Galatasaray won 4-2 away at Lille, and LASK beat Sporting 3-2 in Lisbon. Club Brugge beat Lens 3-0 and Real Betis edged Feyenoord 1-0.

Elsewhere, Arsenal came from behind to draw 2-2 at Bayern Munich, and Manchester United were beaten 4-1 by Como in Italy.
