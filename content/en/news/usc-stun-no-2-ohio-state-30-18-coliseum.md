USC beat **second-ranked Ohio State 30-18** at the Los Angeles Memorial Coliseum on Saturday night, a result that knocks a hole in the Buckeyes' season.

The Trojans struck almost at once. Inside the first minute, **Jayden Maiava found Trent Mosley for a 69-yard touchdown**, and Caden Chittenden's extra point made it 7-0 with 14:19 still on the first-quarter clock. Connor Hawkins answered with a 39-yard field goal for Ohio State, but USC were 14-3 up after one quarter and **never trailed**.

The hosts led 20-9 at half-time and stretched the gap with a third-quarter touchdown while holding Ohio State scoreless. The Buckeyes found nine points in the fourth quarter, too late to matter.

## How the numbers fell

Maiava was efficient rather than spectacular, completing **15 of 22 passes for 222 yards** with a touchdown and no interceptions. Mosley caught five balls for 101 yards, and King Miller ran for 78 yards and a touchdown on 13 carries.

Ohio State moved the ball but could not finish drives. Quarterback Luke Fahey completed 21 of 38 passes for 260 yards and a touchdown, Bo Jackson ran for 121 yards on 21 carries, and **Jeremiah Smith caught 10 passes** for 83 yards and the Buckeyes' only touchdown.

The win came at a big moment for coach Lincoln Riley, whose side had lost twice before Saturday. **USC are now 7-2**, while Ohio State, unbeaten in the Big Ten coming into the game, fall to 6-2.
