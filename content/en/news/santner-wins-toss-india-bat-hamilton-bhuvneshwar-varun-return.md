Mitchell Santner had lost the toss in all four matches of the series, and he said he expected the same again. Instead, New Zealand's captain **won the toss at Seddon Park** on Sunday and chose to bowl first in the fifth and final T20I, sending India in to bat.

"I just assumed I'd lose the toss," Santner said. "We'll have a bowl. Obviously a new pitch. Might be a little bit of dew later." He made **one change to the side that won by 87 runs** at Eden Park on Friday, with Blair Tickner coming in for Matt Fisher.

India, who already lead the series 3-1, made two changes. **Bhuvneshwar Kumar, dropped for Friday's game**, and spinner Varun Chakravarthy replaced Mayank Yadav and Axar Patel. Vaibhav Sooryavanshi again stayed on the bench.

Shreyas Iyer said India would have batted anyway. "We were looking to bat, wanted to test ourselves batting first," he said, before giving Phillips his due for Friday's century. "Credit where it's due, Phillips played an incredible knock."

He also had warm words for the returning Bhuvneshwar. "He's been waiting for his opportunity and even in the huddle he **gave a beautiful speech today**," Iyer said.

## The teams

**New Zealand:** Tim Seifert (wk), Finn Allen, Daryl Mitchell, Glenn Phillips, Bevon Jacobs, Michael Bracewell, Mitchell Santner (capt), Nathan Smith, Adam Milne, Jacob Duffy, Blair Tickner.

**India:** Sanju Samson, Abhishek Sharma, Ishan Kishan (wk), Shreyas Iyer (capt), Tilak Varma, Nitish Kumar Reddy, Naman Dhir, Bhuvneshwar Kumar, Arshdeep Singh, Prince Yadav, Varun Chakravarthy.

Play began at 12:30 pm IST. The five-match ODI series between the two sides **starts in Auckland on 4 November**.
