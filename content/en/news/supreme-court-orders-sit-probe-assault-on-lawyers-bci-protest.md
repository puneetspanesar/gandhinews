The Supreme Court on Monday told the Delhi Police to form a special investigation team to look into an alleged assault on lawyers who had been protesting against Bar Council of India (BCI) chairman Manan Kumar Mishra.

The bench of Chief Justice Surya Kant, Justice Joymalya Bagchi and Justice V. Mohana directed the Delhi Police Commissioner to make sure the lawyers' complaint is properly investigated by an SIT headed by a fairly senior officer of Deputy Commissioner of Police (DCP) rank. The lawyers may meanwhile approach the jurisdictional Metropolitan Magistrate.

## A plea for a CBI probe

The lawyers say they were attacked at the BCI's premises during their protest. They complained to the station house officer at IP Estate on August 22. Appearing for them, advocate Prashant Bhushan said **no FIR** had been registered two months later. He alleged the attackers came at Mishra's insistence and argued that a CBI probe or an independent SIT was needed, since the Delhi Police is under the government's control and Mishra is a ruling-party MP.

The Delhi High Court had earlier turned down their request for a CBI probe, saying they could file a complaint before a magistrate.

## The bench's view

Justice Bagchi noted that Mishra was not present when the alleged assault took place. "It seems you are more interested in prosecuting the Chairman rather than the assailants," he said. When Bhushan pressed for the CBI, the judge said the same concern about government control could apply to that agency too. Bhushan replied that the CBI director is chosen by a panel that includes the Prime Minister, the Leader of the Opposition and the Chief Justice of India.

On CCTV footage, the judge assured the lawyers they would see it. "The Court will give you access, your clients have a right to fair [investigation]," Justice Bagchi said. "Let investigation go on." The Chief Justice said a magistrate could identify the attackers before taking cognisance.
