Andhra Pradesh police have **arrested YSRCP MLC Lella Appi Reddy** in Guntur after a late-night confrontation over a ballot box, hours after polling closed in Thursday's mandal and zilla parishad elections.

Nallapadu police registered a case against the opposition leader for **obstructing election staff in their duties** and for a protest that followed. He was held overnight and was moved to Nallapadu police station at around 2 am on Friday, November 6, 2026.

## What happened at Nallapadu

The trouble began as election staff were moving sealed ballot boxes to a strong room after voting ended at 5 pm. According to the case, YSRCP leaders took a ballot box from polling staff at Vengalayapalem. The party gives a different account: its workers say an official took a box out of a vehicle and set it aside at Nallapadu Centre, and they stopped him because they suspected it was being **taken away to be swapped**.

YSRCP leaders and workers then **blocked the road at Nallapadu Centre for about two hours**, demanding that the box stay in their sight. Appi Reddy, a senior party leader in Guntur, reached the spot and joined the sit-in. Police were reported to have used their lathis to clear the protest and taken the disputed box away.

The YSRCP has called the arrest illegal. Police had not said by Friday morning when he would be produced in court; he was reported to face non-bailable charges.

## A tense count ahead

The arrest comes a day before **votes are counted on November 7**, and as the YSRCP, led by Y S Jagan Mohan Reddy, accuses the ruling alliance of capturing booths and blocking its candidates. Polling in five cyclone-hit mandals and repolls at seven booths are under way on Friday.
