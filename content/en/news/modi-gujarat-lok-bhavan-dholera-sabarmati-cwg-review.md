Prime Minister Narendra Modi **reviewed three major Gujarat projects** on Saturday, November 14, before leaving the state for Delhi, after spending the night at Lok Bhavan in Gandhinagar. According to reports, he met Chief Minister Bhupendra Patel and Deputy Chief Minister Harsh Sanghavi and took stock of the **Dholera Special Investment Region**, the **Sabarmati Ashram redevelopment** in Ahmedabad and preparations for the **2030 Commonwealth Games**.

The meeting closed a two-day tour that began in Karnataka on Friday. Modi reached Ahmedabad airport late on Friday evening, was received by Governor Acharya Devvrat and went to Lok Bhavan. He left Gujarat late on Saturday afternoon, the reports say; none gives a clock time for the meeting or the departure.

## What was reviewed

The reports describe the review in outline: which projects came up, not what was decided. No statement from Modi has been published, and no release from the Prime Minister's Office or the Gujarat government on the meeting had been found when this report was written. Here is what the reports say about each project.

**Dholera.** The Special Investment Region in Ahmedabad district is a planned industrial city aimed at advanced manufacturing and semiconductors. According to reports, **Tata Electronics is building a semiconductor fabrication plant** there, the Ahmedabad-Dholera Expressway is already open to traffic, and the state is developing airport and rail links.

**Sabarmati Ashram.** The redevelopment of the Gandhi-linked precinct is a joint Centre and state project, estimated in reports at **₹1,200 crore** over 55 acres. It includes restoring 28 heritage buildings, adding museums and visitor facilities and keeping the five-acre core of the original ashram. At a February 13 meeting of the Mahatma Gandhi Sabarmati Ashram Memorial Trust's Governing Council in Gandhinagar, chaired by Patel, officials said **22 of the 28 houses had been restored**, about 98,000 trees and plants had been planted, and the work was due to be completed by March 2026; the reports on Saturday's review do not say whether it was. The foundation stone was laid on March 12, 2024.

**Commonwealth Games 2030.** Gujarat has allocated **₹301 crore** for preparations, including ₹200 crore for the host fee, according to reports, and has set up the Amdavad 2030 Commonwealth Games Organisation to handle planning and management. It has begun consultations with Commonwealth Sport, the Indian Olympic Association and international sports federations. Ahmedabad's official bid had been submitted to the Commonwealth Games Federation president on August 29, 2025, Sanghavi said at the time, and the 2030 edition marks the Centenary Games.

## The Ambaji visit that did not happen

The announced itinerary had Modi offering prayers at the Ambaji temple in Banaskantha district on Saturday, for Labh Pancham. Late on Thursday the Gujarat government cancelled it. Assembly Speaker Shankar Chaudhary said Modi **did not want to inconvenience** the nearly 5 lakh devotees expected at the shrine that day. Sanghavi said Modi wanted the state government to make sure pilgrims faced no difficulty, and that he is expected to visit on another occasion. Ambaji is counted among the 51 Shakti Peeths and draws pilgrims from Gujarat and neighbouring states; the Diwali holiday period adds to the crowds.

## Dinner on Friday night

On Friday night Modi attended a dinner marking the Gujarati New Year at Lok Bhavan with Patel, Sanghavi, Cabinet ministers and Ministers of State. Senior state BJP office-bearers were also expected, according to reports, and **nothing has been said about what was discussed**. Governor Devvrat described Modi's leadership as a "source of inspiration" and said Gujarat is becoming the country's growth engine under his guidance.

## How we got here

The Prime Minister's Office announced the tour on November 11. On Friday in Karnataka Modi opened the Pink Line stretch of the Bengaluru Metro and launched projects worth **over ₹15,600 crore**, as set out in our [report on the package](/en/news/modi-karnataka-gujarat-visit-today-bengaluru-mysuru-lead), and took part in programmes in Mysuru. Gujarat's leadership has been busy with the same themes: Union Home Minister Amit Shah held a [security review in Ahmedabad](/en/news/amit-shah-ahmedabad-gujarat-security-review-drug-free-india-2029), and Sanghavi has been courting investors, as in our [report from Seoul](/en/news/harsh-sanghavi-seoul-korean-firms-gujarat-investment).

## Why it matters

Each of the three projects touches a different set of Gujaratis. Dholera is the state's bet on **semiconductor and advanced-manufacturing jobs**. The Sabarmati Ashram work reshapes one of the country's most visited Gandhi sites. The Games bring a ₹301 crore state outlay for preparations, with more to follow as venues and transport are planned. A Prime Minister-level review signals that the Centre wants the timelines kept, though the reports give no revised dates or figures from Saturday's meeting.

## What happens next

Modi's next announced engagement abroad is the ASEAN-India summit in Manila from November 16 to 18, covered in our [report](/en/news/modi-asean-india-summit-manila-november-16-18). A new date for the Ambaji prayers has not been announced.
