Results filed for the September quarter **moved three mid-sized manufacturers sharply** on Friday afternoon, in opposite directions, while the wider market rallied.

**Prism Johnson, the cement and tiles maker, rose 5.43% to ₹108.42** on the NSE around 1:30 pm. Its net profit for July to September climbed to ₹45 crore from ₹3.4 crore a year earlier. The jump came with a catch: profit was **down 52% from ₹94 crore** in the April to June quarter, so the year-on-year gain hides a weaker quarter than the one before.

Ratnamani Metals & Tubes, which makes steel pipes for oil, gas and power projects, went the other way. Its shares **fell 7.09% to ₹2,280** by 1:50 pm after the company's operating margin narrowed to 14% in the quarter, from 16.7% in the April to June period. For a pipe maker, a slimmer margin means rising costs are eating into what it earns on each order.

The sharpest fall came at **Precision Wires India, which slid 8.3% to a day's low of ₹387.25** at about 1:11 pm. The copper winding-wire maker had reported a 21.82% rise in net profit to ₹43.38 crore, from ₹35.61 crore a year ago, but investors sold the shares regardless.

The moves stood out on a day when the Sensex was **up more than 1,000 points by 2 pm**, led by banks.
