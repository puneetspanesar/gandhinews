Tata Motors sold **47,652 trucks, buses and vans in October**, 27% more than the 37,530 it sold a year earlier, as demand picked up at home and shipments abroad soared, the company said in a stock exchange filing on Sunday.

The standout number was exports. Tata sent **7,868 commercial vehicles overseas**, more than three times the 2,422 of October 2025, a rise of 225%.

At home, sales grew 13% to 39,784 units, and every segment was up. Heavy trucks rose **12% to 12,030 units**, intermediate and light trucks 17% to 7,213, and passenger carriers such as buses and vans 20% to 3,826. Small cargo vehicles and pickups, the largest group, rose 11% to 16,715.

Medium, heavy and intermediate commercial vehicles together sold 19,263 units in India, **up 16%**, and 20,712 including exports. Truck sales are closely watched as a signal of freight demand, road building and the wider economy, and the October numbers point to a busy festive month for goods movement. Tata's passenger vehicle arm, now a separate listed company, reported its own October figures earlier on Sunday.
