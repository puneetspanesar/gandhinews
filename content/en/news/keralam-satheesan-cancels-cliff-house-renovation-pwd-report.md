Keralam Chief Minister V D Satheesan on Monday ordered the **cancellation of repairs worth over ₹1 crore** at Cliff House, his official residence in Thiruvananthapuram, saying the work had been approved behind his back.

The Public Works Department (PWD) had issued the order. In a statement, the Chief Minister's office said it was "issued in contravention" of his instructions, and that Satheesan has asked the **PWD Secretary for a report** on why the order went out without informing the Chief Minister or his office.

## A standing instruction

According to the statement, Satheesan had told officials soon after the Congress-led United Democratic Front came to power not to carry out **unnecessary renovation or construction** at the bungalow. The UDF government has been in office since 18 May.

The department has a different account of how the order came about. It said the repairs followed a **safety audit carried out in 2022**, when the previous Left Democratic Front government was in power.

Satheesan's instructions now set the terms for any future work. No repairs are to be based on the previous government's estimates, and only work that is genuinely necessary is to be done.

The statement did not say when the order was issued, whether any work had begun, or what the audit had flagged. The PWD Secretary's report is expected to answer the first of those questions, and **who signed off on the work**.
