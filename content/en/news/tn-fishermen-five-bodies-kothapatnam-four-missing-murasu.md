**Five of the nine Chennai fishermen** who went missing as Cyclone Murasu swept the Bay of Bengal have been found dead on the Andhra Pradesh coast. Their bodies washed ashore at Kothapatnam beach in Prakasam district and were taken to the Government General Hospital in Ongole for post-mortem. Four men are still missing.

The Coast Guard, marine police and local fishermen are **searching for the remaining four**. Prakasam Collector P Raja Babu, who went to Kothapatnam to oversee the work, said the teams were "conducting a search-and-rescue operation for the remaining people" and trying to "bring the capsized boat to shore as soon as possible".

## A distress call before dawn

The trawler left **Kasimedu fishing harbour on October 29** with ten days of food, eight life jackets and five life buoys, bound for the fishing grounds near Nizampatnam. Tamil Nadu's fisheries department issued a cyclone warning on October 31. At about 3 am on November 3, a nearby boat picked up a distress call: water had entered the engine room, the engine had failed, the navigation system was broken and the boat was drifting towards Ramayapatnam. Tamil Nadu officials asked the Coast Guard's rescue coordination centre for an aerial search.

Fishermen spotted the **capsized boat off Kothapatnam** on the evening of November 4. Raja Babu said officials "believe the boat capsized due to Cyclone Murasu, and fear that all of them are dead". He said the crew had set out from Nizampatnam without telling the owner or officials.

## Families travel to Ongole

The bodies are badly decomposed. John Becker, assistant director of fisheries for Chennai North, travelled to Ongole with officials and relatives, who he said "will identify the bodies of their men based on the tattoos and other marks". Tamil Nadu has already announced **₹7 lakh for each family**.
