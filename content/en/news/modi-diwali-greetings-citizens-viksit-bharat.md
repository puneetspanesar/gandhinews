Prime Minister Narendra Modi has sent a **Diwali message to the people of India** on the eve of the festival, which falls on Sunday.

He opened with the words: "Dear fellow citizen, warm greetings on the auspicious occasion of Diwali!" He described the festival as the **victory of light over darkness**, of goodness over evil and of hope over despair.

"May Diwali illuminate every home with happiness, harmony and prosperity," the message says. Modi hoped the season would encourage **kindness and positivity** among people, and spoke of cherishing India's traditions.

He also asked citizens to work towards a prosperous **Viksit Bharat**, the government's goal of a developed India. The message closes with the line: "Once again, wishing you a Happy Diwali."

The message was published on the government's Jan Sampark portal.

In Ayodhya, the 10th Deepotsav is being held on Saturday, with Vice-President C P Radhakrishnan due to join the festival of lights.
