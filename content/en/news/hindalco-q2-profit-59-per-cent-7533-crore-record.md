Hindalco Industries, the Aditya Birla group's metals company, on Friday reported **its highest ever quarterly profit**. Consolidated net profit for July to September rose 59% to ₹7,533 crore, from ₹4,741 crore a year earlier, and revenue from operations climbed 39% to ₹91,708 crore from ₹66,058 crore.

The profit was **well ahead of the street estimate of about ₹6,749 crore**, and it was also about 7% higher than the ₹7,013 crore the company earned in April to June. It includes ₹284 crore of one-time income.

## Copper and aluminium at home

The India business did most of the work. Its earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation (EBITDA) **rose 66% to ₹9,011 crore**. The copper business had its best quarter yet, with EBITDA of ₹1,673 crore, up 164%, while upstream aluminium, the smelting side that gains most from higher global prices, earned ₹7,009 crore, up 55%, at a margin of 52%.

Across all its businesses, Hindalco's segment EBITDA was ₹13,869 crore, against ₹9,104 crore a year ago. The company said nearly half its earnings now come from businesses that do not track the London Metal Exchange price directly: Novelis, copper and downstream aluminium.

## Novelis recovers

Novelis, the US-based maker of rolled aluminium for drinks cans and cars that Hindalco owns, **raised its adjusted EBITDA 20% to $508 million**, on net sales of $5.9 billion. It shipped slightly less metal, 919 kilotonnes against 941 a year earlier, but earned $553 for every tonne, up 23%. The quarter included $39 million in insurance recoveries for the 2025 fires at its Oswego plant in New York, taking the total recovered to $519 million.

## Debt rises with expansion

The growth is costing money. **Net debt rose to ₹80,435 crore** on September 30, from ₹64,841 crore at the end of March, and the ratio of net debt to EBITDA rose to 1.80 times, from 1.23 a year earlier.

Hindalco is building a new Novelis plant at Bay Minette in the US state of Alabama, which it expects to begin commercial shipments in the April to June quarter of 2027. Phase 2 of its Aditya smelter in Odisha is on track to start producing metal in 2027-28. It has also added a 65 MW round-the-clock renewable power project at Aditya Aluminium, taking its renewable capacity to 643 MW.

The company was to discuss the results with analysts on a call at 4:30 pm.
