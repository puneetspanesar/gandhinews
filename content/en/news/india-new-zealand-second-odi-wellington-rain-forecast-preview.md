India will try to go **2-0 up in the five-match ODI series** against New Zealand in Wellington on Saturday, but the weather may have the bigger say. Play at the Wellington Regional Stadium is due to start at **7:30 am IST**, which is 3 pm local time, with the toss half an hour earlier.

The forecast for match day is grim. It gives **about a 75% chance of rain**, with showers most likely between 1 pm and 5 pm local time, the chance rising from about 55% at 1 pm to nearly 75% by 3 pm, just as the first ball is due. Top temperatures of 18 C and humidity close to 90% would also suit the fast bowlers, if they get a full day to use them.

## Where the series stands

Rain had already shaped the opener. At Eden Park on Wednesday, India bowled New Zealand out for 185 and then, after a long stoppage, reached a revised target in a chase cut to 32 overs to **win by five wickets on the DLS method**. They lost four wickets after the restart, the one wobble in an otherwise tidy day.

Shubman Gill leads India for the first three games before Shreyas Iyer takes over for the last two, with Mitchell Santner in charge of New Zealand throughout. Virat Kohli and Rohit Sharma are back in the one-day side after the T20 series, which India won 3-2.

## Siraj back in the frame

The one selection talking point is **Mohammed Siraj**. The fast bowler missed the opener while playing for India A against New Zealand A, and was reported to have joined the one-day squad in Wellington. Arshdeep Singh, Gurnoor Brar and Anshul Kamboj shared the new ball in Auckland, so his return would give Gill another option in the attack.

After the one-day series, the teams play **two Tests**, the first due to start on November 19 at the Basin Reserve, also in Wellington.
