Shubman Gill has set the tone for India's one-day series in New Zealand with a confident message. On the eve of the first of five ODIs at Eden Park in Auckland, India's captain said in a video put out by the BCCI that **any Indian team can beat anyone**.

"I feel that any Indian team playing on the ground is capable of beating any other team," Gill said. "If we win the series here, it will be a big confidence booster for us." He also flagged the odd shape of Eden Park, where the straight boundaries are short. "The dimensions are a bit unusual, and I think it will be a challenge for us to find the right placement for the fielders," he said.

India arrive after **losing the last two T20Is**, though they still took that series 3-2. The one-day squad brings back Rohit Sharma and Virat Kohli, with Shreyas Iyer as Gill's deputy for the first three games. Jasprit Bumrah is not part of the ODI squads.

India have one setback already. **Prasidh Krishna has been ruled out** with a hamstring injury, and left-arm seamer Auqib Nabi has joined the squad for the first three matches. Nitish Kumar Reddy is set to play the opener before he leaves to join India A.

New Zealand are hit harder. Rachin Ravindra and Mark Chapman are **out of the series with injuries**, with Henry Nicholls and Tim Seifert called in. Devon Conway misses the opener after a concussion in training, and Matt Henry needs a fitness check. Mitchell Santner leads the side.

Chasing sides have won 47 of the 86 men's ODIs played at Eden Park, against 34 for teams batting first, so the captain who wins the toss is likely to bowl. Sunny weather is forecast, with no rain expected. The match starts at **7:30 am IST on Wednesday**. The second ODI is in Wellington on November 7.
