**One Mobikwik Systems swung back to profit** in the September quarter, the digital payments company said on Monday, reporting consolidated net profit of ₹11.83 crore against a net loss of ₹28.61 crore a year earlier.

Revenue from operations **rose 9.44% to ₹296 crore**. The swing at the bottom line, of more than ₹40 crore, was far larger than the rise in income at the Gurugram-based company, which runs a digital wallet, UPI payments and small loans. No breakdown by business came with the headline numbers.

The result matters for a company that has spent years trying to show it can make money from payments, a business with thin margins in India because most UPI transfers carry no fee. A **return to profit after a loss-making quarter** a year ago gives it a better story to tell investors.

## Cranes in demand

Later in the afternoon, crane hire company Sanghvi Movers reported a **consolidated net profit of ₹40 crore**, up 11.19% from ₹36.27 crore a year earlier. Revenue from operations climbed 32% to ₹277 crore, a faster rise than in profit.

Both results came on a busy reporting day, with **ITC, UPL and Marico** among the companies still to announce their September-quarter numbers.
