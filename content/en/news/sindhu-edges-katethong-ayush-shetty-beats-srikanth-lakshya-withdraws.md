P V Sindhu nearly let a winning position slip away before reaching the second round of the French Open in Paris on Wednesday. The Indian beat Thailand's Supanida Katethong 21-14, 8-21, 23-21 in 64 minutes, after leading 19-8 in the deciding game.

Katethong won 11 points in a row to draw level at 19-19, turning what had looked a routine finish into a test of nerve. Sindhu held on to take it 23-21 and improve her record against the Thai to six wins in 10 meetings. She will next face Japan's Riko Gunji, who beat India's Unnati Hooda 21-18, 12-21, 21-17.

## Ayush beats Srikanth

The biggest Indian result of the day came in an all-Indian men's singles match. Ayush Shetty beat former world No. 1 Kidambi Srikanth 19-21, 21-16, 21-12. Srikanth led by four points early in the decider, but Ayush was ahead 11-9 at the change of ends and pulled away with seven points in a row from 14-12.

Ayush's reward is a second-round match against Japan's sixth seed Kodai Naraoka, who went through without hitting a shuttle. Lakshya Sen withdrew before their first-round match. He was reported to have pulled out as a precaution, carrying fatigue and muscle tightness from the Denmark Open the previous week.

## Exits elsewhere

Devika Sihag went out in three games to Indonesia's fifth seed Putri Kusuma Wardani, and sisters Rutaparna and Swetaparna Panda lost 21-6, 21-11 to Japan's Ririna Hiramoto and Chiharu Shida in the women's doubles.

Tanvi Sharma and Ashmita Chaliha had already reached the second round on Tuesday, a day when Satwiksairaj Rankireddy and Chirag Shetty, and Treesa Jolly and Gayatri Gopichand, lost their opening matches.
