Denis Shapovalov was **a break up in the deciding set** against Karen Khachanov at the Paris Masters on Monday night when his body let him down. The Canadian retired with a right thigh problem, sending the Russian into the second round **6-4, 6-7, 1-4 retired**.

It had been a tight first-round match. Khachanov took the first set 6-4, and Shapovalov **levelled it in a tie-break**. He then broke early in the third and was serving at 3-1, 40-30, when he stopped, clutched his right thigh and called for the physio.

He came back after **a six-minute medical timeout**, limping, and still held serve for 4-1. But he could not carry on, and after signs from his box he quit during the following game. Whether the problem was a muscle tear or cramp was not clear on the night.

Khachanov will play **13th seed Alejandro Davidovich Fokina or French wildcard Moise Kouame** in the second round. Those two meet on Tuesday.

## The rest of Monday

Elsewhere at the Paris La Défense Arena, Lorenzo Musetti beat Hubert Hurkacz in **two tie-break sets**, 7-6 (3), 7-6 (10). Ninth seed Rafael Jodar **beat Alejandro Tabilo 6-2, 6-3**, and Casper Ruud needed only an hour to see off qualifier Juan Manuel Cerundolo 6-3, 6-2.

Cameron Norrie came through in three sets against Matteo Arnaldi, 6-4, 1-6, 6-3. The French wildcards had a harder day: **Kyrian Jacquet lost 7-6 (7), 6-1** to qualifier Tomas Machac, and Titouan Droguet went down 4-6, 6-4, 6-4 to qualifier Alex Molcan. Qualifier Rinky Hijikata beat Luciano Darderi 6-2, 6-2.
