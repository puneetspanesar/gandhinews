The second one-day international between India and New Zealand has been held up in Wellington, where **the toss was delayed** on Saturday morning by rain and then by a wet outfield. The match was due to start at 7:30 am IST, with the toss half an hour earlier.

The rain had stopped by about 6:56 am IST, but the outfield was slow to clear. Umpires and ground staff met at about 7:22 am IST and **there was still no clearance for play to begin**. A fresh inspection was set for 7:45 am IST (3:15 pm local), and the toss was then reported to be due at **8:05 am IST**. No decision on the number of overs had been announced at the last report.

## What is at stake

Shubman Gill's side lead the five-match series **1-0**. They won the opener at Eden Park in Auckland by five wickets after rain forced the DLS method into the chase, which India completed on a target of 143. A win in Wellington would give them a 2-0 lead.

Forecasts for the day had warned of a **60% chance of rain** around the start, with a drier spell expected later in the evening local time, which gives a shortened game some room. There is no reserve day, so a washout would mean the match is abandoned and India keep their 1-0 lead.

## Teams

The final XIs had not been confirmed. Fast bowler Mohammed Siraj has joined the India squad in Wellington, and Matt Henry is expected back for New Zealand after being cleared. Devon Conway is out of the series with concussion.
