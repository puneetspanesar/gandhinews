The India Meteorological Department (IMD) said on Monday, November 16, that the deep depression over the Bay of Bengal is now 820 km south-southeast of Visakhapatnam and expects it to reach the west-central Bay **near the Andhra Pradesh coast around the night of November 19**. It is the first time the department has put a time frame on the system's approach to the coast. The bulletin, based on observations at 5:30 am, says the system is moving north-northwest at about 8 km/h.

The depression is due to become a **cyclonic storm this evening**, when it will be named Akvan, a name contributed by Iran. The IMD expects it to turn into a severe cyclonic storm over the west-central Bay on Tuesday evening, November 17.

## What the bulletin says

At 5:30 am the system lay over the southeast and adjoining southwest Bay of Bengal. It was about 720 km east-southeast of Chennai, 940 km south of Gopalpur in Odisha, 680 km west of Sri Vijaya Puram in the Andaman and Nicobar Islands and 630 to 640 km east-northeast of Trincomalee in Sri Lanka.

On Sunday the system was 1,010 km south-southeast of Visakhapatnam, so it has **closed about 190 km in a day**. Our [Sunday report](/en/news/akvan-deep-depression-monday-imd-landfall-night-nov-19) carried the department's table that takes it to a very severe cyclonic storm by early November 19, with winds of 120 to 130 km/h and gusts to 145 km/h. Monday's bulletin does not change that peak. It adds the timing of the approach, and the department has still not named a landfall point.

The Andhra Pradesh State Disaster Management Authority (APSDMA) said strong winds of **40 to 60 km/h** are expected along the coastal belt from Monday evening. Heavy to very heavy rain with strong winds is forecast along coastal Andhra on Thursday and Friday, November 19 and 20, with isolated extremely heavy falls in Kakinada, Konaseema, Krishna and Yanam.

## Warnings along the coast

Fishermen have been told not to go to sea. The IMD advises them to avoid the Andaman Sea and the southeast Bay until Tuesday, the southwest Bay until Thursday and the central Bay until Friday. Shipping, port and offshore operators have been asked to review their preparedness.

In Tamil Nadu, the Regional Meteorological Centre in Chennai forecast **moderate rain on Monday** in isolated places of the northern coastal districts, Puducherry and Karaikal. Heavy showers are likely on Tuesday in Chennai, Tiruvallur and Chengalpattu, and very heavy rain on Wednesday and Thursday, November 18 and 19, in the same three districts. Heavy rain is also forecast on Wednesday in Kancheepuram, Ranipet, Tiruvannamalai, Villupuram, Cuddalore, Mayiladuthurai and Puducherry.

Odisha's impact begins on **Wednesday, November 18**, with a yellow warning for 16 districts. Orange warnings for heavy rain follow for Puri, Ganjam and Gajapati on November 19; Ganjam, Gajapati, Rayagada, Koraput and Malkangiri on November 20; and Bargarh, Jharsuguda and Sundargarh on November 21. In West Bengal, heavy rain is forecast in coastal South 24 Parganas and Purba Medinipur from November 19 to 21, and in Howrah, Paschim Medinipur and Jhargram on November 20 and 21.

The Odisha government has cancelled leave, opened 24-hour control rooms in the districts and put the National Disaster Response Force, the Odisha Disaster Rapid Action Force and fire services on standby. Farmers have been urged to harvest standing crops before the rain on November 18 to 21. Ganjam Collector V. Keerthi Vasan told officers: "If required, people will be taken to safe locations and cyclone shelters." He asked them to check drinking water, food, lighting and toilets at every shelter.

## How we got here

The IMD first flagged the system on Friday, November 13, as a low that would become a cyclonic storm by Monday, as our [first report](/en/news/akvan-deep-depression-monday-imd-landfall-night-nov-19) set out. On Saturday it raised the outlook to a [severe storm](/en/news/akvan-deep-depression-monday-imd-landfall-night-nov-19) and on Sunday it added the [very severe stage](/en/news/akvan-deep-depression-monday-imd-landfall-night-nov-19) on November 19. Andhra's disaster authority had earlier said the system could reach the north Andhra coast by that date, as our [Andhra report](/en/news/akvan-deep-depression-monday-imd-landfall-night-nov-19) records.

Cyclone Arnab struck the Andhra coast in late October, and an official statement said at least 1.8 lakh people were evacuated. Cyclone Murasu crossed between Nellore and Narasapur on November 3 as a severe cyclonic storm, and Andhra's damage estimate for it has since been [revised to ₹2,484 crore](/en/news/murasu-andhra-damage-revised-2484-crore-central-team-naidu). The two storms are reported to have killed 15 people between them.

## Why it matters

Akvan would be the **third cyclone to reach the Andhra coast in about three weeks**. Coastal districts that are still repairing roads, power lines and fields will have only a few days between the first rain on Tuesday and the peak on Thursday night. A storm with winds of 120 to 130 km/h brings down trees and power lines and damages kutcha houses, and the sea along the coast will be very rough to phenomenal from November 19, which makes any boat trip unsafe.

The practical question for residents of Visakhapatnam, Kakinada, Konaseema and Krishna is where the storm will cross. Reports differ on the likely zone: some place the impact in south Andhra and the border with northern Tamil Nadu, while the Sunday forecasts pointed to the north Andhra coast. The IMD says the track will be clearer as the system develops over the Bay.

## What happens next

The first marker is **this evening**, when the IMD expects the deep depression to become a cyclonic storm and name it Akvan. The severe stage is due on the evening of Tuesday, November 17, and the approach to the coast around the night of Thursday, November 19. The IMD's next bulletins, issued through Monday and Tuesday, are the ones in which a landfall coast and time could be named. Until then, fishing remains off limits and districts in Andhra, Odisha and Tamil Nadu are preparing for rain from November 17 and 18.
