Victor Wembanyama scored **43 points and blocked six shots** as the San Antonio Spurs beat the Minnesota Timberwolves 121-111 on Saturday night, Sunday morning in India.

The French centre made 17 of his 34 shots, **a career-high number of attempts**, and added seven rebounds, four assists and a steal in 33 minutes. Only once has he scored more in his four NBA seasons: **50 points against the Washington Wizards** in November 2024. He had set his season high of 36 against the Utah Jazz only on Wednesday, adding 14 rebounds in a 117-111 overtime win in Salt Lake City.

Minnesota arrived without Anthony Edwards, who had **knee surgery on Thursday** and could be out for up to two months. Edwards had scored a franchise-record 133 points in the Timberwolves' first four games. Coach Chris Finch had said after the surgery: "We'll have to kind of fill that scoring void by committee." The Timberwolves had beaten the Los Angeles Clippers 127-101 at home on Friday and were playing the second night of a back-to-back.

The Spurs lost their first two games of the season but have now **won four in a row**, moving to 4-2, while Minnesota drop to 4-3. Wembanyama ends October averaging 28 points, 10.7 rebounds, 1.3 steals and **4.2 blocks a game**.

San Antonio stay at home to face Utah on Monday and the Dallas Mavericks on Wednesday.
