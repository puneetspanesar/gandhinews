India's defence of the SAFF Championship title is in doubt, with the All India Football Federation yet to decide how, or whether, it can field a side in Bangladesh while its senior team travels to New Zealand.

The tournament is scheduled for Dhaka from November 5 to 17. India are due to open against Pakistan on November 5 and play their second and final group match against the Maldives on November 11.

## A crowded November

The senior team is booked for **two friendlies in New Zealand** on November 12 and 15, marking 100 years of sporting relations between the two countries. It would be India's first match against New Zealand on their home soil, against a side that played at the 2026 FIFA World Cup.

The federation's plan had been to send an under-23 squad to the SAFF Championship and the senior side to New Zealand. That plan is still in place, but has not been confirmed.

"We are waiting and discussions are on. We have not finalised anything," said a person involved in the discussions.

## Why it is complicated

The SAFF Championship starts four days before the FIFA international window opens on November 9, which means clubs are not obliged to release players for the opening game. "It is outside the FIFA window, so getting players will be difficult," the same person said.

Indian Super League clubs are in the middle of their season, and several players likely to be picked are key to their sides. There is also a question over whether the organisers would accept an under-23 team, since SAFF Championship matches count towards the FIFA rankings. India have sent younger squads to past editions.

The federation has not said when it will decide.
