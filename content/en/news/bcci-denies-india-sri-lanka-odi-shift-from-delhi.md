The BCCI has denied that India's first one-day international against Sri Lanka will be moved out of Delhi. Secretary Devajit Saikia said on Monday that reports of the December 13 match going to Ahmedabad were wrong.

The reports had linked the move to construction work under way at the Arun Jaitley Stadium. Saikia said the board had made no such call, and that bilateral matches are allotted to venues under its standard policy.

> Reports suggesting that the India-Sri Lanka ODI match scheduled in Delhi may be shifted to Ahmedabad are not accurate. There has been no such decision by the BCCI.
>
> Source: Devajit Saikia, BCCI secretary, October 12, 2026

He asked the media to "refer to official BCCI communications for any updates regarding the schedule".

## The December tour

Sri Lanka are due in India from December 13 to 27 for three ODIs and three T20Is, with the opening ODI in Delhi on Sunday, December 13. Before that, India tour New Zealand, starting with five T20Is from October 22. After the Delhi opener, the second ODI is in Bengaluru and the third at the Narendra Modi Stadium in Ahmedabad on December 19. The T20Is follow in Rajkot, Cuttack and Pune.

For now, the schedule stands as announced, with Delhi hosting the opening match.
