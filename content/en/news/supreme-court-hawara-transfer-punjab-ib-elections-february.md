The Intelligence Bureau has told the Supreme Court that moving **Jagtar Singh Hawara**, serving life for the 1995 assassination of Punjab chief minister Beant Singh, from Delhi's Tihar Jail to a prison in Punjab would have **a huge impact on the state's elections**.

A bench of Justices M M Sundresh and P B Varale, which had earlier asked the Delhi government to obtain the IB's view, described the agency's report as **"very damning"** on Tuesday. The IB placed Hawara in the high-risk category and said he may not be transferred to Punjab.

## Two sides in court

Additional Solicitor General Aishwarya Bhati, for the Delhi government, argued that a transfer could **raise the risk of escape**, citing a Home Ministry advisory on high-risk prisoners. Punjab's own affidavit warned of trouble for law and order. Bhati reminded the court that Hawara had been convicted for digging a tunnel out of a Chandigarh jail.

Senior Advocate S Muralidhar, for Hawara, said his client's prison record had been without blemish apart from the 2004 jailbreak, that no case was pending against him in Delhi, and that a co-convict had already been moved from Tihar to Chandigarh. Hawara is from Fatehgarh Sahib district and his daughter lives in Punjab.

> "If elections are a concern, this may be taken up after three months."
>
> Source: S Muralidhar, Senior Advocate for Jagtar Singh Hawara, Supreme Court, November 3, 2026

The bench did not rule on the transfer and **posted the matter to February**.

## The long road from Burail

Hawara, a Babbar Khalsa member, and three others escaped from Burail Jail in Chandigarh on January 22, 2004, through a tunnel of about 100 feet. He was caught again within two years. A trial court sentenced him to death in 2007; in 2010 the Punjab and Haryana High Court **commuted it to life without release**. Appeals by both Hawara and the prosecution are still pending in the Supreme Court.
