Niveditha Veloor Nair won **bronze in the women's 25m standard pistol** at the ISSF World Championship in Doha on Sunday, November 8, and then joined Manvi Jain and Simranpreet Kaur Brar to take **team silver**. The two medals lift India's tally in the Qatari capital to **seven: one gold, five silver and one bronze**.

## How the final finished

Nair shot **567-13x**, one point behind Hungary's Veronika Major (568-13x) and two behind China's Xiao Jiaruixuan, who won gold with 569-10x. Nair and Major each had 13 inner tens, so the one point on the main score separated silver from bronze. Nair's stage scores were 189 in the 150-second series, 193 in the 20-second series and 185 in the 10-second series. Xiao and Major are both Olympic medallists in the women's 25m pistol, which makes the Indian's place on the podium a notable one.

Jain finished **eighth with 560-10x** and Brar tenth with 555-10x, so all three Indians made the top ten of an event that is not on the Olympic programme.

## The team medal

The three scores add up to the team total: 567 from Nair, 560 from Jain and 555 from Brar make **1,682-33x**. That was good enough for silver, 16 points behind China, whose **1,698-43x was a new world record**. Hungary took bronze with 1,676-30x, six points behind India.

In the men's 25m standard pistol team event, India finished fourth, behind Latvia, South Korea and Ukraine. Harsh Gupta was fifth in the individual final with 573-13x.

## How Doha has gone for India

India's first gold of the championship came in the men's 50m pistol team event, won by an Indian trio and announced on November 3. Silver medals followed in the women's 10m air rifle team event and the men's 50m pistol individual event, where Ravinder Singh finished second.

On November 5, Manu Bhaker, Suruchi Inder Singh and Sainyam took the **women's 10m air pistol team silver with 1,738 points**, seven behind China's 1,745. As we reported in our [team silver story](/en/news/manu-bhaker-suruchi-sainyam-team-silver-doha-air-pistol), Bhaker then finished fifth in the individual final with 179.9 and Suruchi sixth with 158.3, so neither won an Olympic place.

The one medal that carries an Olympic place is Jaypal Malviya's silver in the men's 10m air pistol, which scored 242.2 against Kai Hu's 243.9 and gave India its **first shooting quota for the 2028 Los Angeles Games**, as our [Malviya story](/en/news/jaypal-malviya-silver-air-pistol-worlds-first-la-2028-shooting-quota) set out. The championship offers 36 quota places across 12 individual Olympic events, so the standard pistol medals count for rankings and prestige, not for Los Angeles.

The full list so far, as reported on November 9, reads as follows. Gold: men's 50m pistol team. Silver: Ravinder Singh in the men's 50m pistol, the women's 10m air rifle team, the women's 10m air pistol team, Jaypal Malviya in the men's 10m air pistol, and the women's 25m standard pistol team. Bronze: Niveditha Nair in the women's 25m standard pistol. That makes seven medals, and the **only one that brought an Olympic place** is Malviya's.

Friday, November 6, was a lean day: India had no finalist in skeet or in the 10m air pistol mixed team events, as our [report that day](/en/news/india-no-finalist-skeet-air-pistol-mixed-team-doha-worlds) recorded.

## What it means and what next

A team silver in an event outside the Olympic programme, with all three Indians in the top ten of the individual final, shows depth in the women's pistol group beyond the names that usually carry the Olympic events. Nair's bronze is the lone individual medal in the standard pistol for India in Doha.

India's tally of seven is as of the morning of November 9. The ISSF results page carries the final order and the medal table when the programme closes.
