NASA is taking applications for its next class of astronaut candidates, and **the window closes at 11:59 pm Eastern time on December 3, 2026**, which is 10:29 am IST on December 4. The agency announced the call on November 7, 2026, and said the recruits will help return humans to the Moon and, later, support the first crewed mission to Mars. Applications go through the US government's USAJOBS site, with the details at go.nasa.gov/astro2026.

For readers in India the practical point is the first condition: **US citizenship is required**, though dual citizens may apply. The call is a window into how the agency is staffing its Moon programme, and into what kind of person it wants to fly.

## What the call asks for

The job is full time and in person at Johnson Space Center in Houston, not remote. It pays **about $164,395 a year** under 2026 pay schedules, with possible adjustments for 2027 and 2028. Travel is 76% of the time or more. The first appointment runs 30 months and becomes permanent after the candidate completes training.

The standard education requirement is a master's degree in engineering, a biological or physical science, computer science or mathematics. Alternatives include two years of relevant doctoral study or a qualifying medical degree, such as an MD, a DO, a physician assistant or a nurse practitioner qualification. A recognised test pilot school programme also counts if it is completed by July 2027. Medical residency counts towards experience too, if it is finished by that month.

Candidates need at least three years of related professional experience after the qualifying degree. Pilots have hour minimums: **750 hours as pilot in command** in high-performance jets; 1,500 total hours in other fixed-wing aircraft, including 750 as pilot in command; or 1,000 total hours in helicopters and other rotary-wing aircraft, including 300 as pilot in command. People without flight experience can apply through a related-experience route.

Every candidate must also pass NASA's long-duration astronaut flight physical, and the agency looks for demonstrated leadership, teamwork and communication skills.

## How to apply

Applicants submit a résumé or CV, the online application and an assessment questionnaire. The questionnaire asks for STEM transcripts and five references, including the most recent supervisor, and the work history must list dates and weekly hours. NASA's instruction is plain: "Materials mailed or emailed to the Astronaut Selection Office are not considered."

Supporting documents can be updated until the announcement closes, but assessment responses, once submitted, cannot be changed. Some applicants may be sent a further USA Hire assessment, with a separate notice, an access link and its own deadline. People who applied in earlier rounds must reapply and go through the full process again.

## How we got here

NASA's last class was announced on September 22, 2025, and had 10 candidates. This call does not say how large the next class will be, so the number of seats is not known. The recruitment comes as NASA works towards its Moon plans, which we reported when administrator Jared Isaacman set out a [Moon base plan, an Artemis III landing in summer 2027 and a landing in 2028](/news/nasa-isaacman-moon-base-artemis-iii-summer-2027-landing-2028).

Our earlier report on the space station's cargo traffic, [a SpaceX Dragon docking at the International Space Station](/news/spacex-crs-35-dragon-docks-space-station), covers the orbiting laboratory where new astronauts are eventually expected to work.

## What happens next

The schedule NASA has given is tentative. **Applications are reviewed from December 2026 to April 2027.** Interviews follow in the spring, with final interviews in the summer. Selection is expected in late 2027, and the class would be announced in the autumn of 2027 and report for training in the winter of 2027 to 2028.

Candidates who are chosen start as trainees, not as crew. They go through a training period at Johnson Space Center before they can be assigned to any flight, and the permanent post depends on finishing that training successfully.

Readers who qualify and want to apply have until the deadline above; anyone else can follow the class announcement in 2027.
