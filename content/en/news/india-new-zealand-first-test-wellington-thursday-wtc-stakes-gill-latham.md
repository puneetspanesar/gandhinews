**India begin their two-Test tour of New Zealand on Thursday, November 19**, when Shubman Gill's side take on Tom Latham's team at the Basin Reserve in Wellington. Play is due to start at **11 am local time, which is 3:30 am IST**, and the series then moves to Hagley Oval in Christchurch for the second Test from November 27. This report is as of 6:15 am IST on Monday, November 16.

The series comes straight after India's one-day win. Arshdeep Singh's 3 for 30 helped India defend 209 at Bay Oval on Sunday and take the five-match ODI series 3-1, as our [report on the fifth ODI](/en/news/india-beat-new-zealand-fifth-odi-arshdeep-series-3-1-bay-oval) set out. Several players from both ODI squads are now moving to their Test camps, and the teams have four days before the first ball.

## India's side

Gill captains the 16-man squad, with KL Rahul as vice-captain. The big change from the white-ball leg is **Jasprit Bumrah's return**. He sat out both the T20 and ODI parts of the tour and is available for the Tests, where he is expected to lead the pace attack alongside Mohammed Siraj, Prasidh Krishna, Akash Deep and Anshul Kamboj. According to reports, Bumrah has taken 234 wickets in 52 Tests at an average of 19.79, and nine wickets at 45.44 in five Tests against New Zealand. He bowled six overs without a wicket in the third India A game at Lincoln last week.

Rishabh Pant is with the squad after the BCCI medical team cleared him following a side strain, and Dhruv Jurel is the second wicketkeeper. Gurnoor Brar was added to the group on November 14 and joined the Test camp from the ODI squad, while Kuldeep Yadav was released to play domestic cricket at home. Our report on [Pant joining the squad](/en/news/india-test-squad-new-zealand-pant-joins-kuldeep-released-brar-added) has the full list of 16. Mohammed Shami, who [retired from Test cricket](/en/news/mohammed-shami-retires-test-cricket-229-wickets-64-tests) this month, is not part of the plans, which puts more weight on Bumrah.

Gill left the one-day squad after three matches to concentrate on red-ball preparation. According to reports, he and the Test group trained at the Basin Reserve while the fast bowlers from the ODI side stayed back to bowl in the nets, and Brar was wanted to help the batters get used to New Zealand's tall seamers.

## New Zealand's squad

New Zealand named a **14-man squad on November 10**: Tom Latham (captain), Tom Blundell, Devon Conway, Jacob Duffy, Matt Henry, Kyle Jamieson, Daryl Mitchell, Henry Nicholls, Glenn Phillips, Rachin Ravindra, Mitchell Santner, Nathan Smith, Blair Tickner and Will Young. Duffy returns, while Ben Sears (ankle), Zak Foulkes (hip) and Will O'Rourke, who is being managed, are out.

Three of the batters come in with fitness questions. Blundell has a quadriceps problem, Conway is recovering from concussion and Ravindra has a shoulder issue, though all three are in the squad and are aiming to be fit for the opening match. Head coach Rob Walter said the series "marks the start of an exciting summer of Test cricket for the Black Caps" and that the group is "largely consistent with the team that competed in England earlier this year", where New Zealand won the three-Test series.

## What is at stake

The two Tests count towards the **World Test Championship**. According to the ICC, New Zealand are second in the table behind South Africa, with Australia third and India fourth, and both sides are still in the race for next year's final at The Oval. Walter put it plainly: "Valuable Championship points are on the line of course."

According to reports, India had 68 points and a percentage of 51.52 after their drawn series against Sri Lanka, with seven matches left. A 2-0 win in New Zealand would lift them to about 59 per cent, a split would leave them close to where they are now, and a draw is worth four points out of 12. India have not won a Test series in New Zealand since 2009, according to the same reports.

The recent history also hangs over the series. New Zealand beat India 3-0 in India in 2024, the only time India have lost all three Tests of a home series, and this is the first Test meeting between the sides since then.

## What happens next

The first Test runs from **November 19** and is scheduled to finish on November 23 at the Basin Reserve, with the second Test at Hagley Oval from November 27. Both start at 11 am local time. The India squad can still change before the toss, and New Zealand will decide in the next few days whether Blundell, Conway and Ravindra are fit to play.
