India's Test preparations in New Zealand have begun in earnest, with a camp in Wellington that opened on **Wednesday, November 11**, and the first Test against New Zealand due to start on November 19, according to reports. Test captain Shubman Gill, vice-captain KL Rahul and fast bowler Anshul Kamboj have left the one-day squad to join it.

The three had been picked only for the first three ODIs, as the selectors said on October 16, and went to Wellington after the third match at Hamilton was abandoned on November 10. Nitish Kumar Reddy, who played the first ODI, is also joining the Test group. Our [report on the October squads](/en/news/gill-iyer-split-odi-captaincy-new-zealand-bumrah-jadeja-out) has the full Test squad, with Gill as captain, Rahul as vice-captain and Jasprit Bumrah named.

## Who is where

Batting coach Sitanshu Kotak and fast bowling coach Morne Morkel are running the Test camp. Head coach Gautam Gambhir has stayed with the ODI team for the fourth and fifth games on November 13 and 15, a decision Kotak said rested with Gambhir. "Me, Morne and some other staff will go to Wellington," Kotak said, as reported after the Hamilton washout.

The split is not clean. Mohammed Siraj is in the ODI squad for Mount Maunganui but is to join the Test group later. Kotak has said Gurnoor Brar, Auqib Nabi and Arshdeep Singh will help the Test side as bowlers, with the hedge "Gurnoor, maybe Nabi" in his remarks on November 9, even though all three are also named in the ODI squad. Kotak also said: **"I think three bowlers are going to stay back anyway."** The reports do not settle who will actually bowl at Bay Oval and who will be in the nets at Wellington, and Kotak himself said **the plan "can still change."**

Tilak Varma, wicketkeeper Kumar Kushagra and Yash Thakur were added to the ODI squad to cover the departures. The [fourth ODI preview](/en/news/iyer-first-odi-captaincy-mount-maunganui-fourth-odi-preview) has the likely sides.


## The preparation window

Kotak had said the Test specialists would get **five or six sessions** from November 11. He said preparation had been good and added: "Fingers crossed, we should be ready for the Test series as well." The camp therefore has about a week of work before the opening Test, with players such as Rohit Sharma and Virat Kohli, who are not in the Test squad, staying with the ODIs.

New Zealand have done the same. Tom Latham, Daryl Mitchell, Henry Nicholls, Glenn Phillips and Nathan Smith have left their one-day squad for the Test camp, and the home side's list of absentees is longer than India's: Lockie Ferguson is out of the one-day series with a calf injury and Blair Tickner is serving a seven-day stand-down after being hit on the helmet by a Kamboj bouncer.

## What happens next

The Test group trains in Wellington while the ODI side plays at Bay Oval on Friday and Sunday. The first Test begins on November 19, 2026. Which of the fast bowlers move from the one-day squad to the Test camp, and whether Siraj joins before or after the fifth ODI, should become clear once the ODI series ends on November 15.
