The United States military struck Iran's **Qeshm Island**, the largest island in the Strait of Hormuz, on Monday night, according to US officials, who described the raid as a defensive move to stop Iran attacking commercial shipping in the waterway.

Iran's state news agency IRNA reported **five explosions in rapid succession** on the island, and residents said they saw two aircraft overhead. The semi-official Fars agency reported blasts at the mainland port of Bandar Lengeh, coming from the direction of the sea. Tehran had not issued an official statement by early Tuesday and no casualties have been reported, though an Iranian official was later reported by state media to have said that US forces attacked fishing boats off the island.

One US official said the targets were "Iranians using surface to air missiles against US assets". Officials said Monday's raid was the latest in a **series of limited strikes kept quiet** in recent weeks, so as not to signal a return to full-scale war. Washington has not announced them, and US Central Command did not respond to requests for comment.

The strike came on the **eve of Tuesday's US midterm elections**, after Donald Trump had said there would be no attack on Iran before Americans voted. On Monday the President said a deal with Tehran "could happen" and played down the conflict. "We had a war with Iran. We won," he said, adding: "I don't know if you even call it a war because we haven't been doing any fighting for months." Asked whether he was weighing strikes after the elections, he replied "no".

Qatari mediators have held two rounds of talks with Iranian officials on a framework under which Iran would stop attacks in Hormuz and the US would **lift its naval blockade** of Iranian oil tankers. A third US aircraft carrier is on its way to the region.

## Why it matters for India

Oil, which had **fallen more than 7% on Monday** on hopes of a deal, turned higher in early Asian trade. Brent rose 0.5% to $105.25 a barrel before settling near $104.4 by about 8 am IST.
