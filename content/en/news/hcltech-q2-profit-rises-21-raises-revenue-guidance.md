HCLTech's net profit rose **21% to ₹5,132 crore** in July-September from ₹4,235 crore a year earlier, and the company raised the bottom of its revenue growth forecast for the year, results released on Monday showed.

Revenue from operations rose **11.5%** to ₹35,620 crore. On the June quarter, profit was up 11% and revenue 3%. In constant currency, which strips out exchange-rate moves, revenue grew 2.3% sequentially and services revenue 3%.

## Guidance goes up at the bottom

HCLTech now expects revenue for 2026-27 to grow **2.5% to 4%** in constant currency, up from its earlier range of 1% to 4%. Services revenue is expected to grow 3% to 4%. The operating margin forecast is unchanged at 17.5% to 18.5%.

The operating (EBIT) margin for the quarter was **18.2%**, up 131 basis points from the June quarter.

The board declared an interim dividend of **₹12** a share. The record date is October 16 and the dividend will be paid on October 26.

## AI work picks up

Revenue from what the company calls advanced AI rose to $219 million, up 28.4% on the quarter and 78% on the year in constant currency. New bookings grew 27% from a year earlier to about $3.3 billion, and bookings for the first half of the year were a record.

> Our AI propositions are resonating well with our clients, giving us confidence in our growth trajectory.
>
> Source: C Vijayakumar, CEO and Managing Director, HCLTech, October 12, 2026

Roshni Nadar Malhotra, the company's chairperson, said "the accelerating growth in Advanced AI revenue validates our business strategy."

## How it compares

HCLTech's numbers follow TCS, which reported a 15% rise in profit and constant-currency revenue growth of 0.5% for the quarter last week. Wipro and Tech Mahindra are due to report on Thursday, October 15.

HCLTech shares ended Monday's session higher even as the Sensex fell 548 points. The company's market value stood at about ₹3.31 lakh crore.
