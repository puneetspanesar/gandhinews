Prime Minister Narendra Modi will **inaugurate the Ropuiliani Tribal Freedom Fighters Museum** at Kelsih near Aizawl, Mizoram, by video link on Sunday, November 15, 2026. The Union Ministry of Tribal Affairs has provided **₹25.59 crore** for the museum, which honours the Mizo chieftainess who resisted British rule and which opens on Janjatiya Gaurav Diwas, the 151st birth anniversary of Birsa Munda.

The opening is one of 246 projects the Prime Minister's Office says he will inaugurate or begin on the day, when he joins the [Gadchiroli programme in Maharashtra at 11 am](/en/news/modi-janjatiya-gaurav-diwas-gadchiroli-246-tribal-projects-3000-crore). The Office's statement of Saturday singled out the museum, along with a Tribal Research Institute building in Itanagar, among the buildings he will open.

## What the museum will hold

The museum stands inside the Tribal Research Institute campus at Kelsih, a village in the Aibawk block of Aizawl district. Besides the **museum gallery**, the complex has an open-air theatre, a traditional hut, a heritage walk, the Birsa Munda Tribal Pride Garden, a cafeteria and staff quarters. The Indian National Trust for Art and Cultural Heritage is reported to be curating and developing the project, whose project report was prepared in June 2025.

It is named after Ropuiliani, a chieftainess in the Lushai Hills who stood against British colonial rule and its taxation. She was taken into custody and **died in British captivity at Rangamati in January 1895**, according to reports on the museum. It is meant to commemorate that resistance and the contribution of Mizo freedom fighters to the independence movement.


## The Governor's inspection

Mizoram Governor General Vijay Kumar Singh (retired) inspected the preparations on November 10. He called the museum "a significant tribute to the courage, sacrifice and contributions of Mizo freedom fighters to the freedom movement", said it would be **a heritage asset** for Mizoram and the country, and asked officials to develop it into a "world-class facility". He told them to finish the remaining work using local resources and to keep the design true to Mizo culture and heritage.

Union Minister of State for Tribal Affairs Durgadas Uikey, who is among the officials associated with the event, said: "The North East has got national attention only after Prime Minister Narendra Modi came to power in 2014." That is the minister's view; no reaction from Mizoram's political parties was available this morning.

## How we got here

The museums are a Ministry of Tribal Affairs programme. In a written reply in the Lok Sabha on February 5, 2026, Uikey told the House that **11 Tribal Freedom Fighters' Museums had been sanctioned across 10 states**, funded under the centrally sponsored schemes "Support to Tribal Research Institute" and the proviso to Article 275(1). Four were then complete, in Jharkhand, Chhattisgarh and Madhya Pradesh (two in the last).

The Prime Minister has opened them on or near Janjatiya Gaurav Diwas. The Birsa Munda Memorial Park and Freedom Fighter Museum at Ranchi was inaugurated on November 15, 2021, the first Janjatiya Gaurav Diwas. The Chhindwara museum followed on November 15, 2024, and the one at Nava Raipur on November 1, 2025.

The same reply listed Mizoram as one of five museums under construction or curation, with completion expected by the end of 2026, and described the Ropuiliani museum as **proposed to open by mid-2026**. It has opened about five months after that mark. The reply put Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Kerala and Gujarat in the same group. Goa and Manipur were at an early stage: the Rani Gaidinliu museum at Luangkao in Tamenglong, Manipur, was then at the stage of the detailed project report, still being prepared by the state government.

## Why it matters

For Mizoram the museum puts a woman chief who resisted British taxation into a Union-funded memorial, and gives Aizawl district a visitor destination of the kind the Governor has asked to be built to a high standard. The scale is modest next to the day's ₹3,000 crore package, but the museum is the one item on the list that records history rather than delivering a service.

Questions remain that the announcements do not answer: the museum's opening hours and ticketing, how many galleries are ready, and whether the "remaining work" the Governor spoke of on November 10 is complete.

## What happens next

The Prime Minister speaks at **11 am on Sunday**, and the Mizoram inauguration is part of that programme. His next scheduled engagement is the [ASEAN-India summit in Manila](/en/news/modi-asean-india-summit-manila-november-16-18) from November 16 to 18. Of the other museums, the ones in the southern and western states are due by the end of 2026, according to the February reply.
