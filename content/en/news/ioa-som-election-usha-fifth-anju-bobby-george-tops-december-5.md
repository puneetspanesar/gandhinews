**PT Usha, the president of the Indian Olympic Association, finished fifth** in the election for the women's Sportspersons of Merit seats on Monday, November 9, and will not sit in the IOA General Assembly through that route. **Anju Bobby George topped the poll with 53 votes**, and Rani Rampal, Dola Banerjee and Saina Nehwal took the other three seats.

The result is a setback for Usha less than four weeks before the IOA elects its Executive Council for the 2026-2030 term on **December 5**. Under the IOA constitution a presidential candidate must be a Sportsperson of Merit (SOM) or a member of the General Assembly, and Usha is now neither.

## How the vote went

All **80 SOM voters cast ballots** in a virtual poll that ran from 9 am to 5 pm, according to the Electoral Officer, Sanjay Kumar Srivastava. Six women contested four seats. Anju Bobby George, the World Championships long jump medallist, polled 53. Rani Rampal, the former hockey captain, got 50, Dola Banerjee, the archer, 45 and Saina Nehwal, the badminton Olympic medallist, 43. **Usha polled 40**, three votes short of the last winning seat, and Anitha Paul Durai got 6.

The men's seats were settled earlier. On Saturday, November 7, **Gagan Narang, Vijender Singh, Yogeshwar Dutt and Kamlesh Mehta were elected unopposed** after eight other candidates withdrew, among them Adille Sumariwalla and Achanta Sharath Kamal. The withdrawals have been described as inexplicable; the field had already shrunk from 27 to 23 by November 5.

## Complaints before the poll

The vote was preceded by complaints to the Electoral Officer. Usha wrote that **Dola Banerjee broke the model code of conduct** by forwarding a WhatsApp message that backed a "New Bharat Panel" and listed four candidates with details of how to mark the ballot. Weightlifter Sathish Sivalingam separately alleged that "an organised effort is underway to influence members of the Electoral Roll" for a candidate who is also the IOA president, and that IOA resources were being used for it. He did not name Usha.

## What Usha can do now

Usha came to the presidency in December 2022, when she was **elected unopposed as the IOA's first woman president**, and she reached the post through the SOM route. That route has now closed. She can still stand on December 5 if a National Sports Federation nominates her to the General Assembly, but that looks unlikely: she is not expected to be one of the Athletics Federation of India's two representatives.

The eight newly elected SOMs join the General Assembly and can both vote and contest in December. One man and one woman from the group will also take **seats on the Executive Council**. Some reports have raised Saina Nehwal's name as a possible challenger for the presidency, but no candidacy has been announced.

## Why the SOM seats matter

The Sportspersons of Merit are the athletes' voice inside the IOA. Eight of them, four men and four women, now sit in the General Assembly alongside representatives of the National Sports Federations, and they can vote on December 5 as well as stand. That is why the women's contest drew such attention: the four women who won are all former international athletes, and the **result gives athletes a larger say** in who runs the body for the 2026-2030 term.

For Usha, the loss is also a matter of timing. Her four years in office began in December 2022 without a contest, so Monday's ballot was the first time her standing among fellow athletes was tested at the polls. She finished only three votes behind the last winner, Saina Nehwal, in a poll where each of the 80 voters could back several candidates.

## The field narrowed before polling

The men's side shows how much was settled before voting began. Of the 27 contestants listed at the start, 23 remained by November 5, and by Saturday eight men had withdrawn, leaving the four places to be filled **without a single ballot being cast**. The women's seats were the only SOM contest that went to a vote, which is why the complaints about campaigning centred on it.

## What happens next

The IOA election for the 2026-2030 Executive Council is on **December 5**. The question for the next four weeks is whether any federation puts Usha forward, whether the complaints are acted on by the Electoral Officer, and who files for the presidency.
