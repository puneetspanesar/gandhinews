Prime Minister Narendra Modi is due to **open the Bharat Renewable Energy Summit and Expo** at Bharat Mandapam in New Delhi on Monday, the first edition of a four-day event the government hopes will pull in investment for its clean energy push.

The summit, known as BRESE 2026, runs **from November 2 to 5** and is being organised by the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy under the theme "Invest. Innovate. Inspire." Policymakers, investors, manufacturers and researchers from more than 130 countries are expected.

For the first time, three events share one stage: BRESE itself, the **ninth session of the International Solar Alliance Assembly**, to which 128 countries were invited, and the fourth International Conference on Green Hydrogen. Australia, Denmark, the European Union, France, Germany, the Netherlands, Norway, Sweden and the United Kingdom are country partners, and 13 states and Union Territories, among them Andhra Pradesh, Gujarat, Rajasthan and Uttar Pradesh, will pitch for projects. More than 400 companies, startups and research bodies are taking part in the exhibition.

## The money question

India has about **308 GW of non-fossil fuel capacity** installed and is aiming for 500 GW by 2030. Ministry Secretary Santosh Kumar Sarangi has said getting there will take roughly $220 billion of investment between 2027 and 2030, along with stronger transmission lines, more energy storage and more domestic manufacturing.

Sessions over the four days cover financing, battery storage, green hydrogen and manufacturing, with ministerial meetings and CEO roundtables alongside.
