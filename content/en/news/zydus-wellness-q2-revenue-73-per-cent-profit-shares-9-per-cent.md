Zydus Wellness **returned to profit** in the September quarter on Tuesday, and its shares rose sharply as investors cheered a sharp jump in its sales.

The Ahmedabad-based consumer products company, part of the Zydus group, reported consolidated net profit of ₹5.1 crore for the three months to September 30, 2026. A year earlier it had **lost ₹52.8 crore**.

Revenue from operations **jumped 73.2% to ₹1,126.6 crore**, from ₹650.5 crore in the same quarter last year, a rise of about ₹476 crore in a single year.

## Stock rallies on the numbers

The market reacted quickly. Zydus Wellness shares **rose as much as 9.01%** to an intraday high of ₹526.85 on Tuesday as the figures came through.

The stock moved as the company came **back into the black** after a loss-making quarter a year ago. The company did not give a breakdown of the growth in its first set of figures.

The results came on a heavy earnings day on Dalal Street, with Bajaj Finserv, State Bank of India and Lupin among the large companies reporting September quarter numbers.
