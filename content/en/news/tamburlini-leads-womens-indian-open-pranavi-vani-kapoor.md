Chiara Tamburlini tightened her grip on the Hero Women's Indian Open on Friday, adding a one-under 71 to her opening 67 to reach six under par at the halfway stage at DLF Golf and Country Club in Gurugram.

The Swiss golfer, who led by a shot after the first day, was two clear of Argentina's Magdalena Simmermacher, who was level par for her round with six holes to play. A group at three under included Sara Kouskova of the Czech Republic, Norway's Dorthea Forbrigd and India's Pranavi Urs.

Pranavi, the Asian Games gold medallist, was one under for the day with three holes left. She had battled illness on Thursday to open with a 70. "The [ailment] is not good for this golf course considering how challenging it is," she said after that round.

Vani Kapoor was the day's big Indian mover, shooting a three-under 69 to climb to two under, tied seventh. Tvesa Malik was level par for the tournament after a 73.

It was a tougher day for others. Avani Prashanth, who had matched Pranavi's 70 on Thursday and said her "approach game was the best it has ever been", fell back with a 76 to two over. Defending champion Shannon Tan of Singapore, one shot off the lead overnight, was four over for her round with three holes to play and back at level par.

Tamburlini had put her first-round lead down to the putter. "The putter was definitely what made it possible to go low," she said on Thursday, describing her approach as "simple golf".

The projected cut was eight over. Diksha Dagar, at 13 over after rounds of 77 and 80, was set to miss the weekend. Play continues on Saturday.
