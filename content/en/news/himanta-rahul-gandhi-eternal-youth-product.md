Assam Chief Minister Himanta Biswa Sarma on Friday mocked Congress leader Rahul Gandhi as "an eternal youth product", a day after Prime Minister Narendra Modi accused the Congress of repeatedly trying to launch a "failed product".

Speaking to reporters in Guwahati, Sarma said Gandhi "never grows old and never becomes mature".

"I agree with the Prime Minister that the Congress party is attempting to relaunch the same product every year," he said.

Modi had made his remark at the NDTV World Summit on Thursday, in a speech that took aim at the opposition party. Sarma, a former Congress leader who joined the BJP in 2015, has been one of the sharpest critics of the Gandhi family in the ruling party.

He also poked fun at the Congress's efforts to present Gandhi as a leader of young Indians. If the Congress could count a man in his late fifties as Gen Z, Sarma joked, then he should qualify as well, since he is 57. He added his own charge that Gandhi tends to go abroad after political setbacks.

No response from the Congress had been reported by Friday night.

The remarks are the latest round in a long-running war of words between Sarma and Gandhi. Earlier this month Sarma was among the BJP leaders who seized on a video of the Congress leader, and the two have clashed repeatedly since Sarma left the Congress.
