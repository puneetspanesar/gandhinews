Haryana has raised the **dearness allowance to 63%** for its employees and pensioners, Chief Minister Nayab Singh Saini announced in Chandigarh on Friday, as he released ₹4,470.77 crore to 52,21,083 beneficiaries of state schemes.

The 3 percentage point rise, from 60%, takes effect from **July 1, 2026**. Saini said it covers about 5.38 lakh people: 2.78 lakh government employees and 2.60 lakh pensioners. Dearness relief for pensioners goes up by the same amount.

## Lado Lakshmi and pensions

The money included the **13th instalment of the Deendayal Lado Lakshmi Yojana**: ₹222.52 crore credited to 10,59,000 women. Saini said ₹2,689.15 crore has now been paid out under the scheme across 13 instalments. Its second phase, which began on September 17, raised the annual income limit to ₹1.80 lakh.

Social security pensions under 15 state schemes accounted for **₹1,135.21 crore**, reaching 35,26,358 people. Under the Har Ghar-Har Grihini Yojana, ₹24.25 crore in September's subsidy went to 6,05,000 women who had refilled their gas cylinders, taking the total so far to ₹404.46 crore.

The Deen Dayal Upadhyay Antyodaya Parivar Suraksha Yojana (Dayalu) is paying ₹202.99 crore to 5,429 families, which brings its total to ₹3,682.83 crore.

## Funds for villages and towns

Saini said **₹1,158 crore** of the ₹2,316 crore earmarked by the State Finance Commission for rural bodies has been released in two instalments to 22 zila parishads, 143 panchayat samitis and 6,230 gram panchayats. Of a ₹2,754 crore budget for urban bodies, ₹1,727.80 crore has gone to municipalities.

At the same briefing, the Chief Minister said the state had paid ₹9,880 crore to paddy farmers this season and brought beekeepers under its horticulture insurance scheme.
