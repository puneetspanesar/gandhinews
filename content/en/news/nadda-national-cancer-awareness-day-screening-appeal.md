Union Health Minister J P Nadda marked National Cancer Awareness Day on Saturday with an appeal to **get screened early**. In a social media post he said awareness and access to screening remain vital in the fight against cancer.

Nadda asked people to spread awareness of prevention, learn the early warning signs, and support timely screening and treatment. He urged **screening for the common cancers, including oral, breast and cervical cancer**, as recommended.

## Habits and symptoms

The minister's advice on daily habits was plain: **avoid tobacco and alcohol**, exercise regularly and eat a healthy diet. Anyone with unusual or persistent symptoms, he said, should consult a doctor.

For information on screening, he asked people to visit their nearest **Ayushman Arogya Mandir** or other health facility. The post gave no new figures or scheme announcements. The appeal is aimed at catching the three cancers named early, when treatment is most likely to work, and at getting people to a health facility before symptoms become serious.

## Cost of treatment in the news

The day comes weeks after the Centre told the Supreme Court it will cap trade margins on cancer medicines at 30% of the maximum retail price, a step the government expects to cut prices of these drugs by 20% to 70%. The court has asked why the cap is limited to cancer drugs.
