P V Sindhu is into the semi-finals of the French Open after a performance of real authority in Paris on Friday. The Indian beat Chiu Pin-chian of Chinese Taipei **21-13, 21-14 in 39 minutes** at Arena Grand Paris, and will play Japan's Hina Akechi for a place in the final.

India's other quarter-finalist could not follow her. Ashmita Chaliha, a day after the biggest win of her career, went down in three games to Japan's Nozomi Okuhara, **10-21, 21-17, 16-21**.

That leaves Sindhu as India's last player standing at the Super 750 event.

## Never in danger

Sindhu, ranked 11th in the world, controlled the match from the start. She led 11-5 at the first interval and pulled away to take the opening game, then went to the break in the second leading 11-2. Chiu found a few points late on, but there was no way back for her.

It was Sindhu's second win over Chiu, after their round-of-32 meeting at the Asian Games last month, and it took her to her fourth semi-final on the BWF World Tour this year. In Paris she has now dropped no games since the first round: on Thursday she beat Japan's Riko Gunji 21-12, 21-16.

## Akechi waits

Her opponent on Saturday, Akechi, earned her place by beating Indonesia's Putri Kusuma Wardani 21-17, 21-18. Putri said afterwards that the Japanese player had been very tidy, safe and accurate, and admitted she had been run all over the court in the second game.

## Ashmita's fightback falls short

Ashmita had come into Friday on a high after knocking out Japan's Akane Yamaguchi 21-19, 21-18 on Thursday. Against Okuhara she was blown away in the first game, trailing 11-6 at the interval and losing it 10-21.

She hit back in the second, leading 11-5 at the break and holding on to level the match at 21-17. The decider was tight to the interval, with Okuhara ahead 11-9, but the Japanese player pulled clear to close it out 21-16 after 59 minutes.

It was Okuhara's first win over Ashmita in international badminton. Their only previous meeting, at the 2024 Badminton Asia Team Championships, went to the Indian 21-17, 21-14.

Even so, Ashmita leaves Paris with plenty to build on. The Yamaguchi win was the best of her career.

## What comes next

Sindhu and Akechi meet in Saturday's semi-finals in Paris, with the winner going through to the final.
