Prime Minister Narendra Modi is in Karnataka on Friday, November 13, for a day that takes him through **Bengaluru and Mysuru in five stops**: a yoga university, a new metro line, a public meeting where projects worth over ₹15,600 crore will be launched, a tree plantation and a planetarium. The timings below are the approximate times in the Prime Minister's Office release of November 11, and Modi leaves for Gujarat on Saturday.

## The day, hour by hour

At about **10:45 am** Modi attends the Golden Jubilee celebrations of Prashanti Kutiram, the residential headquarters of the S-VYASA yoga university in Bengaluru, and addresses the gathering. He will lay the foundation stone of its Golden Jubilee Administrative Block and launch the S-VYASA off-campus at Thiruvananthapuram. The celebrations, themed "Towards Viksit Bharat 2047", run from November 13 to 23 and also mark the birth centenary of H V Sheshadri, the RSS general secretary from 1987 to 2000.

At about **12:45 pm** he flags off the Pink Line and rides from Tavarekere to IIM Bangalore station. At about **1:15 pm** he addresses a public gathering where the ₹15,600 crore of projects will be inaugurated, dedicated to the nation or have their foundation stones laid.

In Mysuru, at about 3:30 pm, he plants trees at Bharat Vann under the Ek Ped Maa Ke Naam campaign, an urban afforestation project over 17.63 acres that aims at more than 2 lakh native trees. At about **4 pm** he inaugurates the COSMOS-Mysuru Planetarium, described as India's first LED dome planetarium, built by the Indian Institute of Astrophysics on the University of Mysore campus. Our [earlier report](/news/cosmos-mysuru-planetarium-led-dome-modi-inaugurates-november-13-preview) has its specifications: a 15-metre tilted dome, about 6,150 panels and shows in Kannada, English and Hindi.

## The Pink Line

The first section of the Pink Line runs **7.5 km, elevated, from Kalena Agrahara to Tavarekere**, according to published reports. The stations named are Kalena Agrahara, Hulimavu, IIM Bangalore, JP Nagar 4th Phase, Jayadeva Hospital and Tavarekere. The release puts its cost at about ₹3,470 crore.

The Commissioner of Metro Railway Safety cleared the stretch on October 28, reports say. Opening it takes **Namma Metro from 96.1 km to 103.6 km**, which reports describe as the second-largest metro network in India after Delhi.

The full line is 21.3 km from Kalena Agrahara to Nagawara, with 18 stations. The remaining 13.8 km is underground, from Dairy Circle to Nagawara via MG Road and Shivajinagar, and is targeted for 2027 according to one report.

## The ₹15,600 crore list

**Power.** Two transmission schemes are to be inaugurated: the Koppal-II and Gadag-II scheme (about ₹4,450 crore, about 2 GW) and the Bidar solar zone scheme (about ₹1,370 crore, 2.5 GW). Foundation stones go down for two more, in the Davanagere, Chitradurga and Ballari area (over ₹3,450 crore) and a Davanagere and Ballari strengthening project (over ₹1,100 crore).

**Gas and fuel.** Modi dedicates India's **largest underground LPG storage cavern**, at Mangaluru: over ₹850 crore, 80,000 metric tonnes, with a 9.4 km pipeline from New Mangalore Port. He also dedicates the ₹330 crore MRPL Devangonthi marketing terminal in Bengaluru and lays the foundation of a GAIL compressed biogas plant at Haralakunte, to process about 300 tonnes of municipal waste a day.

**Railways.** He flags off the **Bengaluru-Mangaluru-Madgaon Vande Bharat Express**, which serves Hassan, Sakleshpur, Mangaluru, Udupi, Murdeshwar, Gokarna, Karwar and Madgaon. Four redeveloped Amrit Bharat stations open (Chamarajanagar, Chikkamagaluru, Sakleshpur and Subrahmanya Road), along with the 45 km Bagalkot-Wandal doubling and the electrification of the 55 km Sakleshpur-Subrahmanya Road ghat section, which completes electrification of the Bengaluru-Mangaluru route.

## What the state government wants

On Thursday night Chief Minister D K Shivakumar said Karnataka will **welcome Modi respectfully** and raise the state's issues. Asked whether he would seek drought relief and funds, he answered "Definitely", but would not give details. He said he had asked for a private meeting and that Modi told him to come to Delhi. Details are in [our report](/news/shivakumar-modi-visit-bengaluru-welcome-drought-relief-private-meeting).

## What it means for commuters and the coast

For Bengaluru's south, the Pink Line adds a metro link from Kalena Agrahara through JP Nagar to Tavarekere. For the coast and ghat districts, the Vande Bharat and finished electrification change how the Bengaluru-Mangaluru route is run. The two inaugurated transmission schemes can together carry about 4.5 GW of renewable power out of Koppal, Gadag and Bidar.

## What happens next

The release gives no timings for Gujarat. On **Saturday, November 14**, Modi will perform darshan and pooja at the Ambaji Temple in Banaskantha district, one of the 51 Shakti Peethas. The underground section of the Pink Line is the next stage, targeted for 2027.
