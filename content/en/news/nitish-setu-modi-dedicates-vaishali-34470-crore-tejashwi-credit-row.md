Prime Minister Narendra Modi on Saturday dedicated the **Nitish Setu, a 9.7 km bridge over the Ganga** that links Kachhi Dargah on the edge of Patna with Bidupur in Vaishali district, and launched projects across Bihar that the government values at about ₹34,470 crore. The ceremony came a day after RJD leader Tejashwi Yadav said the bridge had been open to traffic for 17 months and the Centre had put no money into it.

It was Mr Modi's first visit to Bihar since May, when he attended the cabinet expansion of Chief Minister Samrat Choudhary, the first BJP leader to head a government in the state. The programme also fell in the first year of the present NDA government in Patna.

## What the Prime Minister's Office lists

According to the Prime Minister's Office, Mr Modi first visited the bridge at about 1:15 pm and then, at about 2 pm, inaugurated and dedicated projects, laid foundation stones and addressed a public meeting.

The Nitish Setu is an **extradosed cable-stayed bridge costing about ₹4,280 crore**. The 19.7 km project is a 9.7 km main bridge plus 10 km of approach roads, joining NH-22 in Patna district to NH-322 in Vaishali district. The office says it will relieve traffic on the Mahatma Gandhi Setu and the JP Setu, shorten journeys between north and south Bihar, and give people in Raghopur Diara easier access to Patna, hospitals and colleges. State officials expect it to speed up the movement of litchi, banana, maize and vegetables from north Bihar.

Projects inaugurated or dedicated are worth **over ₹15,600 crore**:

- Six-laning of Aurangabad to Chordaha on NH-19, Chorma to Bairgania on NH-227F and Umagaon to Saharsa on NH-327.
- Unit 2 (660 MW) of the 1,320 MW Buxar Thermal Power Project, and transmission and distribution works, among them loss-reduction works under the RDSS worth over ₹1,130 crore.
- Canal and embankment restoration, a floating solar project at Phulwaria Dam in Nawada and a sewerage system at Kahalgaon.
- Buildings for schools, health services, agriculture, sports and administration.

Foundation stones were laid for works of **around ₹14,450 crore**. They include four-laning of Sahebganj to Areraj to Bettiah on NH-139W (over ₹3,800 crore), power evacuation for the 3 x 800 MW Pirpainti Super Thermal Power Project (about ₹3,350 crore), sewerage and water supply in towns from Begusarai and Hajipur to Bodh Gaya and Sitamarhi, an IIT Patna research park and hostel, Dr Bhimrao Ambedkar residential schools, and police data centre infrastructure.

He also flagged off 100 PM-eBuses and took part in the housing handover for more than 26,900 beneficiaries under the urban Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana. Vehicle movement on the highways concerned was to be suspended for a set period in Vaishali and Patna during the visit.

## The row over who built it

The bridge is named after former Chief Minister Nitish Kumar, under whose watch it was conceived and brought close to completion.

On Friday evening Mr Yadav, the Leader of the Opposition in the Bihar Assembly, released a video questioning why a second inauguration was needed. He said **Mr Kumar had already opened the bridge on June 23, 2025**, that "lakhs of vehicles" had used it since, and that the department had issued a formal advertisement for that opening. "There is no financial contribution by the Centre in the construction of this bridge," he said, adding that neither the decision to build it nor its execution involved the Prime Minister.

He also said the state had proposed naming it after the freedom fighter Akshayvat Rai or after Guru Gobind Singh, as it lies near the Sikh Guru's birthplace, and that naming a structure after a living leader suggested the BJP regards the JD(U) leader as "politically finished". If the BJP wished to honour Mr Kumar, he said, it should name a central scheme after him. Mr Yadav put the cost at more than ₹5,500 crore, against ₹4,280 crore in the Prime Minister's Office release.

The BJP rejected this. Its spokesperson Kuntal Krishna called the remarks irresponsible, according to reports.

## Voices from the NDA

Mr Choudhary said in a video message that the Prime Minister would "dedicate the Nitish Setu to the state" and that the projects would add momentum to Bihar's development. Union Minister Chirag Paswan, who called the visit to his Lok Sabha constituency a "momentous occasion", said his late father Ram Vilas Paswan "would be overjoyed to see this long-held aspiration of the people being fulfilled".

Nishant Kumar, a minister and the son of Nitish Kumar, thanked the Prime Minister and the Chief Minister for naming the bridge after his father. JD(U) working president Sanjay Jha said it would "become a permanent landmark". Bihar BJP president Sanjay Saraogi said a 1,400-acre industrial corridor was planned and that agreements worth nearly ₹2 lakh crore had been signed a day earlier.

## Why it matters

North Bihar reaches Patna over a limited number of Ganga crossings, among them the Mahatma Gandhi Setu and the JP Setu, and the Prime Minister's Office says the new bridge is meant to ease traffic on both. For Raghopur Diara, a river island area of Vaishali, the office points to better access to Patna's hospitals and colleges. Raghopur was where 13 children drowned on October 13 when a boat capsized in the Ganga, as we reported.

For farmers and traders in north Bihar, officials expect a shorter and cheaper run to the capital's markets. Power consumers are affected by the Buxar unit and the loss-reduction works, which add generation and aim to cut losses in distribution.
