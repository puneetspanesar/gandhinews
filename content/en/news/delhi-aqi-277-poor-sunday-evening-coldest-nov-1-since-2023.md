Delhi's air **slid further into the 'poor' category** through Sunday, with the city's air quality index reaching 277 according to the Sameer app, up from 214 at 7 am. It was a chilly day too, with the capital recording its **coldest 1 November since 2023**.

The spread across the city was worse than the headline number suggests. Of 54 monitoring stations, **25 were in the 'very poor' range** and 18 in 'poor'. On the Central Pollution Control Board scale, a reading from 201 to 300 is 'poor' and 301 to 400 'very poor'.

## A cooler day

The India Meteorological Department said the minimum temperature was **18 degrees Celsius**, 1.9 degrees below normal for the season. That was the lowest for the date since 2023, when the city recorded 16.4 degrees. The maximum of 29 degrees was the lowest on 1 November since 2021, when it was 27.2 degrees.

Relative humidity stood at **75 per cent at 5:30 pm**.

The worsening came on the first day of the capital's **new winter pollution curbs**, which took effect on Sunday morning.

## What next

The weather office has forecast **mainly clear skies** for Monday, with a maximum of about 29 degrees and a minimum of 17 degrees.
