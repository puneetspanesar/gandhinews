**Rahul Gandhi** told the closing session of the Bharat Summit in Hyderabad on Saturday that the Congress and its allies "are going to defeat PM Modi and we are going to get rid of PM Modi", calling the outcome "already a done deal". The Leader of the Opposition in the Lok Sabha spoke at the Hyderabad International Convention Centre on the concluding day of the two-day gathering hosted by the Telangana government.

He offered no documents for his claims about **Gautam Adani and the electoral rolls**, and no response from the BJP or the Election Commission to this particular speech was available when we wrote this report.

## What he said

Gandhi said the opposition's confidence could be seen "on the streets" and in what he described as the fear of Modi and Home Minister Amit Shah. The usual instruments of opposition politics, he said, had stopped working: the media, press conferences and public meetings no longer reached people because **institutions had been taken away** from the opposition. What remained, he argued, was a direct connection with the people, which he called stronger than control of institutions.

He said democracy was destroyed in India in 2014, when large amounts of money entered the political system, and accused those in power of authoritarian tendencies.

## The Adani charge

Gandhi pointed to Adani's rise in global wealth rankings and said **the line between business and politics** "between Adani and PM Modi, has evaporated". He also claimed the BJP's assets had grown elevenfold over a decade. These are his allegations; the government and the BJP have not responded to them in the reports available. Adani has not commented on this speech.

## Voter numbers

On elections, Gandhi questioned the legitimacy of the 2024 results. He said **about 70 lakh new voters** were added in Maharashtra in the three months between the Lok Sabha and Assembly elections, and that "every single one of those 7 million voters votes for the BJP". He added that about 14 crore people who voted in the 2024 Lok Sabha election were later declared non-existent or non-voters, and asked how that could be squared with the Election Commission's account of its own work.

Reports of the speech say the Election Commission and BJP leaders have rejected similar allegations from him before, and that BJP leaders have said he has not produced verifiable evidence. We have not seen a fresh statement from either after Saturday's remarks. The figures above are Gandhi's own and could not be checked against the rolls.

## How we got here

The speech closed a summit already caught up in a political row. On Friday, Telangana Chief Minister Revanth Reddy said the Centre had cancelled visas of invited delegates at the last minute; the Ministry of External Affairs replied that visa decisions are a sovereign function. Our [report](/en/news/revanth-bharat-summit-visas-modi-hyderabad) has both sides.

Earlier on Saturday, Gandhi joined Revanth Reddy to launch Telangana's gig workers law, riding pillion on a delivery worker's scooter, and attacked the BJP and RSS for backing big industrialists. That [report](/en/news/rahul-gandhi-hyderabad-gig-workers-act-insurance) has the insurance and platform-fee details.

On Friday he had alleged that the Prime Minister has his phone tapped and repeated his call for an open debate with Modi; BJP leaders dismissed it. Shah had answered on Thursday that the place for such a debate is Parliament and that a special session could be called if the Congress asked formally. See our [earlier report](/en/news/rahul-gandhi-phone-tapped-modi-debate-challenge-foreign-powers).

## Why it matters

The voter-roll argument is the live dispute of this season. The INDIA bloc has campaigned against the Special Intensive Revision of the rolls and against Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar, while the government says infiltrators must be removed from the lists. A claim that millions of voters were added or struck off is a claim about who gets to vote, and it will stay an allegation until a court, the Commission or an independent audit examines it. Gandhi gave no such material at the summit.

## What happens next

Parliament is expected to be recalled within days for a special sitting on the Delimitation Bill and the women's quota, as officials have indicated; our [report](/en/news/special-session-delimitation-bill-shah-361-votes-jairam-bluster) has the numbers. That session would be the first chance for the Leader of the Opposition to put these charges to the government in the House, as Shah has invited him to do. No date has been announced. Watch for a BJP or Election Commission reply to the 70 lakh and 14 crore figures, and for any formal Congress request to the Chair.
