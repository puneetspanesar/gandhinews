A fire broke out at a **Hindustan Petroleum facility in Visakhapatnam** on Monday night, sending a tall column of flames and thick black smoke into the sky over the port city.

Visakhapatnam Police Commissioner Shankhabrata Bagchi said nobody had been hurt. "A fire broke out in Vizag HPCL facility and there were no casualties," he said.

Officials said the **cause had not yet been confirmed** and would have to be established by Hindustan Petroleum Corporation Limited (HPCL), the state-run oil company that runs the facility. They did not say which part of the site caught fire.

Videos of the blaze, showing **a large column of fire and smoke** over the city, spread quickly on social media late on Monday.

## What comes next

By midnight there was **no word from HPCL** on the extent of the damage, how long the fire burned or whether work at the site was affected. The police have not said whether nearby areas were evacuated.

Visakhapatnam is home to HPCL's Visakh refinery, one of the company's two big refineries, and to a cluster of oil and petrochemical installations along the coast, which is why any fire in the area draws quick attention from the city and the state.
