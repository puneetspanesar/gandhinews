The Sensex rose for a **fifth straight session** on Wednesday, adding 383.55 points, or 0.5%, to close at 77,301.4, as buying in realty and defence shares, softer crude oil and steady foreign inflows kept the rally going.

The Nifty 50 gained 149.7 points, or 0.63%, to **finish at 23,960.6**, its highest close of the run. Breadth on the index was firm, with 36 of its 50 shares ending higher and 14 lower.


## Defence and smaller shares lead

Bharat Electronics was the **best performer on the Nifty, up 4.71%**, as defence stocks drew buyers. Adani Enterprises climbed 4.47%, Titan Company 4.22%, Jio Financial Services 3.73% and Max Healthcare Institute 3.53%.

The broader market did better than the benchmarks. The Nifty Midcap 100 rose 1.55% to 59,505.5, and the **Nifty Smallcap 100 jumped 2%** to 19,026.4.

## Oil, foreign money and Washington

Crude oil eased to **about $102 a barrel** after touching $104 during the day, a relief for India, which buys most of its oil abroad. Foreign investors were net buyers of ₹6,836.81 crore of shares on Tuesday, while domestic institutions sold ₹3,678.85 crore.

Traders also kept an eye on the US midterm count, which has handed the Democrats control of both the Senate and the House, and on a pullback in US Treasury yields. Most Asian markets ended higher, with Shanghai up 2.12% and Hong Kong's Hang Seng up 1.4%, though Tokyo's Nikkei fell 1.9%.
