A former Tech Mahindra executive, **Paramjit Dhankhar, 43**, was shot dead in the parking area of his building in Sector 21C, Faridabad, on Friday night after a long-running quarrel with a neighbour over a dog and shared space. Police arrested the neighbour, **Mohan Sharma, 42**, on Saturday.

Dhankhar, who later worked for FPT Software's Singapore office, had flown home on November 5 for a family wedding and to spend Diwali with his relatives. At about **8:30 pm on Friday** Sharma, who lives on the second floor, allegedly asked him to come down and move his car. An argument followed in the stilt parking and Sharma opened fire. Police say he fired at close range and that **at least nine bullets hit Dhankhar**. He was taken to a private hospital, where doctors declared him dead on arrival.

Pinta Ram, who lives on the first floor, said he heard several loud bangs in quick succession and that neighbours rushed down after hearing screams.

## A dispute that had gone to the police

The two families had reportedly been at odds for about three months, over Sharma's German Shepherd puppy fouling the parking area. A week before the shooting Sharma allegedly broke one of three entrance gates Dhankhar had put up at the stilt, and Dhankhar's family complained to the police. On Friday morning Sharma was called to the Ankhir police post over that complaint. A relative says he threatened Dhankhar after the argument there.

Police and the family suspect the killing was planned, because Sharma allegedly went downstairs with his handgun and two loaded magazines.

## Arrest and charges

An FIR was registered at Surajkund police station under Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita sections for **murder, attempt to murder, criminal intimidation and criminal conspiracy**. The complaint was made by Dhankhar's son. Sharma's wife and two children are named as accused for conspiracy but had not been arrested at the last report.

Crime Branch teams traced Sharma, and he was picked up from Dealer Chowk in Ballabgarh on Saturday. Police recovered his **licensed pistol, 59 live cartridges and two magazines**. "The accused was traced after teams of the Crime Branch were asked to look for him given the seriousness of the case," police spokesperson Yash Pal said. Varun Kumar Dahiya, Assistant Commissioner of Police (Crime), said Sharma would be taken on police remand.

Dhankhar leaves behind his parents, his wife and two children, an 18-year-old son and a 14-year-old daughter. He had planned to attend his nephew's wedding in Faridabad on November 24.
