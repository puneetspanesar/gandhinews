Andhra Pradesh Chief Minister N Chandrababu Naidu announced on Thursday that **303 new MoUs worth ₹9.89 lakh crore** will be signed at the 31st CII Partnership Summit in Visakhapatnam. He made the announcement at the inaugural session of the two-day summit, which is hosted by the state with the Confederation of Indian Industry.

With the new agreements, the state's investment commitments since June 2024 reach **₹32.49 lakh crore**, Naidu said, calling it the biggest such figure in the country. He asked every entrepreneur in the hall to sign "at least one MoU before leaving the summit".

## What was announced

Before the summit, the state counted 714 proposals worth ₹22.6 lakh crore since June 2024. Of these, **526 projects worth ₹17.10 lakh crore** have received land and incentive approvals, and 303 projects worth ₹7.30 lakh crore are ready for construction. The ₹9.89 lakh crore announced on Thursday comes on top of that ₹22.6 lakh crore.

The figure is well above the state's own pre-summit pipeline. On the morning of the summit, the state had lined up 141 MoUs and 30 government orders worth ₹12.14 lakh crore, as our [earlier report](/en/news/cii-partnership-summit-visakhapatnam-opens-141-mous-12-lakh-crore) set out. The two numbers count different things, and no sector-by-sector breakdown or list of companies for the 303 has been released. Job figures for them were not given either.

## Investor protection law

Naidu said the state will bring in an **Investors Protection Act** to give investors legal assurances. The Bill is to go to the State Assembly and then to the Centre for approval. IT Minister Nara Lokesh said the state is working on it and asked Union Commerce and Industry Minister Piyush Goyal to help with clearance at the national level.

The state already offers escrow accounts for disbursing industrial incentives, which Goyal said would raise investor confidence and could be an example for other states. Andhra Pradesh hopes to be the first state with a dedicated legal framework for investment protection.

## What the leaders said

Goyal praised the host. "He is a master in instilling confidence in those coming to invest," he said of Naidu, adding that Andhra Pradesh is "becoming a platform for many opportunities just as one lamp lights many more lamps". He credited the state with recovering from two recent cyclones while hosting the summit, and said Visakhapatnam was moving from a steel city to a silicon city.

Deputy Chief Minister Pawan Kalyan called the state a reliable investment destination, pointing to its 1,053 km coastline, and had set a goal of more than 150 agreements at the summit. That target has now been passed. Remarks by Union Home Minister Amit Shah, who was listed for the inaugural session, had not been reported at the time of writing.

## Projects named

Naidu listed what the state is pitching. **Kia will invest about $2 billion**, around ₹17,000 crore, in a new plant that is expected to create nearly 12,000 jobs and lift capacity from 4.3 lakh to 6.3 lakh vehicles a year. See our report on the carmaker's [record October sales](/en/news/kia-record-october-sales-33462-seltos).

Other announcements were a quantum computing manufacturing facility on 250 acres in Amaravati, to be inaugurated on February 3, 2027, India's first Hydrogen Valley, a space city in Tirupati, a drone city for civilian and defence drones, and drone air taxis. The state has set a target of 160 GW of renewable energy, toward India's 500 GW goal, and says approvals for 114 GW have been granted. Its six operational ports handled 209 million tonnes in 2025-26.

Naidu also proposed a Godavari-Cauvery water grid and said the Polavaram project is targeted for dedication by June 2027.

## Why it matters

An MoU is a statement of intent, not money spent. Naidu's own figures show the gap: of ₹22.6 lakh crore in pre-summit proposals, ₹17.10 lakh crore has cleared land and incentive approvals, a conversion rate the state puts at 74%, and ₹7.30 lakh crore is ready for construction. The investor protection law is aimed at that stretch between signing and building, where companies wait for land, power and incentive payments.

## What happens next

The summit continues on Friday, November 13, with sessions on space, AI, quantum technology, skills, tourism and energy, and a conversation with Naidu on the state's P4 anti-poverty programme. Vice-President C P Radhakrishnan attends the valedictory session at 4 pm. The final count of MoUs signed and their value is expected when the summit ends.
