ICICI Prudential Life Insurance reported a 25% rise in profit for the July-September quarter on Tuesday, helped by strong demand for protection and annuity policies, though its shares slipped on a day the wider market rallied.

Net profit for the quarter came in at ₹374.22 crore, up from ₹299.26 crore a year earlier. It was 3.1% lower than the ₹386.18 crore the company earned in April-June. Net premium income rose 16.6% to ₹13,803.90 crore.

For the first half of the financial year, profit rose 26.5% to ₹760 crore. The value of new business, a measure of the profitability of policies sold, grew 22.3% to ₹1,283 crore, with a margin of 26%.

"Retail protection Annual Premium Equivalent (APE) registered strong growth of 47.2 per cent YoY," the company said in a statement. Annuity APE, which tracks sales of pension-style products, grew 50% over the six months. Assets under management stood at ₹3.29 lakh crore at the end of September.

The stock did not share in the market's cheer. On the NSE it rose to ₹465.20 in the morning, then fell about 4% from that high to ₹446.30. In the afternoon it was trading 2.7% lower at ₹450, while the Nifty was up 1.5%.

The shares are close to their 52-week low of ₹441.80, touched on October 1, and well below the high of ₹706.80 they reached on January 13.
