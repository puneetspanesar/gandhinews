India's main jobs survey will try harder to find gig workers from next year. The National Statistics Office (NSO) said on Friday that it will change the sampling design of the Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS) from January 2027 and add questions on unpaid work, and invited comments on the plan within 15 days.

The survey has run since 2017 and was revamped in January 2025 to give monthly, quarterly and annual figures. Questions on digital platform workers, a subset of gig workers, have been asked on an experimental basis since January 2026. The office said that experience showed the current design was not picking up enough of them.

## A new layer for platform workers

From January, households will be sorted into a new second-stage stratum based on digital platform work, while the existing ones stay. Rural areas will have four such strata and urban areas five, with 14 households surveyed in each selected first-stage unit. Every household will be asked a yes-or-no question on whether any member has ever done digital platform work in the reference period.

Fresh samples for 2027 and 2028 will be drawn from an updated frame that reflects new urban areas and the creation, splitting or merger of districts. For the first three months, samples from the old and new frames will be pooled, and relative standard errors will not be published for those months.

## Unpaid work enters the count

New questions will cover making goods for one's own use, unpaid household and caregiving work, volunteering and unpaid trainee work. They will be asked of people aged 15 and above in both rural and urban areas, on the first visit only, so that the figures can be reported once a year.

The NSO said this would give a fuller picture of work beyond conventional jobs. Policymakers, researchers and the public can send views on the concept note, which is posted on the ministry's website.
