KRBL, the company behind the India Gate basmati rice brand, on Monday reported a **50% jump in September-quarter profit** as sales grew at a steady clip.

Consolidated net profit for the quarter ended 30 September came to ₹258.93 crore, against ₹172.11 crore a year earlier. Revenue from operations **rose 14.3% to ₹1,727.36 crore** from ₹1,511.08 crore. Profit before tax climbed to ₹349.04 crore from ₹232.52 crore, and earnings per share rose to ₹11.31 from ₹7.52.

## Flat on the previous quarter

Against the April-June quarter, the picture was steadier. Revenue rose from ₹1,495.86 crore, while profit **edged down slightly** from ₹260.74 crore.

For the first six months of the financial year, KRBL's profit reached **₹519.67 crore**, up from ₹322.69 crore in the same period last year. Half-year revenue was ₹3,223.22 crore, against ₹3,095.43 crore.

## Shares rose before the numbers

KRBL describes itself as an integrated rice company, with basmati processing, branding and exports, along with an energy business.

Its shares **ended about 5.2% higher at ₹363** on Monday. The results were filed with the stock exchange at about 4 pm, after trading had closed, so investors will react to the numbers on Tuesday. The stock is still down about 6% over the past year.
