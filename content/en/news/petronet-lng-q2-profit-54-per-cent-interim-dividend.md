Petronet LNG, which imports liquefied natural gas through terminals at Dahej and Kochi, on Monday reported a **54% jump in September-quarter profit**, and its board approved an interim dividend for shareholders.

Consolidated profit for the quarter ended 30 September came to ₹1,234.63 crore, against ₹801.88 crore a year earlier. On a standalone basis, profit was ₹1,235.40 crore, up from ₹805.75 crore.

The higher profit came on a smaller top line. Revenue **fell to ₹7,200.35 crore** from ₹11,009.13 crore in the same quarter last year.

## ₹7 a share for investors

The board declared an **interim dividend of ₹7 a share** on a face value of ₹10 for 2026-27. The company said the record date and the payment date will be announced later. It paid the same ₹7 interim dividend a year ago.

## Claims in the notes

The results carried an emphasis of matter from the auditors, V Sankar Aiyar & Co, on two items. Vessel owners have raised **arbitration claims of ₹625.42 crore** over suspended operations, which Petronet says are not tenable. The notes also list **"use or pay" dues** with a gross value of ₹408.23 crore.

Petronet runs India's largest LNG import and regasification terminal at Dahej. Its promoters are the state-run companies BPCL, Indian Oil, GAIL and ONGC. The results came after the stock market closed for the day.
