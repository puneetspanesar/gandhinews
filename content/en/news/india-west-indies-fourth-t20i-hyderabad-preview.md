India go to Hyderabad on Wednesday one win away from the series. A 14-run victory in Indore on Sunday put Shreyas Iyer's side 2-1 up against West Indies, and a win in the fourth T20I at the Rajiv Gandhi International Stadium would settle the five-match contest with a game to spare. The match starts at 7 pm IST.

It has been a series of big totals and quick swings. Iyer's unbeaten 102 set up an eight-wicket win in Lucknow. In Ranchi, Shai Hope's 102 and Sherfane Rutherford's 84 took West Indies to 252 for 4 in 18.3 overs, chasing down India's 250. Then, in Indore, India defended 223, with Abhishek Sharma making 69 off 30 balls and Arshdeep Singh taking 3 for 44.

## The squad India have

The squad has changed since the selectors named it on September 16. Naman Dhir replaced Washington Sundar for the T20Is, and Dhir has made his mark: two wickets in Indore, including Shimron Hetmyer and Kamil Pooran.

India's 15 are Iyer (captain), Tilak Varma (vice-captain), Abhishek, Vaibhav Sooryavanshi, Ishan Kishan, Sanju Samson, Dhir, Shivam Dube, Kuldeep Yadav, Axar Patel, Ravi Bishnoi, Nitish Kumar Reddy, Arshdeep, Prince Yadav and Mayank Yadav.

For Indore, India left out Kuldeep and Dube and brought in Prince and Nitish, trading a spinner for a seamer on a ground with short boundaries. Iyer said the plan was built around the death overs.

> Given the dimensions, we made those changes and we also needed someone who can hit the yorkers. It was wonderful to see the bowlers being confident.
>
> Source: Shreyas Iyer, India captain, October 11, 2026

## West Indies looking for an answer

West Indies have shown they can chase anything, but in Indore their bowlers conceded 223 and the batters fell short despite 43 from Hetmyer and 42 from Pooran. Hope, the captain, said the margins were small. "Probably with the ball, we could've executed a little bit better," he said after the match.

## New Zealand in view

These last two games are India's final T20Is before they fly to New Zealand. The selectors named a 16-man squad for that five-match series on October 6. Bhuvneshwar Kumar and Varun Chakaravarthy come in, while Bishnoi is not in it. The first match is in Christchurch on October 22.

The fifth and final T20I against West Indies is at the M. Chinnaswamy Stadium in Bengaluru on Saturday, October 17.
