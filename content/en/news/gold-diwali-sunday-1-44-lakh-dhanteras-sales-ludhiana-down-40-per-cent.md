Gold was around **₹1.44 lakh per 10 grams on Dhanteras**, about ₹51,000 below its peak of nearly ₹1.95 lakh in January, and jewellers say buyers are holding off on Diwali week because they expect the slide to continue.

In Ludhiana, jeweller Nilesh Garg said festive-season sales were **down by nearly 40%** on a year ago. Another trader, Anand Sikri, said the number of customers was far lower and that the price had slipped by about ₹1,000 in the 24 hours before Dhanteras. An investment consultant, Atul Mongia, said high prices and the expectation of a correction were keeping investors away, and that buying makes sense only once prices stabilise.

## What buyers saw on Saturday

City rates reported for Saturday, which reflect the previous day's close, were mixed. In **Bengaluru and Chennai a 10-gram lot of 24-carat gold cost ₹1,43,570** and 22-carat gold ₹1,31,600, both unchanged. Other cities such as Nagpur and Visakhapatnam saw small rises. The national average for 22-carat gold was ₹13,240 a gram, up ₹12. Silver was ₹2.05 lakh a kilogram in both southern cities.


## The wedding season question

The sources do not say where prices will settle or whether the fall will continue into the wedding season, which jewellers rely on. For now, sellers describe a market in which **customers wait for a lower price** and the festive buying that normally lifts Diwali week has been weaker than in 2025.

The stock exchanges are closed today for Diwali except for the Muhurat trading session from 2:20 pm to 3:30 pm.
