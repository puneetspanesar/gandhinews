An 18-year-old Class 12 student was found burnt to death on the premises of a school in Krishna Nagar in east Delhi on Saturday, police said.

The body was found behind the school's guard room. "Police received a PCR call about a fire at the school, following which personnel from Krishna Nagar police station rushed to the spot," police said.

Officers recovered some materials from the spot and from the student's school bag. A crime team was called in to inspect the scene.

Police have not named the student or the school, and have not said whether they suspect anyone else was involved. They said the circumstances of the death were being examined and the investigation was continuing.

No statement from the school had been reported by Saturday afternoon. It was also not known whether anyone else was on the premises when the fire was spotted, or who made the call to the police control room.
