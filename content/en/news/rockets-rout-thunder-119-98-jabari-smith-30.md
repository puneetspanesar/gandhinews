The Houston Rockets brushed aside a short-handed Oklahoma City Thunder on Saturday night, winning **119-98 at the Toyota Center** a day after their first defeat of the season.

**Jabari Smith Jr led the way with 30 points**, nine rebounds and five assists, making six of his 12 three-point attempts. Marcus Smart, starting in place of the rested Fred VanVleet, scored 24 and hit **seven of his 10 threes**, 12 of his points coming in a fast first quarter.

Houston were 35-26 up after one quarter, helped by 10 of 11 free throws, and led 62-51 at half-time. Alperen Sengun came close to a triple-double with 16 points, nine rebounds and nine assists, and Kevin Durant scored 14, 10 of them in the opening quarter. The Rockets **made 18 of 41 threes** and at one stage led by 27.

## A thin Thunder side

Oklahoma City were missing Jalen Williams, Chet Holmgren, Cason Wallace, Alex Caruso, Nikola Topic and Thomas Sorber, all injured. A 10-0 run in the second quarter kept them in touch for a while, and two straight threes from Shai Gilgeous-Alexander in the third cut a 21-point gap to 83-70.

That was as close as it got. Houston went into the final quarter 92-75 ahead, and Smith's pull-up jumper and another three made it 103-81 with 8:39 left. **Gilgeous-Alexander finished with 26**, Kenrich Williams added 11 points and seven rebounds, and Jared McCain and Isaiah Hartenstein had 10 each.

The defeat leaves the **Thunder at 4-2** for the season, while the Rockets bounced back from Friday's road loss to the Dallas Mavericks.
