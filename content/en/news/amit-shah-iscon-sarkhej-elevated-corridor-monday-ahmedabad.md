Union Home Minister Amit Shah will inaugurate the **₹464 crore elevated corridor on SG Highway** in Ahmedabad on Monday, November 9, 2026, the second day of the Diwali holidays, according to the schedule reported for his Gujarat visit.

The six-lane structure, built by the National Highways Authority of India, runs for about 4 km from the **Iskcon junction towards Sarkhej Chowkdi**. It has three ramps on each side to connect traffic at the junctions along the way. The idea is to lift through traffic over the signals on the ground-level road, cutting travel time and fuel use on one of western Ahmedabad's busiest stretches.

## Who it is meant to help

Officials expect relief for **about two lakh vehicles a day**, including traffic between Ahmedabad and Gandhinagar and vehicles coming from Saurashtra. The corridor will open to the public only after the inauguration.

## A five-day visit

Shah reached Ahmedabad late on Friday, November 7, and is in Gujarat until November 11. During the visit he is to inaugurate or lay foundation stones for **more than 30 projects worth over ₹1,500 crore** in Ahmedabad and Gandhinagar. They include a Knowledge Hub in Jodhpur built by the Ahmedabad Municipal Corporation, an urban health centre in Gota and key handovers to beneficiaries of the affordable housing scheme at Ramapir na Tekra.

He spends Diwali and Bestu Varsh, the Gujarati New Year, in Ahmedabad with family and BJP workers. On Friday he chaired a review of the state's security, narcotics and cybercrime with Chief Minister Bhupendra Patel and senior officials. On November 11 he will host a get-together at his residence for party workers and citizens before returning to Delhi.

Details of the other projects, and the exact timings on Monday, had not been published when this report was filed.
