The Ministry of Labour and Employment will hold a two-day state-level "Shram Samvad" in Lucknow from Monday, October 26, on the labour codes, labour welfare and social protection, the ministry said on Saturday.

The programme is being organised with the state government. The ministry did not immediately set out who would speak or which groups of workers and employers would take part.

Lucknow is one stop in a wider push. On September 28 Labour Minister Mansukh Mandaviya launched Shram Samvad as a mass awareness campaign on the labour codes, aimed at industrial clusters in 316 districts across all 36 states and Union Territories over 90 days. "Wherever there is Samvaad, there is Samadhan," Mandaviya said at the launch, stressing continuing dialogue among all stakeholders.

## Why the states matter

The Lucknow programme comes in the last week before a deadline. Many provisions of the codes depend on rules framed by each state. The Centre has asked every state to have its rules in place by October 31. Earlier this month the Labour Secretary said ten states had final rules and the rest had only drafts.

The release gives the programme three strands: the labour codes themselves, labour welfare, and social protection. The full agenda has not been published.

For Uttar Pradesh, the programme falls in the same week as the October 31 deadline. The ministry's announcement does not say where the state stands on its own rules.
