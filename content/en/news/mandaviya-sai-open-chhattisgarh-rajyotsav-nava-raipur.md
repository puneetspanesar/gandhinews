Chhattisgarh's 26th foundation day festival got under way on Sunday evening, with Union Minister Mansukh Mandaviya and Chief Minister Vishnu Deo Sai **formally opening Rajyotsav 2026** at the festival ground in Nava Raipur Atal Nagar.

The opening came at the end of a busy day for the state government. Mandaviya flew into Raipur in the late afternoon and was due at a hospital programme in the city before heading to the venue, where the Chief Minister was scheduled to arrive at 6 pm. Singer Palak Muchhal was to headline the first night.

The festival, built around the theme "Empowered Women, Youth Flight", runs until Tuesday at the Dr Shyama Prasad Mukherjee Industry and Trade Complex. Government departments have set up exhibitions on their schemes, and local folk troupes perform alongside national acts.

The organisers have planned for big crowds, with **parking for about 40,000 vehicles**, two entry gates, around 60 LED screens, a 20-bed hospital with an ICU and 25 ambulances on standby. The Indian Air Force's Sarang helicopter team and Suryakiran aerobatic team are flying displays on Sunday and Monday.

The closing ceremony on Tuesday will be attended by Union Home Minister Amit Shah, when **41 state honours will be presented**. Two awards are new this year, one named after the Pandavani singer Teejan Bai and one after the saint Ravidas. Singer Abhijeet Bhattacharya is due to perform that night.
