An Indian high school student, Amina Nasreen A.S., has won the **Global Youth Institute award** at the World Food Prize Foundation's annual gathering in Des Moines, Iowa, the agriculture ministry said on Friday.

She was one of **seven Indian high school students** chosen through a joint selection process run by the National Academy of Agricultural Sciences (NAAS) in New Delhi and the foundation. The students presented research papers on food security at the 2026 Norman E. Borlaug International Dialogue, held from October 20 to 22 under the theme "Before Hunger Wins". The award carries a cash prize of US$500.

## India's academy at the dialogue

NAAS ran an exhibition stall of its own and ICAR publications and held a roundtable on agricultural higher education. It also signed a memorandum of understanding with the Council for Agricultural Science and Technology (CAST) in the United States.

Dr M. L. Jat, the NAAS president, who is also Secretary of the Department of Agricultural Research and Education and Director General of ICAR, addressed the roundtable online. He called for agricultural higher education to be reshaped so graduates are ready for climate change, the degradation of land and water, the loss of biodiversity and rising demand for nutritious food.

He urged a move away from teaching built only around raising production, towards an approach that covers the whole agri-food system and its value chains, and he called for closer links between universities and industry and with the Indian diaspora.
