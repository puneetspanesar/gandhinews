India began their SAFF Championship preparations in Kolkata on Monday with **only 15 players at the first session**, three days before they open their campaign against Pakistan in Dhaka.

Head coach Naushad Moosa and his assistant Bibiano Fernandes put the group through its paces at the National Centre of Excellence. Three more players were expected to arrive on Monday night, which would give Moosa **18 players for the Pakistan match**.


The thin numbers are the cost of the calendar. The tournament starts before the FIFA window, so **players from clubs still busy in the ISL** will join only in Dhaka, after the league's fifth matchweek. Liston Colaco of Mohun Bagan Super Giant is due after his side's game against Odisha FC on November 5, and East Bengal's Sandip Mandi and PV Vishnu after the trip to Chennaiyin FC two days later.

Those in Kolkata included Mohun Bagan's Abhishek Suryavanshi, Priyansh Dubey and Suhail Bhat, FC Goa's Rony Wilson and Mohammedan SC's Adison Singh. Punjab FC's Nikhil Prabhu **completed the full session despite a limp** from Saturday's match against Mohun Bagan.

The team fly to Dhaka on Tuesday. India, the defending champions, play **Pakistan on November 5** and the Maldives on November 11 in their second and final group match.

Only a fortnight ago it was not clear whether India would send a side at all, because the Dhaka dates clashed with friendlies in New Zealand. Monday's session settled that question, even if Moosa will have to start the title defence without several of his first choices.
