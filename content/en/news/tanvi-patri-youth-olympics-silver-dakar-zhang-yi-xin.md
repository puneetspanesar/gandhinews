Fifteen-year-old Tanvi Patri won **silver in women's singles** at the Youth Olympic Games in Dakar on Thursday, November 5, 2026, becoming the first Indian woman to win a badminton medal at the Games. She lost a gripping final to China's Zhang Yi Xin, the reigning world junior champion, **21-15, 13-21, 24-26** in one hour and 22 minutes.

Tanvi started slowly but took the opening game in 25 minutes, turning a 3-5 deficit into a 10-5 lead with **a run of seven straight points**. She was 4-0 up early in the second game too, but a drift in the air began to upset her clears, and Zhang took charge after the interval to force a decider.

The third game looked over when Zhang led **19-10** with a stream of cross-court drops. Tanvi clawed back to 19-15, then an error on a cross-court smash handed Zhang five match points. The Indian **saved all five** and earned three chances of her own to win gold, but Zhang held her nerve to close it out at 26-24.

Only two Indians had won an individual badminton medal at the Youth Olympics before her, both men and both silver: **HS Prannoy in 2010 and Lakshya Sen in 2018**. Sen also took gold that year in a mixed team made up of players from eight countries.

The Badminton Association of India hailed the result. "Tanvi Patri has made the entire nation proud with a historic achievement at the Youth Olympic Games," its general secretary said. "Winning a silver medal at just 15 is an extraordinary accomplishment."

It was a final of fine margins. Tanvi led by a game against the world junior champion, saved five match points and **stood one point from gold three times** before the decider slipped away 26-24.
