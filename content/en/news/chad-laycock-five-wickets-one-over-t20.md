Chad Laycock has become the first South African to take five wickets in a single over in a T20 match, ripping through the Titans lower order for KZN Inland in the Pro20 Cup on Tuesday.

The Titans were cruising at 193 for 4 when Laycock came back for the 19th over. He removed Leus du Plooy, conceded two runs to Dayyaan Galiem, and then took four wickets in four balls: Galiem, Aphiwe Mnyanda, Janco Smit and Esosa Aihevba. The Titans were all out for 204.

Laycock had already dismissed Lhuan-dre Pretorius, who made 81 off 36 balls in an opening stand of 112 with Sinethemba Qeshile. He finished with a career-best 6 for 26 from his four overs.

Domestic Cricket South Africa called it "an extraordinary performance that will be remembered for a long time".

## Not enough for victory

The record did not bring a win. Chasing 205, KZN Inland reached 183 for 6, with Laycock himself unbeaten on 31 off 25 balls, and lost by **21 runs**. The Titans are now unbeaten in six matches in the competition and remain top of the table.

Laycock is only the **fourth bowler** to take five wickets in an over in men's T20 cricket, after Bangladesh's Al-Amin Hossain in 2013, India's Abhimanyu Mithun in 2019 and Indonesia's Gede Priandana in 2025. Priandana's feat remains the only one in a T20 international.

Laycock made his T20 debut in November 2024. He can bat too: his highest scores are an unbeaten 108 in first-class cricket and 99 not out in List A matches.
