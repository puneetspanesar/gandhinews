Bihar's Chief Minister, Samrat Choudhary, announced on Friday, November 13, that the six-lane Kachchi Dargah-Bidupur bridge over the Ganga will be named **Nitish Setu**, after Nitish Kumar, who held the chief minister's post before him. Prime Minister Narendra Modi is scheduled to inaugurate the bridge and dedicate it to the nation during his visit to the state on **November 21**.

The crossing links Kachchi Dargah on the Patna side with Bidupur in Vaishali district and is meant to give traffic between north and south Bihar a faster way across the river.

## What the Chief Minister said

Choudhary said the bridge would ease road travel for commuters in Patna, Vaishali and nearby areas, and that it would support **regional economic activity and employment**. He placed it among a run of projects in roads, bridges, power, education, health and industry that Bihar is building with central help, and credited Modi's guidance and the Centre's continued support. He repeated the state government's goal of a "Viksit Bihar", a developed Bihar, saying better connectivity would help reach it.

The remarks were reported in paraphrase, and no direct quotation from the Chief Minister or the Prime Minister was available at the time of writing.

## The bridge in numbers

According to project figures reported in July, the structure has a **9.76 km main bridge over the Ganga**, and the full corridor with approach roads is 22.76 km. The deck is 32 metres wide, stands on 67 piers and is designed for a speed of 100 km an hour. The cost was put at **₹4,988.4 crore**, of which about ₹3,000 crore is an Asian Development Bank loan and the rest comes from the state.

A later report on the November 21 programme describes the project as a 19 km link with a 9 km main bridge, so the exact length depends on where the approach roads are counted from.

The work was built by a joint venture of **Larsen & Toubro and South Korea's Daewoo Engineering & Construction**, and the Korean firm also prepared the design.

## How the bridge got here

Nitish Kumar laid the foundation stone on **January 31, 2016**. A first stretch of 4.57 km, connecting NH-31 to Raghopur, opened to traffic on July 23, 2024. The final segment of the main structure was placed on Pier No. 61 in April 2026, and by July the Road Construction Department put physical progress at 99.6%, with road markings and safety fittings still to finish. The bridge has not yet opened to general traffic.

Raghopur, at the Vaishali end, is the same riverside stretch where thirteen children died when a boat capsized in the Ganga in October. Our report on that accident is [here](/en/news/vaishali-boat-capsize-ganga-raghopur-children-killed).

## What happens on November 21

The Prime Minister's Bihar visit is an official government programme. Besides the bridge, he is due to attend an event at the **Jafarpatti Industrial Area in Rajapakar block**, Vaishali, which is meant to focus on industrial development, infrastructure and jobs. Bihar BJP president Sanjay Saraogi gave the date at a joint press conference in Patna on Monday, November 9.

The visit falls a day after the **first anniversary of the NDA government in Bihar** on November 20, as reported in the November 10 account of the programme.

## Why the name matters

The bridge was Kumar's own project from the foundation stone in 2016, so the name ties the opening to the leader who began it as well as to the Prime Minister who will open it. The announcement comes from Choudhary, whose government is led by the BJP.

No response from Kumar, from his party, the JD(U), or from the opposition was available in the reports found on Friday afternoon.

For daily commuters, the practical question is how much time the crossing saves between Patna and Vaishali and whether tolls will apply. Neither has been announced in the reports available so far.

## What happens next

The next marker is **Saturday, November 21**, when Modi is due to open the bridge and then go to Vaishali. Officials have yet to say when the bridge will open to regular traffic after the ceremony, or whether the name will appear on road signs and in official records through a formal notification.
