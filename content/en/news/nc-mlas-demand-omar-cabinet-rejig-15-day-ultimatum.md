A group of National Conference legislators has turned on its own leadership. About **17 NC MLAs and one Independent** met in Srinagar on Friday and passed a joint resolution demanding a fresh reshuffle of Chief Minister Omar Abdullah's cabinet, three days after he had just expanded it.

The legislators want every minister who has completed two years in office replaced. Their resolution targets "coterie culture" and "unilateral decisions", and gives party president Farooq Abdullah and Omar Abdullah, the party's vice-president, a **15-day ultimatum**. They were also reported to want two close aides of the Chief Minister, Nasir Aslam Wani and Shammi Oberoi, removed.

Nazir Gurezi will lead a **four-member delegation to Farooq Abdullah** with a memorandum. He described Friday's gathering as a "friendly lunch".


## An expansion that left seniors out

The unrest began on Tuesday, 27 October, when Lieutenant Governor Manoj Sinha swore in **four new ministers** at the Lok Bhavan Auditorium in Srinagar: Ali Mohammad Sagar, Mir Saifullah, Shameema Firdous and Altaf Kalo. Five sitting ministers had resigned before the reorganisation, which took the council of ministers to its maximum strength of nine.

Mubarak Gul, a **six-time MLA from Eidgah**, was left out. His supporters protested at the party headquarters at Nawa-i-Subh within hours of the ceremony, and Farooq Abdullah then made him the party's general secretary. Abdul Majeed Larmi, also passed over, was blunt. "This was not fair to me," he said. "I was dedicated, honest and loyal."

The NC leadership has not publicly responded to the resolution.
