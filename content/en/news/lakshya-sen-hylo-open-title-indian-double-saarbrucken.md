Lakshya Sen won the **Hylo Open men's singles title** in Saarbrücken on Sunday evening, beating Indonesia's Jonatan Christie 21-17, 21-10 in a final that turned into a procession in the second game.

The win completed **a memorable Indian double** at the Super 500 event in Germany. PV Sindhu had taken the women's title earlier in the day, so India left Saarbrücken with both singles crowns.

The result was all the sweeter given the history. Christie had **beaten Lakshya in three of their four previous meetings**, though the Indian had won the one that mattered most, at the Paris Olympics.

## How the final was won

Lakshya came out quickly and led 8-4. Christie fought back to 9-8, but a generous net cord put the Indian ahead again and he pulled away to **16-10 and then 18-12**. A service error from Christie gave Lakshya three game points, and the Indonesian netted to hand him the opener.

The second game was one-way traffic. Lakshya led 6-3 and was **four points clear at the interval**. Late on, a line call by Christie was overturned on video review, giving Lakshya 10 championship points at 20-10. He needed only the first, as Christie found the net again.


The title adds to a career that includes **Commonwealth Games gold in 2022** and an Australian Open win. Lakshya had finished runner-up at the Hong Kong Open in 2025 and at the All England Championships earlier this year. This time there was **no stumble at the final hurdle**.
